The genetics

Genetics enters Dune through the Bene Gesserit breeding programme, a long-term scheme of selective breeding in which the sisterhood arranges unions between noble bloodlines to produce a being with superhuman mental powers. Paul is the product of that programme, and he exists only because Jessica disobeyed the order to bear a daughter. The programme is a form of eugenics carried out over many generations by a secretive religious and political order, not by scientists, and it is presented as a tool of power. The film does not use the vocabulary of genes or DNA, and much of Paul's transformation comes from the spice and the Water of Life rather than from heredity alone. Genetics is therefore an important background element of the story rather than its main subject.

The story

In the year 10,191, the emperor Shaddam IV hands control of the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of the life-extending spice melange, to House Atreides, while secretly plotting with the rival House Harkonnen to destroy it. Lady Jessica, concubine of Duke Leto Atreides, belongs to the Bene Gesserit, a sisterhood with extraordinary powers that has spent centuries on a breeding programme to produce the Kwisatz Haderach, a male "superbeing". Ordered to bear a daughter, Jessica instead bore a son, Paul. After the Harkonnens attack and Leto dies, Paul and Jessica take refuge with the desert people, the Fremen, who see Paul as their prophesied messiah. Paul leads them in raids that halt spice production, drinks the poisonous Water of Life and gains psychic powers. He defeats the emperor's troops, kills the Harkonnen heir Feyd-Rautha in a duel and is declared the Kwisatz Haderach.
