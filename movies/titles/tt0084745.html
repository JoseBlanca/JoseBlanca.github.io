The genetics

Genetics is present as the scientific premise of a comic-book horror story. The Hollands' research aims to combine plant and animal traits in a hybrid organism able to withstand extreme conditions, and Alec becomes, accidentally, the subject of his own experiment, fused with the swamp's vegetation. The film's notion of the formula is vague: it regenerates tissue, makes plants grow explosively and, on people, "amplifies what is already there", turning the timid Bruno into a dwarf and the cruel Arcane into a monster. Alec and Linda are idealistic researchers working for the government, while the villain is a wealthy outsider who seeks the formula for power and immortality, a familiar contrast between well-meaning science and its misuse. There is no attempt at scientific realism: the transformations are instant and the mechanism is never explained. Real transgenic organisms combining genes from distant species exist, but they gain specific traits, not wholesale changes in body form.

The story

In the Louisiana swamps, government agent Alice Cable joins a secret bioengineering project led by Dr. Alec Holland and his sister Linda, who are developing a plant-animal hybrid able to survive in hostile environments. When Linda's formula proves to trigger explosive plant growth, the lab is raided by the paramilitary forces of Anton Arcane, who wants the formula for himself. Linda is shot, and Alec, doused in the chemicals and set on fire, plunges into the swamp. He re-emerges as the Swamp Thing, a powerful plant creature that protects Alice from Arcane's men. Arcane recovers Alec's notebooks, recreates the formula and tests it on his henchman Bruno, who shrinks into a small, misshapen man, because the formula only amplifies what a person already is. Arcane takes it himself, turns into a beast, and is killed by the Swamp Thing in the swamp.
