The genetics

The whole mystery is genetic. Professor Galen, an archaeologist, has been collecting DNA from life forms on many planets; Dr. Crusher combines the fragments by protein-link compatibility, Geordi recognises in them an algorithm rather than a natural sequence, and the program is completed with DNA from the races competing for it and from lichens on the last planet. The answer is a message written into the genomes of the galaxy by an ancient humanoid species that seeded worlds with its genetic material to steer evolution towards humanoids like itself. The episode thus uses directed panspermia as an in-universe explanation of why most Star Trek aliens look human, and treats the discovery of a common ancestry as a call for unity, which the Klingons and Cardassians reject. The ancient engineers are presented as benevolent creators, and the scientists who decipher their work as heroes. Hiding a readable message in genomes for billions of years of mutation and evolution is not plausible, and the episode was inspired by Carl Sagan's Contact.

The story

In this Star Trek: The Next Generation episode, Captain Picard's old archaeology mentor, Professor Richard Galen, asks him to join an expedition of enormous importance; Picard declines, and soon afterwards Galen is fatally wounded in an attack on his shuttle. In Galen's files the crew finds blocks of numbers that turn out to be DNA fragments from nineteen species on different worlds. Combined, they form an algorithm coded at the molecular level some four billion years ago, still incomplete. Following Galen's trail, the Enterprise competes with Cardassian and Klingon ships, which expect a weapon or a power source, and with a hidden Romulan vessel. On Vilmor II, Dr. Crusher completes the program with DNA from surviving lichens, and it projects a hologram: an ancient humanoid species, alone in the galaxy, seeded many planets with its genetic code so that humanoid life would arise. The Klingons and Cardassians are disgusted by their common ancestry, while the Romulan commander hints at a shared future.
