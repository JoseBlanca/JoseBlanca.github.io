The genetics

Genetics is mentioned rather than explored in this superhero comedy. Emily's parents were geneticists researching a way to give ordinary people powers to fight the Miscreants, whose own abilities come from cosmic rays; Emily completes their work as a serum and pills that give strength, invisibility and speed. The sources never say how the treatment works, and the science is a quick device to create the heroines. Emily is presented as an obsessive but cautious scientist, the German Wikipedia noting that critics saw her as a dull mad-scientist stereotype; her research is ultimately a force for good, used to defeat the villain.

The story

In 1983 cosmic rays give superpowers to sociopaths, who become supervillains called Miscreants; one of them kills the parents of Emily Stanton, both geneticists. Bullied at school, Emily is befriended by Lydia, who supports her dream of giving ordinary people powers to fight the Miscreants, but the two drift apart. In 2024 Emily runs a company continuing her parents' research. Visiting her, Lydia accidentally triggers a device that injects her with Emily's serum for super-strength, which requires a full course of treatments; Emily takes a pill version and becomes invisible. As the superhero team Thunder Force, they foil crimes, Lydia falls for a Miscreant called the Crab, and they refuse to work for mayoral candidate William Stevens, secretly the Miscreant King. When Stevens plans to bomb the new mayor's celebration, Emily's daughter Tracy injects herself with the serum to gain speed, and the trio defeats him; Lydia survives carrying the bomb into the river.
