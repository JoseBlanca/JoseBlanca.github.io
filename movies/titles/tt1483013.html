The genetics

Cloning is a plot twist rather than a subject of the film. The central revelation is that the protagonist is one of thousands of copies of the astronaut Jack Harper, produced and brainwashed by an alien intelligence to serve as its workforce and army against the surviving humans. The film uses the clones to ask whether identity and love survive copying and memory erasure: Jack and a second clone both regain fragments of the original Jack's memories and his attachment to his wife. There are no scientists or genetic research on screen; cloning is an alien technology that is never explained. As in many films, the clones are adult copies that retain the original's latent memories, which real cloning cannot do: a clone is a genetic copy that would grow from an embryo and share none of the original's experiences. Genetics plays only a minor role in the film.

The story

Sixty years after an alien invasion destroyed the Moon, Earth is said to be uninhabitable and humanity to have moved to Titan. The technician Jack and his partner Victoria maintain drones that guard machines converting seawater into energy for the Tet, a huge station in orbit, and their memories have been wiped for security. Jack dreams of an unknown woman. When a spacecraft crashes, he rescues one survivor, Julia, the woman of his dreams, who turns out to be his wife. Captured by the supposed alien "Scavengers", Jack learns from their leader Malcolm that they are surviving humans, and in the radiation zone he meets another Jack, number 52. The truth is that the Tet is an alien intelligence harvesting Earth, which defeated humanity using thousands of brainwashed clones of the astronaut Jack Harper and his colleague Victoria. Jack sacrifices himself to destroy the Tet, and later another clone of Jack finds Julia and their daughter.
