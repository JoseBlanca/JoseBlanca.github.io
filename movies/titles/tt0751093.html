The genetics

Human cloning is at the centre of this mythology episode. The victims are a group of genetically identical men, first explained as products of a Soviet genetics programme and then revealed to be alien clones living among humans, and the episode builds the series' mythology around them. No geneticist appears on screen: the clones are hunted, a laboratory linked to them is destroyed, and the governments and the bounty hunter are presented as hiding the evidence. The episode also uses forensic biology: Scully's autopsy finds that a victim's blood has coagulated abnormally. Real cloning can produce genetically identical animals but not adult copies with shared appearance and occupations, so the episode uses cloning as conspiracy-thriller material rather than as a realistic depiction.

The story

In the Beaufort Sea, the crew of a ship recovers a body from a crashed object: an alien bounty hunter. He soon begins killing identical-looking doctors who work at abortion clinics, stabbing them in the back of the neck and burning their clinics. Mulder and Scully trace further copies of the same man, but the bounty hunter keeps reaching them first, at one point disguised as an FBI agent. A man introducing himself as CIA official Ambrose Chapel tells them the doctors are clones from a Soviet genetics programme being eliminated by both governments; "Chapel" is in fact the shapeshifting bounty hunter. Scully discovers a laboratory and four more clones, who are killed after she puts them in custody. Meanwhile Mulder's father summons him home, where his sister Samantha, abducted as a child, has apparently returned and tells him that the clones and the bounty hunter are aliens. The story continues in "End Game".
