The genetics

The film is a story about a geneticist who uses cloning to escape himself. Each William creates a copy to take over a life he can no longer bear, and the film follows the consequences: the copies are confused about their identity, feel abandoned by their makers and, being clones of clones, inherit worsening genetic damage that will kill them. Next Corp is shown as a secretive company whose animal experiments are failing and whose security agents treat the theft of material as their main concern. The idea that cloning damages the copies reflects real findings of abnormalities and high losses in cloned animals, although the rapid decline shown here is dramatised. The adult clones who must be taught to walk and talk are also a fiction: real cloning starts from an embryo. In the final scene William Zero and Jules are shown with a cloned version of their dead son, which turns the film's warning into an ambiguous ending.

The story

After causing the accidental death of his son, the geneticist William Blakely wakes in a house cared for by a man who says he is his twin brother. William remembers nothing and must relearn the most basic things. The "brother" is in fact the William Blakely who works at the company Next Corp, where he has stolen material to create this new clone, William 2; meanwhile the company's cloned test animals are dying within weeks. William 2 discovers the truth and seeks out William's estranged wife, Jules. At Next Corp he learns that cloning a clone causes severe genetic problems, and realises from his symptoms that he is a second-generation clone. William 1 reveals that he too is a clone, made by the original William, William Zero, who abandoned his wife out of grief; feeling abandoned in turn, William 1 made his own replacement. William 2 saves Jules from William 1 and, knowing he will die, persuades William Zero to return to her.
