The genetics

Genetic enhancement is the background of the story. The Agents are designed people, engineered to be stronger, faster and more perceptive than normal humans and, in Katia's case, with heightened senses that she experiences as overwhelming anxiety until she learns to use them. The geneticist Dr. Litvenko is portrayed as a creator consumed by guilt, who abandoned his project and refuses, even under torture, to restart it for a corporation that wants an army of enhanced killers; he chooses death over handing them the technology. The film thus frames human enhancement as a weapons technology that its own inventor regrets, although the plot is mostly a series of action sequences and the science is never explained. Engineering complex traits such as speed, strength or perception in this way is fiction: no such enhancement of human beings exists.

The story

Agent 47 is one of the "Agents", genetically enhanced assassins created by the geneticist Dr. Piotr Litvenko, who abandoned the programme out of guilt and disappeared. Now working as a hitman for the International Contracts Agency, 47 is tracking Litvenko's daughter Katia, while the corporation Syndicate International wants to find Litvenko to restart the Agent programme. In Berlin, Katia, who suffers from anxiety and a superhuman awareness of her surroundings, is searching for an unknown man. A Syndicate operative, John Smith, offers her protection from 47, but 47 captures her and reveals that she, too, is an Agent, designed by her father to surpass him; her name, Katia van Dees, echoes "quatre-vingt-dix", French for 90. Together they fight the Syndicate and find Litvenko in Singapore. Litvenko refuses to revive the programme and kills himself and the Syndicate's director with a booby-trapped inhaler, and 47 and Katia face another Agent, 48.
