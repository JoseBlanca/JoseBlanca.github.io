Statistical Probabilities

The genetics

The episode explores the consequences of human genetic enhancement in the Star Trek universe, where the Federation bans genetic augmentation partly because of its fictional Eugenics Wars. Jack, Lauren, Patrick and Sarina were enhanced as children, and their brilliance comes with severe psychiatric and social difficulties, presented as unintended neurological side effects of the procedure. Their situation also shows the discrimination enhanced people face: they are kept in an institution and barred from careers where their talents could be used. Bashir, enhanced himself but able to pass as normal, serves as their bridge to society, and the episode questions whether superior intelligence makes people right; their confidence in their statistical predictions almost leads them to treason. No geneticist appears on screen. The episode raises recognisable questions about enhancement, disability and the limits of prediction.

The story

Dr. Julian Bashir, whose own genetic enhancement as a child has recently come to light, volunteers to help four genetically enhanced patients whose augmentation left them unable to function in society: the hyperactive, paranoid Jack, the hypersexual Lauren, the childlike Patrick and the silent Sarina. Bashir concludes that they are mostly bored, because the law bars them from any profession that would use their abilities. With Captain Sisko's permission, the group analyses peace talks with the Dominion and correctly deduces the Dominion's real aim. Given more intelligence, they build a statistical model predicting that the Federation will lose the war with hundreds of billions of casualties, and recommend surrender. When Starfleet refuses, they decide to pass Starfleet's plans to the Dominion. Bashir objects, is tied up, and is freed by Sarina, which lets him stop the others. He realises that one person's unpredictable choice has proved their model wrong.
