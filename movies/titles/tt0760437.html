The genetics

Genetics provides the series' central gadget. The Omnitrix contains the DNA of many alien species, and by using it Ben temporarily rewrites his body into one of those species, an idea that presents DNA as a code that fully determines an organism's form and abilities and can be swapped at will. The device can also scan new species, as happens by accident with the alien Zs'Skayr, so DNA acts almost as a collectable resource. The clearest scientist figure is the villain Dr. Animo, a veterinary scientist whose career ended when he was discovered carrying out twisted genetic experiments on animals, and who keeps creating mutant creatures to defeat Ben and rule the world. Kevin 11's transformation into an irreversible hybrid of Ben's aliens shows the risks of such power. The series is aimed at children and does not attempt realism: an adult body cannot be transformed by changing its DNA, since DNA directs development and cell function rather than reshaping an existing organism instantly.

The story

Ten-year-old Ben Tennyson is spending his summer holidays travelling across the United States in a motorhome with his cousin Gwen and their grandfather Max. On their first night camping, Ben finds an alien pod containing the Omnitrix, a prototype device shaped like a watch that attaches itself to his wrist and lets him transform into a range of alien creatures, each with its own powers. As he learns to use it responsibly, the trio face a series of extraterrestrial and supernatural enemies, above all the alien warlord Vilgax, who wants the Omnitrix for himself. Other recurring villains include Dr. Animo, a scientist who mutates animals, and Kevin 11, a delinquent who absorbs energy and eventually turns into a permanent hybrid of Ben's alien forms.
