The genetics

The series centres on illegal weapons research on human beings. Sara, designated HW-9, belongs to the HW series, women genetically engineered into living weapons, a successor to the BW series, whose glass-based weapons make their targets turn to glass and burst into flames. The English Wikipedia says the programme was meant for military use. Its head, Kito, is a cruel mad scientist obsessed with recovering his escaped subject, who uses unwilling people as test subjects, as when his new drug kills the journalist Sofia. Sara, by contrast, is a sympathetic victim who rejects her number and tries to build a life with the detectives who protect her. The series frames the genetic programme as exploitation of the marginal, set in a slum where society's 'failures' are dumped. The engineered traits, a high body temperature and electrical discharges triggered by men's touch, are fantasy; the sources say little about the science.

The story

In Rags Town, a slum on the edge of a prosperous near-future Asian city, former police detective Shuu runs the S&A Detective Agency with his younger brother Akira and two high-school apprentices, Manami and Ayaka. He takes in an amnesiac girl who turns out to be HW-9, an escapee from a secret programme that turns women into living weapons; she hates her designation and takes the name Sara. Her body runs at about 40 °C and releases powerful static electricity when a man touches her. The detectives protect her from a group of women hunting her for the programme's leader, the cruel scientist Kito, who is obsessed with recapturing her. Kito tests a new drug on Sofia, a journalist investigating the 'Glass Maiden' incidents, who turns to glass and dies in front of Shuu. The rogue weapon BW-Alpha kills Kito's bodyguard Kyrie, and in the end Sara stays in Rags Town, working at Porilyn's shop.
