The genetics

The episode revolves around a genetic weapon. The Harvesters are biomechanical 'gene disruptors' that both sides used in their war; Bashir neutralises them with a radiation frequency that destroys their genetic integrity, and O'Brien, infected by a single drop, rapidly loses his sight and ability to walk before being treated. The episode's moral twist is that destroying the weapons is not enough for the two governments: to make sure the Harvesters can never be rebuilt, they decide to kill everyone who knows how they work, their own scientists and the Federation officers included. Bashir is presented as a capable, heroic scientist using his knowledge to dismantle the weapons, while the knowledge itself becomes a death sentence. An early pitch, abandoned, had the weapon encoded in O'Brien's DNA.

The story

Chief O'Brien and Dr. Bashir help the T'Lani and the Kellerun, former enemies, destroy their stockpiles of Harvesters, deadly biogenic weapons from their long war; Bashir finds that a particular radiation frequency disrupts the weapons' genetic structure, and O'Brien erases all the technical data. As the last Harvesters are destroyed, Kellerun soldiers burst in and kill the scientists; in the struggle a drop of Harvester liquid falls on O'Brien's skin. O'Brien and Bashir beam down to the planet and hide while O'Brien, increasingly ill from the infection, guides Bashir through repairing an old transmitter. On Deep Space Nine, the two ambassadors tell Commander Sisko that both men died in an accident and provide a recording. Keiko notices that it shows O'Brien drinking coffee in the afternoon, which she is sure he never does, and Sisko and Dax find evidence they survived. The ambassadors reveal that they planned to kill everyone who knew how to make Harvesters, but Sisko rescues the pair.
