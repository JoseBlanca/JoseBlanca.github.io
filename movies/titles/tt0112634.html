The genetics

Genetics provides only the backstory. The dinosaurs come from a biotech company's experiments with fossilised DNA, events of the first Carnosaur film, and the sequel concerns the military's attempt to hide the results rather than the science itself. No geneticist appears on screen; the relevant figure is a Defense Department officer who conceals the danger from the team he leads, so the film's suspicion is directed at government secrecy around biotechnology. The rest is a monster-siege action film, often compared with Aliens, produced on a small budget by Roger Corman, who had launched the first film after learning that Jurassic Park was entering production. Recreating dinosaurs from fossil DNA is not possible: DNA breaks down over time, and the dinosaur DNA sequences reported in the 1990s turned out to be contamination.

The story

At the Yucca Mountain nuclear waste repository in the Nevada desert, unseen creatures kill the night staff, leaving only Jesse, a teenage hacker and the nephew of one of the workers. A team of civilian technicians, led by Major Tom McQuade of the Department of Defense, is sent to fix the site's power and communications. As they explore the lower, classified levels, they are picked off by Velociraptors. McQuade finally admits that a biotech firm had recreated dinosaurs from fossil DNA, and that dozens of eggs seized by the government were stored in the facility, where they hatched. Radiation is leaking from damaged containers holding nuclear warheads, and the survivors fight their way to the surface using dynamite. McQuade and an injured technician blow themselves up to kill the remaining raptors, and Jesse defeats a Tyrannosaurus rex before destroying the facility.
