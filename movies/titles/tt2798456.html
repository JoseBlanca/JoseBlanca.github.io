The genetics

Genetic engineering creates the film's monster. Dr. Irvine, lured by the Soviets with the promise of research free of any ethical or legal restraint, used DNA from a meteorite to create human-alien hybrids, then bred them and was prepared to sacrifice the female to obtain a purer specimen, a callousness that enrages even a hardened mercenary. Her former assistant, Dr. Healy, works for a corporation that wants to recover the hybrids as assets and is willing to kill her own team to do so. The film thus presents both the scientist and the company as ruthless, and the creation turns on its maker: the intelligent male hybrid kills Irvine and escapes into the world. A mercenary is also mutated by a syringe attack, though the source does not say what it contained. The science is pure genre premise.

The story

The mercenary Jim Powell is released from prison to lead a corporate mission: escort the scientist Dr. Lyla Healy to a former Soviet laboratory in Eastern Europe, now held by a militia, and recover its 'assets'. His team infiltrates the bunker, but one soldier, Corry, is stabbed with a syringe by an unseen attacker and begins to mutate. In the lab they find Dr. Irvine, Healy's former mentor, who explains that the Soviets, behind in genetic engineering, found what they believed was alien DNA in a meteorite and offered her unfettered research. She created two human-alien hybrids, has bred them, and plans to let the female die in pregnancy to obtain a purer specimen; a horrified soldier kills the female out of mercy. The male hybrid, Goethe, proves intelligent, kills Irvine and escapes. Healy turns on the team, and in the bloody aftermath most of them die. Goethe asks Powell to kill him, but Powell hesitates and the hybrid escapes to Britain.
