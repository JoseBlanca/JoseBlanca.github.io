The genetics

The film's world rests on de-extinction: the dinosaurs were genetically revived and 'hand made' by the geneticist Elizar Kane and Dr. Veronica Shade, and cloning is described as common practice for dinosaurs and other humanoid species, who form a minority facing prejudice from humans. DNA records play a forensic role when a clone database identifies the murdered dinosaur and his makers, opening the investigation. Kane, the creator of the dinosaurs, is the villain: a ruthless billionaire who wants to alter the course of evolution by triggering an Ice Age. The heroes are his creations, presented sympathetically. Critics noted how loosely the film moves from genetically re-engineered dinosaurs to an allegory of an intelligent minority; the science is pure fantasy.

The story

In a futuristic New York where humans live alongside intelligent dinosaurs created in a lab, a dinosaur named Oliver Rex is murdered by an exploding butterfly. Theodore 'Teddy' Rex, a gentle, clumsy Tyrannosaurus who works in police public relations and dreams of being a detective, gets himself assigned to the case and is paired with tough detective Katie Coltrane. The victim is identified through a clone database that records who made him: the billionaire Elizar Kane and Dr. Veronica Shade. Teddy and Coltrane find that Kane, helped by a corrupt official and a weapons-making smuggler, plans to launch a missile that will bring a new Ice Age, wiping out humans and dinosaurs while his own animals hibernate; Oliver was killed because he found out. Teddy stops the missile, captures Kane and is promoted to detective.
