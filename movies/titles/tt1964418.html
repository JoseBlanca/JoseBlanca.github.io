The story

At the 1964 New York World's Fair, a young inventor, Frank Walker, is given a lapel pin by a mysterious girl, Athena, which takes him to Tomorrowland, a futuristic city in another dimension founded by a secret society of visionaries. In the present, Casey Newton, an optimistic teenager who sabotages the demolition of a NASA launch platform, finds a similar pin among her belongings; touching it shows her Tomorrowland, but only for a short time. Searching for answers, she is attacked by robots and rescued by Athena, who turns out to be an Audio-Animatronic recruiter. Athena takes her to the adult Frank, a bitter recluse expelled from Tomorrowland, who has built a machine predicting the end of the world. In Tomorrowland, they discover that its governor, David Nix, has been broadcasting visions of catastrophe to Earth that have become a self-fulfilling prophecy. Athena sacrifices herself to destroy the machine, and Casey and Frank send new recruiters out into the world.
