The story

Sekar is an IT employee who is unhappy with his job. His neighbour Rajarajan is a scientist determined to prove that the DNA of humans and animals can be altered so that people take on animal characteristics. One of his experiments has unexpected results: after Sekar is bitten by Rajarajan's Labrador, Padayappa, the man and the dog swap characteristics, in effect exchanging their souls. The rest of the film follows the consequences of the swap and the pair's efforts to return to their original selves, which they finally manage.
