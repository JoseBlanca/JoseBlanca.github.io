The genetics

Genetically modified crops are the subject. The episode imagines an engineered corn whose trait escapes into a wild plant and then into animals and humans, causing tumours in those with high growth-hormone levels, and it ends on a warning that the contamination cannot be contained. It names real techniques of plant transformation, the particle gun and the bacterium Agrobacterium tumefaciens, and criticises safety testing done only on DNA from middle-aged white men, which misses the people at risk. The scientist-executives of Macroseed are driven by profit, conceal what they know and are ready to kill to protect the product, while the investigator is a local veterinarian. The episode aired amid public controversy over genetically modified crops, including the StarLink corn recall of 2000. Gene flow from crops to related plants is a real subject of study, but a transgene jumping from corn to an unrelated weed and then into people, where it causes tumours, has no basis in science. The episode is best read as an expression of the era's anxieties about agricultural biotechnology.

The story

The biotechnology company Macroseed chooses the farming town of Hobson to test TX-40, a fast-growing genetically engineered corn that promises bigger harvests. The local veterinarian, Dr. Linda Andrews, grows suspicious when a farmer brings in a dead cat from his field with a tumour that sprouts a fifth leg. Aggressive tumours then appear in people, including teenagers and her own nephew. Her husband Rex and her lover Harold Zimmer, both Macroseed employees, insist the corn is safe and that her inquiries could ruin the town's new prosperity. Reading the company's test data, the town doctor explains that crops are modified either with a particle gun or with the tumour-inducing bacterium Agrobacterium tumefaciens, and he and Linda notice that the safety tests used DNA only from middle-aged white men, while the victims all have high levels of growth hormone. TX-40 has crossed from the corn to milkweed and from there to people. Harold admits he knew, shoots the doctor and tries to kill Linda, but Rex stops him. Workers burn the crops, but a crow carries off a piece of contaminated plant.
