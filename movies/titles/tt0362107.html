The genetics

Cloning is the comic premise of this musical. The villain, Victor Gaghan, is a stock mad scientist, a disgraced genetics professor who clones pop stars from memorabilia he buys online, including their underwear, and uses the copies to pursue world domination through pop music. The clones are adults made to order with implanted, programmed memories and a telltale lack of belly buttons, and they have to learn simple pleasures for the first time once they rebel. The film plays the idea entirely for laughs, and its sympathy lies with the clones, who are treated as people in their own right and are freed rather than destroyed. Its science is pure fantasy: a real clone would start life as an embryo and would grow into a much younger twin, without the memories or skills of the original.

The story

The pop group S Club are finishing a tour in Barcelona when their manager Alistair is abducted. The next day they see an S Club performing on television that they do not recognise: perfect copies of themselves, managed by Alistair. Arrested for running out on their hotel bill and for allegedly impersonating S Club, they escape and make their way to Los Angeles. Swapping places with some of their doubles, they discover that the clones have no belly buttons, live by strict rituals and are brainwashed at night. They learn that the clones were made by Victor Gaghan, a former genetics professor fired for illegal cloning experiments, who plans to take over the world through music, since celebrities attract more attention than politicians. At his Hollywood castle, Eagle Peak, the band find Gaghan's other cloned stars, from Elvis Presley to Madonna. Clones and originals join forces, Gaghan is arrested, and the clones end up standing in for the band when it wants a holiday.
