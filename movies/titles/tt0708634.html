The genetics

The episode's genetic element, in this database's sense, is a biological weapon: the blight is a virus the Dominion deliberately inflicted on the Teplans as a lasting warning to others, so that every Teplan is born carrying it and dies young. Against it stands Dr. Bashir, whose biomedical work first fails disastrously, because the virus mutates under the electromagnetic fields of his instruments and kills his volunteers, then succeeds in an unexpected way when his antigen, passing through the placenta, immunises a newborn. The episode contrasts the cruelty of a weaponised disease with the humbling, partial success of medicine, and the doctor's arrogance with the Teplans' acceptance of death. Its mechanisms are fictional, though vaccine protection passed from mother to foetus has real counterparts in maternal immunisation.

The story

Answering a distress call in the Gamma Quadrant, Dr. Bashir, Major Kira and Lieutenant Commander Dax find the Teplan world devastated by the 'blight', a disease the Dominion's Jem'Hadar inflicted on its people two centuries earlier to punish their resistance. Everyone is born with the blight's lesions, which eventually 'quicken' into a fatal red form. Bashir is appalled to find that the local healer, Trevean, offers the quickened only a painless death by poison. He stays to find a cure and develops an antigen, but his volunteers die in agony: the electromagnetic fields of his instruments make the virus mutate. Left behind by Kira and Dax, Bashir works without his equipment to help Ekoria, a pregnant woman who has quickened. She dies after giving birth, but her son is free of the blight: the antigen crossed the placenta and acted as a vaccine. Trevean agrees to give it to every pregnant woman. Back on the station, Bashir is still trying to cure the living.
