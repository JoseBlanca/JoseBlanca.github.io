The genetics

Human cloning is the premise. As in the original Orphan Black, which the Japanese Wikipedia describes as a drama about genetic manipulation and human clones, the heroine discovers that she is one of a set of clones, and the remake's Japanese title refers to 'seven genes'. In the original series, the clones were created and secretly monitored by a biotechnology institute promoting 'Neolution', the deliberate acceleration of human evolution, while one clone, a biology graduate student, uses her science to help her sisters understand what was done to them; the story explores identity, bodily autonomy and the ethics of genetic engineering. The available sources do not describe how the remake handles these elements, so this summary rests on its premise only.

The story

This Japanese remake of the Canadian series Orphan Black, broadcast from December 2017 to January 2018, stars Kang Ji-young as Sara Aoyama, a broke and desperate single mother who witnesses the suicide of a woman who looks exactly like her, and other characters she encounters. In the original series, on which it is based, the heroine takes the dead woman's identity and discovers that she is one of many clones, women of different backgrounds born in the same year, created and secretly monitored by a powerful biotechnology organisation devoted to directing human evolution, and hunted by a religious group opposed to it; with her 'sisters' she investigates her origins. The sources available describe only the remake's premise and cast, not how its story develops or ends.
