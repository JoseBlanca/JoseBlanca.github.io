The genetics

Genetics appears in two ways. The villain, Colonel MacDougall, is a genetically 'improved' child, the product of secret CIA experiments, whose psychic powers make him a weapon, a figure that critics compare to the psychic children of Akira. And the Ark itself is reinterpreted as alien technology that can manipulate genetic structure as well as the weather, a power the Pentagon unit wants to seize for global supremacy. Genetic engineering is thus associated with a covert, power-hungry US military establishment, against Japanese heroes who guard dangerous ancient technology. The film, adapted from a chapter of the manga Spriggan, does not explain the science; the genetics is a genre device in an action anime.

The story

Deep inside Mount Ararat, the secret organisation ARCAM, which studies dangerous relics of ancient civilisations and seals them away, has found what appears to be Noah's Ark. ARCAM sends Yu Ominae, a seventeen-year-old Japanese agent or 'Spriggan', to guard the site, joined by the French Spriggan Jean-Jacques Mondo. They face the US Machine Corps, a clandestine Pentagon unit that wants the Ark as a means of world domination. Its commander, Colonel MacDougall, is a child with lethal psychic powers produced by secret CIA genetic experiments. The Ark turns out to be an alien artefact of immense power, able to control the weather and alter genetic structure, and MacDougall seizes it to turn that power against the world. Yu and Mondo fight cyborgs and psychic attacks to stop him, in a climax of large-scale destruction.
