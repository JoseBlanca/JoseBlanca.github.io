Go Fish

1998 Episode

Buffy the Vampire Slayer, season 2, episode 20

The genetics

The episode uses genetic manipulation as a twisted form of doping. A coach obsessed with winning the state championship, assisted by the school nurse, exposes his swimmers to fish DNA hidden in the sauna steam, supposedly to make them faster in the water; instead they turn into fish–human hybrids that kill and eat their teammates. The experimenters are not professional scientists but school staff, and the episode connects the experiment with its main satirical theme: the privileges granted to star athletes and the willingness of adults to cover up anything in the name of victory. Both experimenters end up killed by their own creations. The premise is pure fantasy: exposure to another species' DNA does not transform an organism, and the episode makes no attempt at a scientific explanation.

The story

Members of the Sunnydale High School swim team, the Razorbacks, are being killed one by one: all that is left of each victim is his skin, next to a creature resembling the Creature from the Black Lagoon. Buffy and her friends suspect that the team's best swimmers are the targets and that they are taking steroids. Xander joins the team as an undercover investigator and discovers that a substance is being pumped into the sauna steam, which he is inhaling too. The truth is that the swimmers are not being eaten but transformed: Coach Marin, with the help of school nurse Greenliegh, has been experimenting on them with fish DNA to improve their performance, and the creatures are the swimmers themselves, shedding their human skin. When the nurse wants to stop the experiments, Marin pushes her into the sewer, where the creatures kill her. Marin later dies the same way, and Xander and the surviving swimmers are treated in time.
