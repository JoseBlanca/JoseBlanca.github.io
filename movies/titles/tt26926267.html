The genetics

Genetics is incidental. The protagonist, Lana, is a geneticist who leads a medical research lab, but her profession plays no part in the story beyond characterising her as an accomplished scientist; the film is a romantic comedy about a destination wedding and an old romance rekindled.

The story

Emma Winslow, who works for the resort company Discovery in London, accepts her boyfriend RJ's proposal and tells her mother Lana, a geneticist who leads a medical research lab in San Francisco, that the wedding will take place in a month at a resort in Phuket, paid for by Discovery in exchange for publicity. In Thailand, Lana discovers that RJ's father is Will Jackson, the college boyfriend who disappeared from her life thirty years earlier, and fears RJ will hurt Emma the same way. Excluded from the planning by Discovery's brand manager, Lana clashes with Emma after a skinny-dipping escapade, while her best friend Janice sets her up with a doctor. Stranded with Will on a remote beach, Lana makes peace with him, then suspects him again after overhearing a call. After the wedding, Will explains the misunderstanding and proposes to her with the ring he bought decades ago.
