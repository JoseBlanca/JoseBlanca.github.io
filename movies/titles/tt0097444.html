The genetics

Genetic engineering is at the heart of the film. Godzilla's cells are treated as a valuable biological resource, and governments, a company and spies compete for them, foreshadowing later stories about corporate control of genetic material. Biollante is a hybrid of plant, human and Godzilla cells, created by a grieving geneticist who wants to keep his dead daughter's soul alive; his motive is personal rather than commercial, but his experiment produces a monster that he cannot control. Other scientists work for the army on a bacterial weapon derived from Godzilla's cells. The story came from a public contest won by a dentist whose entry focused on the dilemmas of biotechnology, and director Kazuki Ōmori drew on his own background in biology to make the genetic engineering plot more plausible. Scholars of kaiju cinema have noted that the film criticised genetic engineering and biotechnology several years before Jurassic Park brought the subject to mainstream Hollywood. Merging whole cells of different species to transfer a person's identity is, of course, fantasy.

The story

After Godzilla's attack on Tokyo, samples of its cells are smuggled to a research institute in the fictional Middle Eastern country of Saradia, to be merged with genetically modified plants that could turn the desert into farmland. A terrorist bombing destroys the laboratory and kills Erika, the daughter of the geneticist Genshiro Shiragami. Five years later, in Japan, Shiragami has merged Erika's cells with those of a rose to preserve her soul, while the scientist Kazuhito Kirishima and the army are using Godzilla cells to develop an "anti-nuclear energy bacteria" as a weapon against the monster. Saradian agents and the American company Bio-Major fight over the cells. Shiragami secretly adds Godzilla cells to one of his roses, which grows into the giant plant monster Biollante. When Godzilla is freed, it fights Biollante twice; the bacteria and an artificial lightning storm weaken Godzilla, and Biollante dissolves into spores that rise to the sky in the image of Erika.
