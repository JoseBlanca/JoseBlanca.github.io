The genetics

The episode rewrites the Time Lords' origins in biological terms. Tecteun, an explorer, studied a foundling child who could regenerate, subjected her to repeated forced regenerations, and grafted the ability into her own species, the Shobogans, turning them into the Time Lords, who then limited themselves to twelve regenerations. The Doctor is that child, so her genetics are the template for Time Lord society, a fact the Master resents because it means he was made from her. The Master in turn uses the Cyberium and the bodies of dead Time Lords to create CyberMasters, Cybermen that can regenerate. The revelation casts the Time Lords' defining trait as the product of experiments on a child, carried out by an ambitious scientist and covered up by the Division, and the Master as the villain who weaponises it. The genetics of regeneration is not explained; it is a narrative device of the series.

The story

In the conclusion of a two-part story, the Master takes the Thirteenth Doctor to the ruins of Gallifrey and traps her in the Matrix, the Time Lords' repository of knowledge, to reveal their secret history. The explorer Tecteun found a child from another dimension who could regenerate, adopted and studied her, and grafted the ability into her own people, creating the Time Lords; that Timeless Child is the Doctor, whose earlier lives were erased from her memory after she served a secret organisation, the Division. Meanwhile, the Master seizes the Cyberium from the Cyber-Controller Ashad and uses it and the bodies of dead Time Lords to create regenerating CyberMasters. The Doctor's companions escape a Cyber-ship to Gallifrey. Encouraged by a past incarnation, the Doctor overloads the Matrix with her memories and escapes; Ko Sharmus detonates a Death Particle that destroys the Cybermen and Gallifrey, the Master escapes, and the Doctor is imprisoned by the Judoon.
