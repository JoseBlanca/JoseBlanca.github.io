The genetics

Genetic engineering creates the disaster. Dr. Peter Axelrod, a government agricultural scientist, has engineered a hybrid super-locust by crossing the Australian plague locust with the desert locust, producing insects that resist pesticides such as DDT; once a few escape, the swarms devour crops across the United States and even turn on people. The film follows the familiar formula of genetic tinkering with a harmless creature, accidental release, and scientists racing to find a solution before the military takes drastic action. Axelrod is responsible for the catastrophe, but he admits it, tries to help and dies saving the others, while the heroine, a government pest scientist, stops the military's plan to use nerve gas. Real locust species cannot be crossed to produce pesticide-proof, carnivorous swarms.

The story

At a Department of Agriculture laboratory in Virginia, Dr. Maddy Rierdon, an agriculture official specializing in pests, discovers that her former professor and boss, Dr. Peter Axelrod, has bred a hybrid of the Australian plague locust and the desert locust that shrugs off DDT. She orders the insects destroyed and fires him, but a few escape, some through a sink and others when samples taken by the military are dropped in California. Within weeks, swarms are stripping farms in California and attacking people in the East, putting Peter's daughter in a coma, and the eastern swarm turns carnivorous. Peter, arrested by the FBI, warns Maddy that the authorities plan to use VX nerve gas, and Maddy wrecks the military's test run. Sheltering in a grain silo, Peter dies fetching fuel so that the others can electrify it. Finally the national power grid is rerouted into two giant electric traps that destroy the swarms.
