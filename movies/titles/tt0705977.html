The genetics

Genetics is the label on the episode's parody conspiracy. Towelie is a genetically modified towel made by the company Tynacorp, aliens plan to conquer the Earth with genetically modified towels, and, according to the Russian Wikipedia summary, the military wants to capture Towelie to copy his DNA for weapons of mass destruction. Corporation, military and aliens are all shown as scheming and violent, and the episode resolves the plot by blowing up the building. The science is never more than a phrase: the point is to spoof conspiracy thrillers and, as Wikipedia notes, to mock South Park's own merchandising through a deliberately shallow character who exists to sell products. The boys' total indifference to the plot is the main joke.

The story

Stan's mother buys the boys an Okama Gamesphere console, and they plan to play all weekend, but they are pestered by Towelie, a talking towel who appears whenever water is mentioned, reminds them to bring a towel and asks if they want to get high. The console is stolen, and a ransom call demands that they hand Towelie over at a gas station at night. There an old man from Tynacorp, the company that made Towelie, receives him, and the United States military ambushes the meeting. For the rest of the episode the boys shuttle between a secret military base and Tynacorp, caring only about their console while a conspiracy unfolds in which aliens plan to take over the Earth with genetically modified towels. The Tynacorp building is blown up, Kenny falls into molten metal, and Towelie defeats the evil prototype towel GS-401 and saves the boys.
