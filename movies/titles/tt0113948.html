The genetics

Genetic engineering creates the heroine. In the backstory, rebel scientists fighting the cyborgs develop a new, "perfected" or artificial strain of DNA and implant it in a pregnant volunteer; the child, Alex, grows up with superhuman strength and endurance, which is why the cyborgs send a killer back in time to eliminate her. The scientists never appear on screen and are presented only as the human resistance, so genetic enhancement here is a weapon of liberation against machine oppressors, the reverse of the more common story in which engineered people are the threat. The film does not explain the science, and the reviews treat it as a low-budget Terminator variant in which the genetic premise mainly justifies an action heroine.

The story

In a future where humans have lost the Cyborg Wars and live as slaves of their cyborg masters, rebel scientists create a child with a new, perfected strain of DNA, carried by a pregnant volunteer, in the hope that it will mean the end of the cyborgs. When the cyborgs mark mother and baby for termination, the mother steals a cyborg ship with a time machine and escapes to East Africa in 1980, where pursuing cyborgs kill her. A local tribe saves the baby and raises her as Alex. Twenty-two years later, as she becomes the tribe's first woman warrior, the cyborg bounty hunter Nebula arrives from the future and slaughters the tribe. Fleeing across the desert, between local rebels and a pair of American smugglers, Alex discovers superhuman abilities and finally defeats Nebula, but another cyborg lands as the film ends.
