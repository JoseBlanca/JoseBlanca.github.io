The story

Submarine designer Wick Hayes is blamed for the loss of the experimental submarine Siren I in the Dannekin Rift, a deep trench off Norway, and insists that modifications made by the Contek corporation are to blame. Contek sends him down in the Siren II under the tough Captain Phillips with a NATO crew to find the missing sub's black box. Trapped in a field of toxic weed at a depth where plant life should be impossible, Hayes frees the ship by electrifying the hull, but some of the weed gets inside. Following the black box signal into an underwater cave, the crew discovers that Contek has been running illegal genetic engineering experiments there, which have produced a range of mutant creatures that attack them. The climax brings a fight with a company double agent and the destruction of the submarine.
