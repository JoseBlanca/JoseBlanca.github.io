The genetics

Genetics is only in the background of this episode. The Emohawk is a domesticated polymorph, a shape-changing creature that feeds on emotions, kept as a pet by the chief of a GELF tribe; it brings back the creature type from the Series III episode 'Polymorph'. This episode does not explain where the creature comes from or how it works, and no one in the story does any genetic work. The polymorph serves as a comic device for draining the crew's character traits and reviving two alternative personas from earlier episodes, Duane Dibbley and Ace Rimmer. Critics considered it one of the weaker episodes of the sixth series. Creatures that drain personality traits are pure fantasy.

The story

Charged with looting derelict ships by a computer-controlled Space Corps enforcement vessel and sentenced to death, the crew of Starbug escape but crash-land on a moon inhabited by GELFs. Needing a new oxygen generator, they bargain with the Kinitawowi tribe, but the price turns out to be Lister's marriage to the chief's daughter. Lister flees, the crew escape with the parts, and the angry chief sends his pet Emohawk, a domesticated polymorph, after them. While Lister and Kryten install the new parts, the creature drains the others of personality traits: the Cat loses his cool and becomes the hapless Duane Dibbley, and Rimmer loses his bitterness and becomes the heroic Ace Rimmer. After Rimmer proposes ejecting himself and the Cat through an airlock, the crew agree instead to track down and freeze the creature. Rimmer asks to stay Ace for another day, while the Cat asks to be changed back at once, but as Duane he accidentally freezes the others.
