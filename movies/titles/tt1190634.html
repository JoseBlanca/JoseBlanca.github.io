The genetics

Biological enhancement drives the whole satire. In the series, superpowers are not natural: they are produced by Compound V, a substance that Vought secretly administers, often to children with their parents' consent, and the company maintains the fiction that its heroes were born with their powers. The series ties the substance to Nazi science: Frederick Vought, a German scientist, created Compound V for the Nazis before defecting to the Allies and founding the company, and his first successful test subject, his wife Klara, reappears decades later as Stormfront, who dreams of an army of Aryan supermen. Homelander himself was raised in a laboratory by Vought scientists. Later seasons add a scientist, Sameer Shah, who develops a virus able to kill Supes. The series uses these elements to satirise corporate power, celebrity culture and the eugenic fantasy of a superior race. Compound V is unexplained fiction: no single substance could produce such varied, specific abilities.

The story

In a world where superpowered people, the "Supes", are celebrities owned and marketed by the corporation Vought International, most of them are in private corrupt, reckless or cruel. After his girlfriend is killed by the speedster A-Train, Hughie Campbell joins the Boys, a group of vigilantes led by Billy Butcher, who hates Supes because Homelander, leader of Vought's team the Seven, raped his wife. The Boys discover that the Supes' powers come from Compound V, a substance Vought keeps secret: the newest member of the Seven, Starlight, learns that she was not born with her powers and that her mother let Vought make her a Supe. Later seasons reveal that Vought's founder created Compound V for the Nazis, introduce the Nazi Supe Stormfront and the legendary Soldier Boy, and follow Homelander's growing grip on politics and the Boys' search for a virus able to kill all Supes. The fifth and final season premiered in 2026.
