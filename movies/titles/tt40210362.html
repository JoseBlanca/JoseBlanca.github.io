The genetics

The genetic element is a cloned Adolf Hitler, kept in a cryogenic chamber beneath the Wolf's Lair by the neo-Nazi general Wilhelm Reuss, whose scientists, including Dr. Friedrich Metzger, are working on it in order to found a Fourth Reich. Reuss kills guests and drains their blood as part of the project. The clone plays only a brief part: Becky shoots it as soon as it steps out of its tank, and she later kills the scientists. Cloning here is a shorthand for a Nazi revival, a familiar idea in fiction since The Boys from Brazil, and the sources give no detail of how the clone was made. Real cloning, if it were attempted, would produce a genetic twin who would grow up with his own personality in a different world, not a reborn dictator.

The story

Two years after the events of The Wrath of Becky, eighteen-year-old Becky Hooper works for the CIA but is repeatedly suspended for her violent methods. Confined to her New York apartment on probation, she is recruited by Director Baker for a rescue mission. Baker reveals that the neo-Nazi General Wilhelm Reuss organized an earlier jailbreak, and that the Valknut key once hidden under Becky's lake house was placed there by Reuss's great-grandfather and opens a hidden chamber beneath the Wolf's Lair in Poland. Going undercover, Becky is captured and discovers that Reuss has been killing guests and draining their blood, and that the chamber holds a clone of Adolf Hitler in a cryogenic tank, meant to found a Fourth Reich. Realizing that Baker sent her on a suicide mission, she goes rogue, kills the clone as it emerges, exposes the plot on television and kills Reuss's men and scientists, and finally Reuss, who has killed her love interest Rigby. Wounded, she collapses as the Polish military bombs the site.
