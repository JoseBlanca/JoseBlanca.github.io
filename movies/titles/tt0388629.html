The genetics

Genetics matters in one arc of a vast adventure series. Vinsmoke Judge is a scientist specialised in engineering and genetics, a former member of the rogue research group MADS alongside the great scientist Vegapunk. Together they discovered the "Lineage Factor", the series' equivalent of a hereditary blueprint, and Judge later used it to modify his unborn children into super-soldiers and to build an army of cloned soldiers for Germa 66. He forced his wife Sora to undergo the procedure against her wishes; she secretly took a counter-drug, which is why Sanji alone remained a normal human, kind and capable of empathy. Judge is portrayed as a cold, power-hungry father who values his children only as weapons, and the story frames the emotionless, enhanced siblings as the true cost of his project. The science is pure fantasy, but the arc presents eugenic enhancement as dehumanising and links it explicitly to militarism and dynastic ambition.

The story

Based on Eiichiro Oda's manga, this long-running anime follows Monkey D. Luffy, a young man whose body became like rubber after he ate a Devil Fruit, and his crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, as they sail the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the One Piece, so that Luffy can become King of the Pirates. In the Whole Cake Island arc, the crew's cook, Sanji, is forced to return to his birth family, the Vinsmoke royal family of the Germa Kingdom, a nation of mercenaries ruled by his father, Vinsmoke Judge. Judge, a scientist, had altered his children before birth to make them superhuman soldiers. Sanji's siblings gained great strength and lost much of their capacity for emotion, but Sanji remained an ordinary human, so Judge despised him, locked him away as a child and later let him leave on condition that he never revealed his origins.
