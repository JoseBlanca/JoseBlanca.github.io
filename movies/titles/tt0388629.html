The genetics

Genetics matters in two arcs of a vast adventure series. Vinsmoke Judge, a scientist specialised in engineering and genetics, once belonged to the rogue research group MADS alongside the great scientist Vegapunk. Together they discovered the "Lineage Factor", the series' equivalent of a hereditary blueprint, and Judge later used it to modify his unborn sons into super-soldiers and to build an army of cloned soldiers for Germa 66. He forced his wife Sora to undergo the procedure against her wishes; she secretly took a counter-drug, which is why Sanji alone was born a normal human, kind and capable of empathy. His sister Reiju was also modified and kept her emotions, but cannot disobey Judge. The story frames the emotionless brothers as the true cost of Judge's project, and when Sanji's enhancements later awaken he stays compassionate. The Egghead arc adds Vegapunk's cloned living weapons, the Pacifista and Seraphim, MADS's first human clone, Stussy, and the cruel experiments of the World Government elder Saturn. The science is fantasy, but the series presents eugenic enhancement as dehumanising and tied to militarism and dynastic ambition.

How genetics is portrayed

Overall portrayal of genetics
mixed Judge's dehumanising enhancement of his own children and the World Government's cloned weapons show genetics co-opted for conquest and control, but the Egghead arc centres on Vegapunk, a compassionate scientist who wants his Lineage Factor research to bring peace, cures Bonney and opposes erasing free will, and who is murdered by the Government that exploited his work; science is good in his hands and harmful when powerful institutions seize it.
Outcome of the genetic intervention
negative Judge's modification produces cruel, emotionless sons and a daughter unable to disobey him, kills Sora through the counter-drug and leads to Sanji's imprisonment and abuse; the clones and Seraphim serve as obedient weapons; Sanji's late awakening and S-Snake's loyalty are minor positives.
Vinsmoke Judge
villain Aim: military, power (negative). King of Germa and a geneticist who forces his pregnant wife to undergo surgery altering their sons' Lineage Factor so they become emotionless super-soldiers, and clones an army for Germa 66, to reconquer the North Blue; the story presents him as a cruel, cowardly antagonist whose 'failed' unmodified son Sanji ends up saving the family.
Dr. Vegapunk
hero Aim: benevolent, curiosity, military (mixed). The great scientist co-discovered the Lineage Factor and creates artificial Devil Fruits, cloned Pacifista and the Seraphim; he wants science to bring peace and free energy but builds these living weapons for the World Government. The series treats him as a compassionate ally of the heroes who weeps at erasing Kuma's will and is murdered by the Government.
World Government (Elder Saturn)
villain Aim: military, power (negative). It bans cloning for others while commissioning cloned weapons, and Saturn orders Kuma's free will erased to make the Pacifista obedient; Saturn is the cruel main antagonist of Egghead.

The story

Based on Eiichiro Oda's manga, this long-running anime follows Monkey D. Luffy, whose body became like rubber after he ate a Devil Fruit, and his crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, as they sail the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the One Piece. In the Whole Cake Island arc, the crew's cook, Sanji, is forced to return to his birth family, the Vinsmoke royals of the mercenary Germa Kingdom, ruled by his father, Vinsmoke Judge. Judge, a scientist, had altered his children before birth to make them superhuman soldiers. Sanji's brothers gained great strength and lost much of their capacity for emotion, and his sister Reiju cannot disobey their father, but Sanji was born an ordinary human, so Judge despised him, locked him away as a child and later let him leave on condition that he never revealed his origins. In the later Wano arc Sanji's dormant enhancements awaken while he keeps his emotions, and the Egghead arc takes the crew to the island of the scientist Vegapunk and his cloned soldiers.
