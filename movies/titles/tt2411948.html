The genetics

This episode reveals the genetic origin of the series' villains. The Observers are the product of a 22nd-century experiment that engineered human genes to trade emotion for intelligence, and they reproduce asexually from their own genes. Michael, a child produced from September's genes, is an anomaly who combines emotion with intelligence, and the heroes' plan is to show him to the original scientists to prove that their premise was wrong, thereby preventing the experiment and erasing the Observers. The researchers of 2167 are never seen; the story treats their work as a well-meant but disastrous mistake that produced cold conquerors, and it closes with a message about love and what makes us human. Real genetics offers no way of exchanging emotion for intelligence; both are complex traits shaped by many genes and by environment.

The story

In the final season of Fringe, set in a future ruled by the Observers, Walter Bishop explores visions implanted in his mind by the Observer child Michael and realises that 'Donald', the man who helped him plan the Observers' defeat, is September. The team finds September in Brooklyn, now human: the Observers stripped him of his implant and biologically reverted him for helping the Fringe Division. He explains that the Observers were born from an experiment in 2167 that developed human genes by sacrificing emotion for intelligence, and that Michael, produced from his genes by the Observers' asexual reproduction, is an anomaly with both emotion and great intelligence. September plans to send Michael to 2167 so that the scientists see their error and the Observers never exist. Meanwhile Captain Windmark tracks them down, and in the end Michael gives himself up to the Observers.
