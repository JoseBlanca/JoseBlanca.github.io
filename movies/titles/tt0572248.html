The genetics

Human genetic enhancement is the subject of the episode, which ends the Enterprise arc about the Augments, descendants of the engineered supermen of Star Trek's Eugenics Wars, and links the series to Khan in the original series. Dr. Arik Soong is an idealistic scientist who sees himself as the Augments' father and believes they represent the future, yet the episode shows his creations as arrogant, aggressive and willing to commit genocide. Soong's attempt to edit aggression out of the embryos provokes the mutiny, and his final conclusion is that genetic engineering of humans may be a dead end, and he turns instead to artificial life, foreshadowing the android Data. The episode repeats Star Trek's recurring message that enhancement brings ambition and violence along with superior abilities. Genetic enhancement of human traits, as opposed to the treatment of disease, remains hypothetical.

The story

Concluding a three-part story, Dr. Arik Soong and the "Augments", genetically enhanced humans he raised from embryos, escape Cold Station 12 with 1,800 frozen Augment embryos from the time of the Eugenics Wars and pathogen samples. Captain Archer pursues them into Klingon space in the Enterprise. Soong wants to hide in a remote region and raise the embryos, but Malik, the Augments' leader, plans to fire a pathogen-filled torpedo at a Klingon colony to start a war between the Klingons and Starfleet. When Soong tries to remove aggressive traits from the embryos, Malik mutinies; Soong escapes and helps Archer. The Enterprise destroys the torpedo, Malik scuttles his ship, killing the other Augments and the embryos, and Archer kills Malik when he tries to murder Soong. Back in custody, Soong begins to doubt that humans can be improved through genetic engineering and turns his thoughts to artificial life.
