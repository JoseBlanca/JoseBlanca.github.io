The genetics

The genetic element is minimal: the zombie outbreak starts in a laboratory, where, according to the German sources, a man mutated by a virus escapes and kills a janitor and a scientist. The film never explains what the laboratory was doing or who ran it, and the scientist appears only as an early victim. The director described the film as a gory R-rated mix of The Goonies and Gremlins, and the outbreak is simply a starting point for teenage comedy and splatter. As in many zombie films, a pathogen from a laboratory serves as a convenient source of disaster without any interest in the science behind it.

The story

In this horror comedy, a janitor in a laboratory accidentally disconnects a man lying under observation, who awakens as a zombie and bites him and the scientist, starting an outbreak in a small town. Three teenage Scouts, Ben, Carter and Augie, are about to go camping, though Ben and Carter, tired of being mocked, are thinking of quitting the Scouts. That night Ben and Carter sneak away to a senior party, abandoning Augie, while the zombies overrun the town. With Denise, a cocktail waitress handy with a shotgun, they survive attacks in a strip club, a police station and a neighbour's house, and learn that the army intends to bomb the town. Using their Scout skills, the three friends build weapons, rescue the partygoers and destroy the zombies with a homemade bomb before the military arrives.
