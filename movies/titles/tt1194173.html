The genetics

Genetic enhancement is at the heart of the plot. Operation Outcome, run for the US defence and intelligence apparatus with the pharmaceutical company Sterisyn Morlanta, creates super-agents whose physical and mental abilities are boosted by pills ('chems') and by a virus that makes the enhancements permanent; Cross depends on the blue pills to keep his raised intelligence. Dr. Marta Shearing, a scientist on the programme and the only one to survive its purge, becomes Cross's ally and uses the live virus to free him from the pills. The programme's overseers, led by Colonel Byer, treat agents and scientists as disposable and already have a next generation of super-soldiers. The film thus contrasts a sinister state enhancement programme with an individual scientist who turns the same technology to liberation. Viral vectors are real gene-therapy tools, but nothing like a single infection that raises intelligence exists.

The story

Six weeks after Jason Bourne's escape, the exposure of the CIA programmes Treadstone and Blackbriar leads Colonel Eric Byer, who oversees covert enhancement programmes, to shut down Operation Outcome and kill its agents and doctors. Outcome agent Aaron Cross, training in Alaska, survives a drone strike and fools his pursuers into thinking he is dead. At Sterisyn Morlanta, the pharmaceutical company behind Outcome, a researcher shoots his colleagues, leaving Dr. Marta Shearing as the only surviving scientist. Cross, running out of the pills ('chems') that sustain his enhancements, saves Shearing from assassins. She explains that a virus has already made his physical enhancements permanent, but he still needs pills for his mental ones. They fly to Manila, where Shearing infects him with the live virus at the company's lab. After he recovers, they escape the police and a 'Beta' super-soldier, LARX #3, through the city, and flee by boat.
