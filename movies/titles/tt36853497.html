The genetics

Genetics plays a supporting role in this fantasy. The protagonist's condition, a body that changes sex every twelve hours with two separate personalities, is presented as a divine gift rather than a biological phenomenon, but it becomes an object of science when the founder of the biotechnology startup where the female Rajini works sees the change on a security camera. Fascinated, he wants to study it through unauthorised genetic experimentation, and when she refuses he kidnaps her; the violent confrontation wounds the shared heart, and the transplant that saves her ends the male Rajini's existence. The scientist is thus the villain, a researcher who treats a person as a specimen. The final twist is hereditary: Rajini's child inherits the same condition. Real people with differences of sex development do exist, but nothing like a daily switch between two bodies is biologically possible.

The story

In this fantasy romantic comedy, an elderly couple who have long been unable to have a child pray and are promised two in one by a divine being. Their child, Rajini, is a girl from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. and a boy from 6 p.m. to 6 a.m.; the two share only a heart. The daytime Rajini grows up with school and friends, while the night-time Rajini is homeschooled and isolated, and they resent each other, communicating only through notes. After their parents die, she becomes a researcher at a biotechnology startup and falls in love with her colleague Karthik, while he, a bartender, falls for Priya. Their cycle becomes unstable, triggered by emotions, and they begin to feel each other's feelings. The startup's founder discovers the secret and kidnaps Rajini to study her; in the fight he wounds their shared heart. After a heart transplant, the male Rajini disappears for good. She marries Karthik, and their baby inherits the condition.
