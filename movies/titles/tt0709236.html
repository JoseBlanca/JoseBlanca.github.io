The genetics

The genetic element is Dr. Carson Beckett's experimental retrovirus, the season's central project, meant to turn a Wraith into a human. In this episode a Wraith girl raised by a human takes an unfinished version of the drug in the hope of becoming human herself, without considering the consequences. The source summary does not say how the drug affects her, but the episode sets up the dangers of the retrovirus that the following episode, Conversion, develops when it turns a human into an Iratus bug hybrid. Beckett's unfinished work is thus used before it is ready. Turning one species into another with a drug remains pure fiction.

The story

In this second-season episode of Stargate Atlantis, the Atlantis team investigating a planet comes across a young Wraith girl who survived the crash of a Wraith ship years earlier. She has been raised in secret by a human man, who hides her from the people of the nearby village because he fears for her life. The girl longs to be human and takes an incomplete version of a drug developed by Dr. Carson Beckett to turn Wraith into humans, without considering the consequences. Meanwhile, the team learns that another Wraith who survived the crash is killing villagers.
