The genetics

This remake turns the 1958 alien into a man-made threat. The Blob is a biological warfare experiment from the Cold War, created by a secret government programme and launched into space because it was too dangerous to keep; there, according to Wikipedia, it fed on bacteria mutated by radiation and grew into a predatory mass. The sources do not describe how it was made, only that it is a government bioweapon. Its keeper, Dr. Meddows, poses as a scientist preparing for first contact but is ready to sacrifice the town to recover the weapon, and is consumed by it. As Wikipedia notes, the film is a post-Watergate conspiracy story in which rebellious teenagers oppose sinister government agents, and it ends ominously with a surviving fragment. The organism has no real biological counterpart.

The story

A "meteorite" falls near the Californian ski town of Arborville, releasing a gelatinous organism that dissolves an old vagrant and, at the hospital, Paul, the football star who brought him in with the cheerleader Meg Penny and the local rebel Brian Flagg. The police do not believe Meg and Brian as the growing Blob kills a diner's staff and the sheriff. At the crash site they find a military operation led by Dr. Meddows, a government scientist who quarantines the town. Brian escapes and overhears that the Blob is a Cold War biological weapon, sent into orbit as too dangerous, that grew in space and brought its satellite down; Meddows means to recapture it, using townspeople as bait. Brian exposes him in front of the town before the Blob devours him, and the creature overruns the town until Meg and Brian freeze it with liquid nitrogen. A fragment survives in the jar of a deranged preacher.
