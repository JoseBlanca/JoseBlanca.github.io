The genetics

Genetic engineering explains Stitch's existence and his nature. Jumba Jookiba is a stock mad scientist made comic: an alien geneticist convicted for illegal experiments who designed Experiment 626 as a weapon, with great strength, near-indestructibility and an urge to destroy. The film then sets up a contest between design and upbringing: Stitch was engineered to destroy, but Lilo's affection and the idea of ʻohana gradually change his behaviour, and by the end his creator, too, has become part of the family. The message is that a creature's genetic programming does not fix its destiny, a light-hearted version of the nature-versus-nurture debate. The film does not dwell on the science, and Stitch's abilities are pure fantasy. According to the filmmakers, the relationship of creator and creation was not part of the original concept: Stitch began as the leader of an intergalactic gang with Jumba as a former crony, and the two were recast as scientist and experiment after test screenings.

The story

On the planet Turo, the scientist Dr. Jumba Jookiba is convicted by the United Galactic Federation of illegal genetic experimentation. His creation, Experiment 626, an aggressive, almost indestructible creature built for destruction, is sentenced to exile but escapes and crash-lands on Kauaʻi, Hawaii, where it poses as a dog at an animal shelter. Lilo, a lonely girl being raised by her older sister Nani after their parents' death, adopts him and names him Stitch. Stitch's destructive behaviour costs Nani her job and strengthens the doubts of the social worker Cobra Bubbles about her ability to care for Lilo. Jumba and the agent Pleakley are sent to recapture him, and their fights destroy the family's house. When Captain Gantu captures Lilo, Stitch, who has learned from her the Hawaiian idea of ʻohana, or family, asks for help, and Jumba joins in the rescue. The Grand Councilwoman lets Stitch stay on Earth with Lilo, and Jumba and Pleakley join the family.
