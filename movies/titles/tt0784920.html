The genetics

Genetic enhancement is the origin of the heroes' powers. Andrew Hartford, the team's wealthy mentor, modifies the DNA of the five recruits to give each of them a superpower: super strength for Mack, super hearing and telescopic vision for Will, super agility for Dax, super speed for Ronny and invisibility for Rose. The enhancement is presented as a benevolent gift that equips the team to protect the world, alongside Hartford's technology and the magic of the crown, and it has no harmful consequences in the story. Hartford is a father figure and a positive practitioner, although he also built his son as a robot with false memories, a revelation the series uses to explore identity rather than genetics. As in most superhero fiction, the idea that editing DNA in adults could produce such powers has no basis in real science.

The story

In this season of the Power Rangers franchise, the billionaire explorer Andrew Hartford discovers the Corona Aurora, a powerful crown whose five jewels were scattered across the Earth by its guardian, the Sentinel Knight. The discovery frees two villainous brothers, Flurious and Moltor, who were turned into monsters and banished after trying to steal the crown long ago. To find the jewels before them, Hartford recruits five young adventurers, Mack (his son), Will, Dax, Ronny and Rose, gives them genetic enhancements and high-tech equipment, and makes them the Overdrive Rangers. Travelling around the world, they also face other villains, such as Kamdor and the Fearcats, and are joined by Tyzonn, an alien who can turn his body into mercury. An anniversary episode brings back veteran Rangers. Late in the series Mack learns that he is a robot built by his father with implanted memories; he sacrifices himself to destroy Flurious and is made truly human by the crown.
