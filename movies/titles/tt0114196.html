The genetics

Genetic engineering creates the monster. On a hidden oil-rig laboratory, scientists funded by the dying millionaire Brinkstone, who wants eternal life, combine human and animal DNA and develop an 'artificial, concentrated DNA' called Proteus; tested on a great white shark, it turns the animal into a formless, immortal creature that absorbs other organisms, keeps their knowledge and memories, and imitates them. The scientists, recruited with promises of unlimited research and wealth, become its victims, and a video warning from Dr. Shelley explains the disaster. The film is a low-budget horror thriller in the mould of The Thing and Alien, using genetics as a premise for a shape-shifting monster; a DNA preparation that confers immortality and absorbs other organisms has no basis in real biology.

The story

Undercover agents Alex and Rachel infiltrate a group of heroin smugglers on a trip to Hong Kong. When their yacht sinks in a storm, the survivors drift on a raft with their cargo to an apparently deserted oil rig, which turns out to be a secret biological research station owned by Leonard Brinkstone, a millionaire who has funded the search for an elixir of eternal life. The few remaining staff, Dr. Shelley, Dr. Carol Soames and the guard Buckley, behave strangely. The group learns that the scientists' artificial DNA, tested on a great white shark called Charlie, turned it into a shapeless, immortal creature that absorbs its victims and takes on their form and memories. One by one the castaways are absorbed and imitated, and the survivors fight the creature, which is attracted by heroin, with grenades and fire, until Brinkstone himself arrives on the rig.
