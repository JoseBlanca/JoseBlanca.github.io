The genetics

Cloning and genetic engineering are central to the episode. A secret government programme created clones with enhanced abilities, and one of them, posing as a fertility doctor, secretly uses patients' IVF treatment to produce new copies of herself, so the parents unknowingly raise her clones. The episode links the scientists to eugenics and military research and shows the results as monstrous: the clones are brilliant and strong but psychopathic, and the girls kill their fathers. The idea that "extra chromosomes" produce superhuman traits has no basis; in reality, an extra chromosome is a cause of genetic disorders such as Down syndrome, and most extra autosomes are lethal to the embryo. The episode also plays on the fear, common in the 1990s, that reproductive technology could be secretly misused, and uses identical twins separated at birth as the hook of the mystery.

The story

Two men are killed at the same moment on opposite sides of the United States, one in Connecticut and one in California, each drained of blood through incisions in the neck. FBI agents Mulder and Scully find that each victim had a young daughter, Teena and Cindy, who look identical. Both girls were conceived by in vitro fertilization at a San Francisco clinic, where Dr. Sally Kendrick had been fired for eugenics experiments with patients' eggs. Deep Throat tells Mulder about a Cold War programme that produced genetically engineered clones called "Adams" and "Eves". In a psychiatric hospital the agents meet Eve 6, who looks exactly like Kendrick and explains that the clones had extra chromosomes that gave them superhuman intelligence and strength but also homicidal psychosis. Kendrick, really Eve 7, cloned the girls from herself. The girls poison her, then try to poison the agents. They are locked up alongside Eve 6, where Eve 8 comes to visit them.
