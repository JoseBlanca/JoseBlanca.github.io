The genetics

Pluribus is science fiction rather than a story about genetics, but its catastrophe begins in a molecular biology laboratory. The alien signal contains a genetic sequence for an RNA virus, and the scientists who synthesise it unleash a pandemic that rewires human minds. The virus spreads both through contact and through deliberate dispersal by the infected, and immunity in a handful of people becomes the central puzzle. Later, the hive mind tries to overcome that immunity by tailoring the virus to each individual, starting from stem cells extracted from their tissues; for Carol, it plans to use her frozen eggs, while respecting a rule that it needs the person's consent. The series uses these ideas to ask what is lost in a world without conflict or individuality. The series treats genetic information as something that can be sent across space as a message, but a virus able to merge human consciousness is pure fiction.

The story

Astronomers detect a radio signal from space that encodes the sequence of a viral RNA. Researchers synthesise it and test it on laboratory rats, but an outbreak begins when a researcher is bitten, and the infected work together to spread the virus around the world. Almost all of humanity joins a peaceful, contented hive mind sharing memories and consciousness, the "Others"; hundreds of millions die during the Joining. Carol Sturka, a romance novelist in Albuquerque whose partner Helen dies in the event, is one of only thirteen people immune to it. The hive mind, which cannot lie or kill, offers to satisfy her every need while hoping to bring her in. Carol searches for a way to reverse the Joining, discovers that the Others feed on protein made from dead humans, and learns that they have found a way to convert the immune using a virus customised from each person's stem cells, but only with their consent. The first season aired on Apple TV in 2025.
