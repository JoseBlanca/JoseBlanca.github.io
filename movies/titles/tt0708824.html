The genetics

The weapon in this murder mystery is a genetically targeted virus. Dr. Crusher determines that the micro-virus that killed Volnoth was purposely engineered to attack a specific Acamarian DNA profile, that of the Lornak clan, and to be harmless to everyone else; Yuta reveals that she was genetically altered to carry it, transmitting it by touch, and to stop her own ageing so that she could hunt the clan for decades. The alterations also make her resistant to low phaser settings. Who engineered her is never shown; the genetics serves her clan's vendetta, and the episode presents her as a tragic avenger whose revenge has to be stopped for peace to be possible. Crusher's genetic analysis is what exposes the murders. A weapon that kills only people of one lineage echoes real concerns about ethnically targeted bioweapons, although no real pathogen can discriminate between clans of one population this way.

The story

In this Star Trek: The Next Generation episode, the Enterprise traces raids on Federation outposts to the Gatherers, Acamarian nomads who split from their society a century earlier. Captain Picard persuades Sovereign Marouk, the Acamarian leader, to negotiate a reunification, and she brings her assistant Yuta aboard. During the talks Yuta touches an old Gatherer, Volnoth, who dies of an apparent heart attack. Dr. Crusher finds that he was killed by a fast-acting micro-virus engineered to attack one DNA profile. Data finds that another member of the same clan, the Lornak, died the same way 53 years earlier, with Yuta present and unaged. Riker realises that Yuta is the last survivor of the Tralesta clan, wiped out by the Lornaks, and that her next target is the Gatherer leader Chorgan. When she refuses to stop, Riker kills her with his phaser, and the two Acamarian groups agree a truce.
