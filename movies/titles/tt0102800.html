The genetics

Genetic engineering appears in the 'gen jox' programme, which the Market runs to produce a new generation of pilots; German Wikipedia attributes it to a Professor Laplace. Athena, one of these engineered 'tubies', is the film's second lead and the pilot meant to replace Achilles, and her determination to prove herself drives the final match. The film says little about how the gen jox are made and passes no explicit judgement on the programme, which stays in the background as part of the military-technological world; the story is about the robot duels, espionage and the futility of the conflict, and academic critics read it as wary of the attractions of technology. Breeding pilots in vitro with engineered traits is speculative science fiction.

The story

Fifty years after a nuclear war, the two remaining superpowers, the Market and the Confederation, settle territorial disputes through duels between giant robots piloted by 'jox'. The Market's champion Achilles faces the Confederation's undefeated Alexander for control of Alaska, but the fight goes wrong and Achilles' robot falls on the spectators, killing hundreds. Branded a coward, he retires. The Market replaces him with Athena, a genetically engineered 'gen jox' and the first female pilot. Drawn to her and worried for her, Achilles agrees to fight again, angering her. Meanwhile the strategist Tex Conway, the real Confederation spy, murders the robot designer Matsumoto, and later kills himself when a recording exposes him. On the day of the match Athena drugs Achilles and pilots the robot herself, but is defeated; Achilles takes over, wins, and persuades Alexander that the duel need not end in death.
