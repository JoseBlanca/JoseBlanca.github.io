The genetics

Genetic technology sits in the background of the Avatar story, and in this sequel it gives the villain his body. The avatars are engineered Na'vi bodies grown from human DNA, and the RDA now uses them to make recombinants, avatar bodies loaded with the memories of dead soldiers, so that Colonel Quaritch returns to hunt Jake. Heredity also plays a part: Jake's children with Neytiri have human traits, such as five fingers, which betray them to the RDA and make the sea clan's children mock them. Kiri, born from Grace Augustine's avatar body without any known father, is a mystery the film leaves open. The sympathetic human scientists, Norm Spellman and Max Patel, appear only to treat Kiri. Growing a hybrid body and transferring memories into it is science fiction; memories are not stored in DNA and cannot be copied into a new brain.

The story

Sixteen years after the Na'vi drove the humans of the RDA off Pandora, Jake Sully lives as chief of the Omatikaya with Neytiri, their children and their adopted daughter Kiri, mysteriously born from the inert avatar body of the late scientist Grace Augustine. They also look after Spider, the human son of Colonel Quaritch. The RDA returns to colonize Pandora, bringing recombinants, Na'vi bodies carrying the memories of dead soldiers, among them Quaritch himself, who hunts Jake for revenge. To protect the Omatikaya, the family takes refuge with the Metkayina, a sea clan, where the children are mocked for their human traits. Lo'ak befriends an outcast whale-like tulkun, and Kiri, linking to the sea's Spirit Tree, has a seizure. Quaritch joins a whaling ship and kills tulkun to draw Jake out, and the final battle costs the life of Jake's eldest son, Neteyam. The Metkayina adopt the family, and Jake resolves to fight.
