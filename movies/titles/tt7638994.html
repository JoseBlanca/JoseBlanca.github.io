The genetics

Genetics is a tool of social control in this little-known independent dystopia. The ruling corporation has genetically modified the population so that people depend on a daily drug produced by the company, making obedience a matter of survival. The protagonist is a senior geneticist working for the corporation, who turns against it after the loss of his partner, so the story follows a scientist moving from serving a system to fighting it. The film received very little critical attention, and the available synopses do not describe how the modification works or how the story ends; this summary is therefore limited to the premise. Engineering a population so that it depends on a drug is not something current genetic technology could do, but the film draws on fears about corporate control of medicine and biotechnology.

The story

In 2039, a single corporation, owned by a handful of elite families and run by a sociopathic chief executive, effectively rules much of the world through genetic modification, food and drugs. Its genetically modified population needs to take a daily pill, Preytel, or die. Rand, a senior geneticist at the corporation, and Jill, its head of IT security, are secretly lovers. When Jill learns that she has been marked to "disappear", the choice she makes sets Rand on a path against the chief executive and the ruling families. The film was written and directed by Bob Abell and is based on the novel The Corporation.
