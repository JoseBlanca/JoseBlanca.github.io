The genetics

DNA testing is the turning point of this Tamil crime thriller. When a mother's claim that her newborn has been switched is dismissed by doctors and police as postpartum psychosis, her husband commissions a private DNA test, which shows that the baby they are raising is not biologically theirs and forces the police to open an investigation. From there the film becomes a procedural about a baby-trafficking ring that exploits hospital records, and genetics plays no further role, although identical twins also figure in the plot: the swap was carried out by a nurse and her unknown twin sister. The film presents the DNA test as the tool that vindicates a mother whom everyone refused to believe, in line with the real use of DNA testing to settle parentage and baby-switching cases.

The story

In 2014 Varadarajan, who writes software for hospitals, hits a motorcycle carrying a couple and a baby; the ambulance driver Rajendran covers for him, and the two give the rescued infant to a father whose own child was stillborn. In 2023 Anand, a heavy drinker whose father considers him useless, marries Dhivya, a woman with borderline personality disorder, and their son is born at Bethel Hospital. Dhivya insists that the baby she was given is not the one she delivered, but everyone blames her illness. Anand believes her, and a private DNA test proves the baby is not theirs. With Sub-Inspector Chinnasamy, days from retirement, he uncovers a swap carried out by a nurse and her secret identical twin for a nursing agency, part of a baby-trafficking ring run by Varadarajan and Rajendran using hospital data. Varadarajan has Rajendran killed, but Anand recovers his son from an American couple, and Rajendran's son kills Varadarajan.
