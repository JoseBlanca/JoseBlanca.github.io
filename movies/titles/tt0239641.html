The genetics

Genetics appears here only as backstory. The creature belongs to the "Judas breed", the insect created by genetic engineering in the first film to wipe out disease-carrying cockroaches, which survived and evolved into a predator able to mimic human appearance. Mimic 2 itself focuses on a horror chase in a school and does not show the creation of the creatures, a geneticist or any laboratory work; its scientist character, Remi, is an entomologist and the creature's target, not its maker. The premise implicitly warns about the unintended consequences of releasing engineered organisms, but the film does not develop the idea. Its connection to genetics is weak, and it may not merit inclusion in the database.

The story

Four years after the events of Mimic, three men are found mutilated in New York, their faces removed. Detective Klaski discovers that all of them knew Remi Panos, an entomologist teaching at an inner-city high school. Klaski first suspects Remi, until he sees the creature that has been stalking her: an intelligent mutant insect that can take the face of its last victim and wants to mate with her. Remi, Klaski and two students are trapped inside the school as the creature hunts them, while a special forces unit led by a man called Darksuit prepares to gas the building. Remi escapes, but the creature has killed Klaski and taken his form, and it follows her home. Remi decapitates it in her apartment, yet the creature is not dead, leaving her and a student wondering what to do next.
