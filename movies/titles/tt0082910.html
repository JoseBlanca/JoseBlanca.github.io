The genetics

The monsters are a product of military genetic engineering. Tyler Sherman's government team created a strain of piranha that can fly and survive out of water, described as genetically modified and, in the Spanish source, as a hybrid of piranha with mummichog and flying fish; the fish were meant as an "ultimate weapon". After a cylinder of them is lost in the sea, they breed in a shipwreck and kill tourists and locals. Tyler is a remorseful insider who helps the heroine destroy the nest, but the army is said to consider him unstable and he ends up eaten by his own creations, a cliché the Moria review notes. The authorities and the profit-minded hotel manager who ignore the danger share the blame. As in the first Piranha, a covert weapons programme unleashes engineered animals on civilians; hybridizing fish into fliers is pure fantasy.

The story

At the Hotel Elysium, a Caribbean resort, divers and bathers are killed near a sunken shipwreck. Diving instructor Anne Kimbrough, who studied marine biology, realizes the wounds were not made by sharks or barracudas, but her estranged husband Steve, the local police chief, and the hotel manager Raoul dismiss her warnings. Tourist Tyler Sherman, with whom Anne has an affair, reveals that he is a biochemist from a government team that developed piranhas able to fly and live out of water as a weapon; a cylinder of them was lost and they now breed in the wreck. During a night-time fish party the piranhas fly out of the sea and attack the guests, killing the fisherman Gabby among others. Anne and Tyler dive to blow up the wreck with time bombs; Tyler is eaten by the fish, while Anne escapes and is reunited with Steve and their son.
