The genetics

Genetics is a background element of the world-building. The Garms are clone warriors created by their goddess, Danaan, and, according to the Japanese source, they live on by passing their memories on as data; the heroine, Khara, is described as a clone soldier. The film does not show how the clones are made and does not treat cloning as a subject in itself: the story is a quest about why the creators made and then abandoned their warlike creations, with a final hint that the same gods created humankind on Earth. Cloning thus serves as the explanation for a population of interchangeable soldiers rather than as a theme.

The story

In Mamoru Oshii's live-action and animated film, the planet Annwn was populated by eight tribes of Garms, created by a goddess, Danaan. After she left, the tribes fought an endless war, and only three remain: the Briga, who rule the ground with tanks; the Kumtak, enslaved by the Briga; and the Columba, who rule the skies. Khara, a Columba clone soldier, captures Wydd, a Kumtak man travelling with a dog and Nascien, the last of the Druids who once spoke for the gods. When the Briga destroy the Columba airship, Wydd reveals his aim: to reach the Druids' land and learn why Danaan created the Garms and abandoned them. Skelling dies holding off giant robot guardians; Nascien kills Wydd and reveals that she wants to use the guardians to destroy all Garms, because the gods feared their destructive potential. Khara kills her, and the blue planet above them turns out to be Earth. The film ends as a robotic army rises and the tribes unite against it.
