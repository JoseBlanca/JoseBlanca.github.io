The genetics

Genetics is the source of the film's monsters and its central threat. The villain scientist, Dr. Moloch, injects wrestlers with animal DNA to improve their performance, and they gain the power to turn into human-animal hybrids, at the cost of losing control of their bodies. The climax revolves around a "DNA bomb" designed to mutate the population of a whole city. Scientists appear on both sides: Moloch works for profit and for a criminal organisation, while the geneticist Dr. Leung is the one who can undo the damage and cure the hero, so genetics is shown both as a weapon and as a cure. The genetics is pure comic-book fantasy. Real gene therapy modifies genes in some of a patient's cells to treat a disease, and its effects are limited to the cells it reaches; adding animal DNA to an adult cannot make a body take on another species' shape or abilities, let alone switch between forms at will.

The story

Kan Fung, a former super-soldier who escaped from the organisation that gave him his abilities, fights crime in a futuristic city as Black Mask while looking for a geneticist who can cure him. A wrestling promoter's stars, Iguana, Chameleon, Snake, Wolf and others, turn out to have been injected with animal DNA by Dr. Moloch, which lets them transform into human-animal hybrids. After Iguana dies in a fight with Black Mask, the others seek revenge. Black Mask contacts the geneticist Dr. Marco Leung, but is himself injected with animal DNA and becomes a tiger hybrid until Leung finds a chemical that reverses the change. In the climax, his old enemy Lang, who has overpowered Moloch, plants a bomb capable of altering the DNA of everyone in the city. The hybrid wrestlers are killed one by one, the invisible Chameleon sacrificing herself to hand Black Mask the bomb, and he defeats Lang and defuses it. Leung finally rides off with Kan, implying she has cured him.
