The genetics

As in the Brazilian original, human cloning is one of the central storylines. The scientist Augusto Albieri clones a young man in secret out of grief for his dead twin brother, implanting the embryo in a woman, and twenty years later the clone, Daniel, grows up resentful and searching for the man he regards as a father. The geneticist is portrayed as a lonely, obsessive figure whose private grief overrides ethics and consent, and the story deals with the identity of a person who is a genetic copy of someone else but has a different upbringing and life. The telenovela uses cloning mainly to set up a melodramatic love triangle rather than to examine the science. Human reproductive cloning remains unachieved and widely opposed; a clone would, as here, be a genetic twin born decades later, not a copy of the original's mind.

The story

This Spanish-language remake of the Brazilian telenovela O Clone, co-produced by Telemundo, RTI Televisión and Globo, stars Mauricio Ochmann and Sandra Echeverría. Jade, a young woman of Arab descent, goes to live with her family in Morocco after her mother's death and falls in love with Lucas, the son of a wealthy Miami businessman, who is visiting the country. Cultural and religious barriers keep them apart, and Jade is married to Said. After Lucas's twin brother Diego dies in an accident, the scientist Augusto Albieri, devastated by the loss, secretly clones Lucas and implants the embryo in a woman, Dora, who gives birth to Daniel. Twenty years later, Lucas and Jade are drawn together again, and Daniel, the clone, now identical to the young Lucas, enters Jade's life, creating a love triangle between the man she loved and his younger double. Subplots deal with drug addiction and Islam.
