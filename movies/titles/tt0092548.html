The genetics

Genetics plays a small role in this martial-arts action film. The villain's plan is to breed an army of genetically engineered "super-ninjas" from the genetic material of kidnapped soldiers, using research forced out of an abducted scientist. The genetic engineering is never explained and serves only as a pretext for the fight scenes; the scientist is a victim coerced by a drug lord rather than a central character. The idea that soldiers' genes could be harvested to produce obedient super-fighters has no scientific basis. The film is a minor example of the "genetically engineered super-soldier" motif common in 1980s and 1990s action and science fiction.

The story

US Army Rangers Joe Armstrong and Curtis Jackson are sent to a remote Caribbean island to investigate the disappearance of several Marines. They find that the kidnappings are the work of a drug lord known as "The Lion", who has also abducted a local scientist and is using his research to create a private army of "super-ninjas" from the genetic material of the captured Marines, turning them into brainwashed killing machines. Joe and Curtis team up with Alicia Sanborn, the kidnapped scientist's daughter, and infiltrate the Lion's base on Blackbeard Island. While Joe fights the Lion's chief ninja, Curtis leads an assault with the local Marines.
