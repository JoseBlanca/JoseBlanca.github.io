The genetics

Human reproductive cloning is the premise of the episode. A grieving mother carries a clone of her dead son, made possible through her husband's power and connections, and the story explores the hope that cloning can bring back a lost child. It then adds a supernatural element: the clone inherits the dead boy's memories, which reveal the father's guilt. The doctor who performs the procedure is portrayed as sympathetic, although the final twist suggests she has cloned the dead husband in turn. Scientifically, a clone is a genetic copy, a delayed identical twin, and does not inherit the memories or personality of the original; the episode uses that misconception as a deliberate fantasy device. The episode aired in January 1997, a month before the announcement of Dolly the sheep, the first mammal cloned from an adult cell, which brought human cloning into public debate.

The story

Graham, a powerful public figure, uses his influence to offer his wife Rebecca the chance to clone their son Justin, who died in an accident. Rebecca is at first repulsed but agrees to carry the clone. Six months into the pregnancy, she begins to see things through her son's eyes, and she and Dr. Cole conclude that the unborn child is not only a clone but carries Justin's memories. Rebecca notices that the baby reacts strangely to Graham, and through the fetus's memories she relives Justin's death: Graham, impatient, had pulled a toy from the boy's hands and knocked him down, causing the fatal head injury. When she decides to leave, she comes to believe that Graham will kill her to protect his secret, and she shoots him. Three months later she brings her newborn son in for a check-up, and Dr. Cole, now pregnant, calms her own unborn child by calling it Graham.
