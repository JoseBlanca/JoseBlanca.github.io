The story

In this first-season episode of Stargate Atlantis, Teyla Emmagan is plagued by terrifying nightmares. To understand them she looks into her past offworld and finds an abandoned Wraith laboratory. She brings its findings back to Atlantis, where Dr. Carson Beckett, the expedition's doctor, analyses them and makes a surprising discovery about Teyla's DNA. The discovery opens a possible way for the expedition to gather information about its enemy, the Wraith, whose fleet is approaching the city, setting up the season finale.
