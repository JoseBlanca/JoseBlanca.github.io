The genetics

Genetics is a passing element. The immortals' healing is never explained, and the villain, the pharmaceutical executive Steven Merrick, wants to uncover its secret; according to the Italian source, he persuades Booker to betray the team by promising that their DNA could be exploited to find a cure for all ills. In practice, the research shown is the torture and tissue sampling of captives in a corporate laboratory, assisted by a scientist, and the film presents it as driven by greed and sadism, while the former CIA agent who first hoped to cure the world's diseases changes sides. The film is about immortality, loyalty and the exploitation of people by a pharmaceutical company rather than about genetic science.

The story

In this action film based on the comic by Greg Rucka, Andy leads a small team of immortal mercenaries, Booker, Joe and Nicky, who heal from any injury and have lived for centuries. A former CIA agent, James Copley, hires them to rescue kidnapped girls in South Sudan, but it is a trap: he films their deaths and regeneration. Meanwhile, the US Marine Nile Freeman survives a fatal wound in Afghanistan, and Andy brings her into the group. Copley has shown his footage to Steven Merrick, a young pharmaceutical executive, whose men capture Joe and Nicky and take them to his London laboratory, where they are tortured and sampled to uncover the secret of their healing. Booker, weary of immortality, betrays Andy, who discovers that she has become mortal. Copley, realising that Merrick is driven by greed and sadism, helps Nile rescue the team; Nile kills Merrick. The group exiles Booker for a hundred years, and Copley becomes their ally, showing them the good their missions have done.
