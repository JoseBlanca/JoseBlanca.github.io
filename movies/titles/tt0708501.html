The genetics

The threat is an engineered virus left over from a past war. During the Cardassian occupation of Bajor, a resistance scientist designed a synthetic virus that attacks the brain's language centres and causes fatal fevers, and hid a device in the station's replicators to write it into food; triggered years later by accident, it infects nearly everyone aboard and mutates to spread through the air. Dr. Bashir identifies the agent, and by analysing its DNA and construction determines that it is synthetic and of Bajoran rather than Cardassian origin, which leads to the creator's former assistant. The resolution depends on the expertise of the people who made the weapon: Surmak Ren, reluctant and abducted, uses Bashir's research to make the antidote. The episode presents the weapon as a dangerous legacy of terrorism rather than dwelling on the ethics of its makers, and the doctors as competent heroes. A virus that selectively scrambles language is fictional, although some infections can cause aphasia.

The story

In this Star Trek: Deep Space Nine episode, an overworked Chief O'Brien, repairing a faulty food replicator, unknowingly activates a hidden device. Soon he speaks only gibberish and cannot understand language, and Dax shows the same aphasia. Dr. Bashir finds a virus in food from the command-level replicators, which causes dangerous fevers; Quark has been selling food from an unused replicator, spreading it, and the virus mutates to become airborne. With the station quarantined and most of the crew stricken, Major Kira finds that the virus was engineered by a Bajoran resistance fighter, Dekon Elig, during the Cardassian occupation, to be released through the station's replicators. Elig is dead, so Kira abducts his former assistant, Surmak Ren, now a medical administrator on Bajor, who refuses to help until he learns that he too is infected. Meanwhile, Odo and Quark eject a freighter whose damaged core threatens the station. Using Bashir's notes, Ren creates an antidote that cures the crew.
