The genetics

The monsters of this disaster thriller are genetically engineered dogs. The programme began with a benevolent aim, breeding dogs that react to human voices to find disaster victims, but the military, with foreign backing, redirected it to produce attack dogs for counter-terrorism. All the dogs descend genetically from one female, E9, whose aggression the film attributes to the trauma of seeing her puppies vivisected, so cruelty rather than genes alone makes her a killer. Dr. Yang, the head researcher, is an ambivalent figure: part of the project, she ends by exposing its crimes to the media. The film critiques the use of science and military power that treats living beings as tools. It gives no detail of the genetic methods, and its super-dogs are fantastical.

The story

In thick fog, a massive pile-up traps drivers on Incheon's Airport Bridge, among them presidential aide Cha Jung-won and his daughter Kyung-min, tow-truck driver Joe Park, golfer Yoo-ra and her sister Mi-ran, an elderly couple and Dr. Yang, head researcher of a secret military programme, 'Project Silence'. The crash frees the project's experimental dogs, which were being transported in secret, and they attack the survivors while the damaged bridge threatens to collapse. Dr. Yang reveals that the dogs were first designed as rescue dogs that respond to human voices, but the South Korean military, backed by the US and European governments, turned them into attack dogs to hunt terrorists; they proved uncontrollable. Their founding animal, E9, became vicious after watching litters of her puppies vivisected, and she is hunting humans in revenge. The survivors escape before the bridge falls and expose the project to the press, while E9 and one puppy swim ashore.
