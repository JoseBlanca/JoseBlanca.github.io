The genetics

Genetic engineering creates the monsters: in a secret Russian military project of the 1980s, spiders were crossbred with alien DNA recovered from a frozen spacecraft and kept on a space station, producing creatures that grow to enormous size. The research is driven by military interest in the queen's webbing, and an American colonel collaborates with the Russians to secure the queen egg, ready to kill civilians and witnesses. The scientist Dr. Darnoff, who explains the spiders' origin, helps the heroes escape before the queen kills him. The film is a low-budget giant-spider movie, and critics noted that it makes little effort to explain the experiments; the genetics is a pretext for the monsters, with no scientific plausibility.

The story

Debris from a destroyed Soviet-era space station, where experiments were conducted on genetically altered spiders, crashes into a New York subway tunnel. A transit worker sent to investigate is bitten and dies, and strange eggs are found in his body; his supervisor, Jason Cole, passes them to his estranged wife Rachel, a health official. When the tunnels reopen, bodies and webs are found. The US Army under Colonel Jenkins quarantines the area with a cover story of a viral outbreak. The scientist Dr. Darnoff reveals that the spiders were bred from a spider found in a frozen spacecraft and crossed with alien DNA, and that the dead worker carried the queen's egg; Jenkins, secretly working with the Russians, wants the queen for her militarily valuable webbing at any cost. Giant spiders overrun the city. Jason and Rachel rescue their daughter Emily, and Jason destroys the queen and her nest by crashing a burning subway train, but one spider survives.
