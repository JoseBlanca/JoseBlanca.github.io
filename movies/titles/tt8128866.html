The genetics

The film combines de-extinction with biomedical engineering. Triassic Corporation has recreated Gojirasaurus, an extinct dinosaur, and cultivates organ parts from its genetic code to grow replacement organs for people, while its investor wants the research turned towards artificial immortality. The CEO, Dr. Marisa Martinelli, has also implanted human proteins into the animals to give them a human-like immune system, and this hybridisation has made them carriers of a virus that passes to humans through bites or blood, causing fever, delirium and death. The film presents her as a villain who tries to silence an employee to hide her actions, and the corporation's ambitions end in a massacre. A reviewer called the idea of harvesting organs for humans from a cold-blooded reptile daft; real research on growing human organs in animals uses pigs, whose physiology is close to ours.

The story

In this low-budget film from The Asylum, a mockbuster of Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom, the Triassic Corporation in Los Angeles grows organ parts from the genetic code of the extinct predator Gojirasaurus, with the aim of producing replacement human organs. During a private tour for the investor Steven Hagen, who will only fund research aimed at artificial immortality, a Gojirasaurus escapes, kills security guards and bites Hagen. The escape triggers a countdown to release poison gas that will kill everything in the building. The survivors, including CEO Dr. Marisa Martinelli and her employee Diana, hide and try to reach the exit while the dinosaurs hunt them. Bitten people develop fever and delirium, and Diana discovers that Marisa implanted human proteins in the dinosaurs to give them a human-like immune system, which made them carriers of a virus transmissible to humans. Marisa tries to kill Diana but dies in her own trap; Hagen returns as a zombie-like figure. Only Diana survives the gas.
