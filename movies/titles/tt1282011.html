The genetics

As in the rest of the franchise, Stitch and his 'cousins' are genetic experiments: artificial alien creatures with special powers made by the scientist Jumba. Here they are the series' backdrop rather than its subject. Jumba, originally an 'evil genius' who designed Stitch for destruction, is now a friendly, comic mentor who reprograms experiments to find a good purpose, while the villains Hämsterviel and Delia alter experiments to make them stronger and evil and create a monster, Dark End, to defeat Stitch. Created beings are therefore not inherently bad: what matters is how they are raised and used, and Stitch's growth comes through friendship and good deeds, not science. The genetics is entirely cartoon science fiction, and the sources describe the creatures' powers rather than how they were made.

The story

This Japanese anime spin-off of Disney's Lilo & Stitch is set years after Leroy & Stitch. Stitch, the destructive genetic experiment 626, flees his creator Dr. Jumba Jookiba and crash-lands on Izayoi, an island near Okinawa, where he befriends Yuna, a ten-year-old karate student. A magical spirit stone will grant his wish to become the strongest being in the universe if he performs enough good deeds, and Jumba, now living with them, builds a good-deed counter. Stitch's old enemy Dr. Hämsterviel schemes to steal the stone's power, using mind-controlled experiments. Other experiments created by Jumba appear, including Angel, Sparky, Reuben and the violent prototype Experiment Zero. In the end Stitch uses his wish to stay with Yuna. In the third arc, set in Okinawa New Town, Hämsterviel's partner Delia creates a powerful experiment, Dark End, which nearly kills Stitch before his friends revive him.
