The genetics

The film, the fourth in the Species series, rests on a genetic experiment: Miranda was created by two college friends who combined human and alien DNA, and her human side has been maintained only by regular hormone injections. One creator, Tom, became her protective guardian; the other, Forbes McGuire, carried on alone in Mexico, creating further hybrids and selling half-alien copies of dead pets and relatives, and the film presents him as a vulgar, unethical scientist. When Miranda begins to fail, the cure is an infusion of DNA or stem cells taken from an abducted human donor, a procedure the film equates with human sacrifice, and its botched execution unleashes her alien instincts. The hybrids end up dead and the lab destroyed. The science, from hormones that suppress alien genes to adult DNA infusions that rescue a dying hybrid, is pure B-movie fantasy.

The story

Miranda Hollander, a young university lecturer in classics and biology who can read books by touching them, lives with her 'uncle' Tom and believes her parents died when she was a baby. After collapsing, she is taken to hospital, where she turns into an alien creature and kills several staff. Tom sedates her with human hormones and drives her to Mexico, revealing on the way that she is the product of an experiment combining human and alien DNA that he carried out with his college friend Forbes McGuire, and that he has suppressed her alien nature with hormones since childhood. In Mexico, Forbes, who lives with another hybrid, Azura, and earns money making half-alien copies of clients' dead pets and relatives, finds that Miranda is dying. Only fresh human DNA can save her, so they abduct a mugger as a donor. The botched treatment lets Miranda's alien side take over: she seeks mates, kills Forbes and becomes pregnant. Azura fatally wounds her, Tom kills Azura and blows up the lab.
