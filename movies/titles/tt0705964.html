The genetics

The genetics is the subplot: Dr. Mephesto, the show's recurring mad scientist, has genetically engineered turkeys, which he says were meant to feed the poor, and they have run wild, killing townspeople. The mayor dismisses him as an insane genetic engineer, and when he shows Chef the turkey DNA "growing" under the microscope the scene ends in a crude joke. Mephesto is a comic figure, both the cause of the disaster and the ignored voice warning about it; commentators cited by Wikipedia read the turkeys as a nod to the unpredictability and ethical ambiguity of genetic engineering. The subplot parodies monster films and Braveheart, and its ending, dead turkeys shipped to Ethiopia, ties it back to the episode's satire of charity and food waste. None of it is meant as plausible science.

The story

Lured by the promise of a free sports watch in a Sally Struthers charity advert, Stan, Kyle, Cartman and Kenny sponsor a starving African child and receive the child himself, an Ethiopian boy whom Cartman names Starvin' Marvin. Marvin is shocked by how much food the town wastes. When the FBI comes to send him home, he tricks the agents into taking Cartman instead, and in Ethiopia Cartman discovers that Struthers is hoarding the charity food for herself; he exposes her and the locals take over the supplies. Meanwhile, wild turkeys are attacking and killing South Park's residents. Dr. Mephesto warns the mayor, who ridicules him, that the genetically engineered turkeys he bred have gone crazy. Chef leads the townspeople in a Braveheart-style battle that kills all the turkeys, whose bodies Marvin takes back to Ethiopia to feed his people.
