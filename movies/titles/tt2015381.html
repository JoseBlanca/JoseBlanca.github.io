The genetics

Genetics is a passing element. Rocket, one of the heroes, is described as a genetically and cybernetically modified raccoon, created by unnamed others to be a fighting machine; the director conceived a tragic origin for him, but the film never shows who made him or how. Gamora's enhanced body and Quill's discovery that he is half human, with a father from an ancient alien species, add touches of engineering and heredity without becoming part of the plot. The story is a space adventure about a band of misfits, and genetic modification simply explains why one of them is a talking raccoon. The idea that genetic engineering could give a raccoon human intelligence and speech is fantasy.

The story

In 1988, after his mother dies of cancer, young Peter Quill is abducted from Earth by the Ravagers, space outlaws led by Yondu Udonta. Twenty-six years later, Quill, calling himself Star-Lord, steals a mysterious orb that is also wanted by Ronan the Accuser, a fanatical Kree warlord allied with Thanos. On the planet Xandar, his attempt to sell it draws in Gamora, an assassin sent by Ronan who plans to betray him, and the bounty hunters Rocket, a genetically and cybernetically modified raccoon, and Groot, a tree-like humanoid. Arrested and imprisoned together, they recruit Drax, who wants revenge on Ronan for killing his family, and escape. The orb turns out to contain the Power Stone, an Infinity Stone of enormous destructive power. Ronan seizes it and attacks Xandar, but the five misfits, with the Ravagers and the Nova Corps, defeat him; Groot sacrifices himself and regrows from a sapling. Quill learns that his father belongs to an ancient, unknown species.
