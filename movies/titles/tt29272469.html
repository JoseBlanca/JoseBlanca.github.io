The genetics

This whodunnit hinges on illegal human experimentation by a pharmaceutical company. V-Pharma, co-founded by the murdered Isaac John, secretly tested a first-stage vaccine against a 'Human Mutagen Virus' (Red Virus) on the employees of its sister company, putting it into their food and water and sampling their DNA regularly through the office punch-card machine; the employees' blood contains mutagens capable of altering DNA, and they suffer a stillbirth, chronic symptoms and distress. The source says the aim was to create a supply and demand situation for the future vaccine. The scientist who uncovers the scheme, Dr. Kuriakose, is a sympathetic physician, while the company's leaders, Isaac and his biochemist partner Gibson, are presented as exploiters of their workers. The film takes the victims' side, as the investigating officer covers up their revenge killing.

The story

Isaac John, managing director of the company V-Tech, is found dead in a locked office restroom, apparently after slipping. ASP Sandeep suspects murder and questions the employees; Isaac's partner Gibson seems to have no motive, since Isaac's brother inherits the business. Sandeep finds that most employees were patients of Dr. Kuriakose, a neuropsychiatrist. Kuriakose had found DNA-altering mutagens in the blood of an employee, Meera, who had suffered an unexplained stillbirth, and in other staff with headaches and insomnia. Using files taken from Isaac's computer, he concludes that V-Pharma, a company Isaac co-founded, was testing an unfinished vaccine against a 'Human Mutagen Virus' on the employees through the office snacks and water, monitoring them with a DNA-sampling punch-card machine, and leaving them with permanent health problems. Sandeep works out that the employees jointly killed Isaac with carbon dioxide, but reports the death as accidental and turns to investigating V-Pharma's lab, while Gibson, also a biochemist involved in the scheme, takes over the company.
