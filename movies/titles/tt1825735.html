The genetics

Genetics provides the backstory: humans engineered the Ghen, a race with enhanced DNA, to serve as soldiers, mistreated them and were overthrown in a long war, so that the 'genetic supermen' now rule from below while plague-stricken humans survive on the surface. This is the familiar story of engineered servants turning on their makers, here with the roles of oppressor and oppressed reversed in the present, as the Ghen leadership becomes fascistic in its turn. No genetic work happens during the film; the story is about politics, prophecy and alien possession, and the Moria review judges the idea of a dystopia of genetic supermen unoriginal. No scientific detail is given about the enhancement.

The story

Mankind has lost a 300-year war against the Ghen, a race of genetically enhanced people it created as soldiers and then abused and tortured. The Ghen now rule Earth from utopian underground cities, while the surviving humans on the surface are stricken by plague. The Ghen Baron Crecilius Pryme wants to be voted ruler and stokes fear of the humans above; he creates an elite force, the Ministers, which carries out brutal reprisals on the surface, and recruits his brother Aiden away from scientific research to join it, where Aiden discovers he enjoys the cruelty. On the surface, a woman named Mukayluh channels alien beings, the Kalendoah, who prophesy a figure who will bring peace between humans and Ghen. Crecilius tries to stop her, unaware that the prophesied one is close to home. The film, the second part of the 'Creation Wars' saga, ends with Crecilius escaping into the sunlight of the surface.
