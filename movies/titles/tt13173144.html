The story

Set in 2006, between the games Resident Evil 4 and 5, this animated miniseries alternates between the present and flashbacks to a war in the fictional South Asian republic of Penamstan in 2000. Federal agent Leon S. Kennedy is called to the White House to investigate a hacking of secret presidential files. Claire Redfield, working for the NGO TerraSave on a refugee welfare project in Penamstan, is disturbed by a refugee child's drawing of what looks like a victim of a viral infection, and brings it to the White House, where she meets Leon; he dismisses any link. Soon the lights go out and zombies attack the White House. The two outbreaks, the war in Penamstan and the hack prove to be connected through a conspiracy over the creation and use of a virus, involving new characters such as Jason, a veteran of Penamstan, and Shen May. Leon and Claire uncover the scale of the threat and the immediate crises are resolved.
