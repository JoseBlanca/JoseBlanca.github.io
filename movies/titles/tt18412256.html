The genetics

Genetic engineering is the hidden purpose of the Weyland-Yutani station. Its scientists reverse-bioengineered the Xenomorph recovered from the Nostromo and harvested from the Facehuggers a fluid, Z-01, described as able to rapidly rewrite and adapt DNA, with which the company hopes to create 'genetically perfect humans' fit to thrive in space. The experiments get the station's crew massacred, and the company's android enforces the corporate priority of securing the samples over human lives. When Kay injects Z-01 to heal her wounds, the substance works, but her baby is born a monstrous human–Xenomorph hybrid that kills her. The film thus continues the franchise's picture of a corporation that treats people as expendable in pursuit of biological weapons and enhancement. The science is fictional, though the idea of engineering humans for space echoes real speculation about adapting people to other worlds.

The story

In 2142, a Weyland-Yutani probe recovers the cocooned Xenomorph from the wreck of the Nostromo and takes it to a secret research station. Months later, on the mining colony Jackson's Star, orphaned miner Rain Carradine finds her contract extended indefinitely. With her android 'brother' Andy and a group of young colonists, Tyler, his pregnant sister Kay, Bjorn and Navarro, she boards a derelict station in orbit to steal cryostasis pods for a nine-year voyage to a free planet. The station, the Renaissance, turns out to be the lab where scientists experimented on the creature, and the group accidentally releases frozen Facehuggers. A damaged science android, Rook, reveals that the creature killed the crew and orders Andy to secure Z-01, a substance harvested from the creatures with which the company hopes to create 'genetically perfect humans'. Most of the group die. Rain escapes with Andy, but Kay, who injected herself with Z-01 to heal, gives birth to a human–Xenomorph hybrid that kills her before Rain ejects it into space.
