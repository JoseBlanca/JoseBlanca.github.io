The genetics

Genetics and evolution frame the series' mythology. Sensates are presented as humans set apart by a small genetic mutation, and from the second season as a related species, Homo sensorium, which in the show's lore lived alongside Homo sapiens; a professor consulted by Nomi and Amanita links it to the brain's capacity for empathy. BPO, originally founded to study sensates and to keep a balance between the two species, has become an organisation that hunts them for use as instruments of control. The series uses this framework less as science than as a metaphor for empathy, connection and the persecution of people perceived as different. The idea of a separate, interbreeding human species echoes real human evolution, in which Homo sapiens coexisted and interbred with Neanderthals and other groups, but a single mutation could not produce a shared consciousness.

The story

Eight strangers in different cities, Capheus in Nairobi, Sun in Seoul, Nomi in San Francisco, Kala in Mumbai, Riley in London, Wolfgang in Berlin, Lito in Mexico City and Will in Chicago, discover after the death of a woman named Angelica that they are "sensates", members of a cluster of humans mentally and emotionally linked to each other. They can sense one another, communicate across distances and share knowledge, languages and skills. Helped by Jonas, a sensate from Angelica's cluster, they try to continue their lives while being hunted by the Biologic Preservation Organization (BPO) and its agent Whispers, himself a sensate who has turned against his kind. Over two seasons and two specials (2015–2018), the cluster learns to use its abilities, meets other sensates and uncovers BPO's history and aims. In the finale, they capture Whispers to rescue Wolfgang, and BPO's chairman launches a global attack on sensates and their allies. The series was created by Lana and Lilly Wachowski and J. Michael Straczynski for Netflix.
