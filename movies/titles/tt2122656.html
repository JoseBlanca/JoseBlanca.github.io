The genetics

Genetics runs through this anime's science-fiction mythology. The Apocalypse Virus, which arrived with a meteorite, is turned into a tool by Da'ath, an ancient cult using GHQ as its front, to force mankind's evolution, and by Keido, who spreads it with a 'genetic resonance' broadcast. The pharmaceutical company Sephirah Genomics developed a vaccine and the Void Genome, a genetic weapon derived from the virus that grants the hero his power, and Inori's body was artificially created as a vessel to bear a new race with the soul of Shu's sister. Scientists appear on both sides: Keido is the villain, while Shu's stepmother Haruka, also from Sephirah Genomics, betrays GHQ to help him. The series treats forced evolution and genetic power as the source of oppression and catastrophe; its science is mystical rather than realistic.

The story

In 2039, Japan is ruled by GHQ, an international body sent ten years earlier to contain the 'Lost Christmas' outbreak of the Apocalypse Virus, which arrived with a meteorite and crystallises its victims. High-school student Shu Ouma meets Inori Yuzuriha, singer of the group Egoist and member of the resistance Funeral Parlor, led by Gai Tsutsugami. When a vial of the Void Genome shatters on him, Shu gains the 'Power of the King', the ability to draw weapons called Voids out of people's hearts. GHQ's Anti-Bodies leader Shūichirō Keido unleashes the virus on Tokyo with a 'genetic resonance' broadcast. Shu learns that his sister Mana was the first infected and caused Lost Christmas, and that Inori was created as a vessel for Mana's soul, to bear a new human race. GHQ proves a front for Da'ath, an ancient cult seeking to force human evolution through the virus. Shu defeats Gai and Yu, Keido kills himself, and Inori sacrifices herself to destroy the virus.
