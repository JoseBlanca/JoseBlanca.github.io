The genetics

The genetic element is the MacGuffin of the plot: a genetically engineered virus that stops human ageing. Rather than a weapon, it is a coveted anti-ageing product, and the danger lies in the demand it creates and in the plan of an illegal marketer and a beauty-products magnate to auction its rights to the highest international bidders, which the film presents as a recipe for worldwide chaos. Who created the virus is not described in the available source, and the heroes are law-enforcement agents whose mission is to destroy it rather than to use it. The story

This television action film is set in 2020, in a world at peace that is threatened by a genetically engineered virus able to stop human ageing. As people clamour for it, the risk of worldwide anarchy grows. Special Agent Lucy Chang, code name Phoenix, goes undercover to track down the illegal marketer of the virus, who has conspired with a beauty-products magnate to auction its rights to international bidders. A traitor in her own organisation denounces her as a fugitive, and she is pursued by US Marshal Jake Hawkins. Chang convinces Hawkins that the release of the virus would destabilise the planet, and the two join forces, combining her martial arts with his high-tech weapons, to expose the plot and destroy the supply of the virus.
