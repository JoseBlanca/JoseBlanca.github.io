The genetics

Human cloning is the premise of the series. Two clones who share the same genome are given opposite personalities, one kind and the other a killer, and the drama uses them to ask how far identity is determined by genes, a nature-versus-nurture theme often found in cloning stories. The kidnapping investigation gradually leads to the origin of the clones and to the people who created them, a mystery that drives the series. Reviewers singled out Yang Se-jong's double performance as the two clones. Because the available sources in English and Korean describe mainly the premise and characters, this summary does not cover the details of the cloning project or the ending. Real clones would be genetically identical but distinct individuals, like identical twins, and their personalities would depend on their different lives.

The story

This Korean crime thriller, broadcast on OCN in sixteen episodes, follows Jang Deuk-cheon, a hardened police detective whose eleven-year-old daughter, Soo-yeon, is kidnapped. His only clue is that two men with the same face were seen at the crime scene. Both turn out to be human clones played by the same actor: Lee Sung-joon, a gentle young man with amnesia who is accused of murder, and Lee Sung-hoon, a ruthless serial killer responsible for the kidnapping. Prosecutor Choi Jo-hye leads the search for the girl and draws Deuk-cheon into manipulating the case. Framed, Deuk-cheon and Sung-joon go on the run together and gradually bond while uncovering the mystery of the clones, their purpose and their connection to the detective.
