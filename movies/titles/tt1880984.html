The genetics

Cloning drives the plot of this crude satire. A professor and his assistant obtain Jerzy's DNA through hired skinheads and grow a clone, because their analysis says Jerzy has the makings of a pop star but cannot be controlled; the clone becomes a manufactured celebrity while the original loses his girlfriend and his name. The clone, a copy with none of Jerzy's loyalty, assaults Yola and takes his place, and the comedy turns on mistaken identity until Jerzy publicly exposes the impostor. The scientists are villains in the mad-scientist mould: they order the original's murder, the assistant ends up arrested, and the professor coolly abandons his creation. The film uses cloning as a vehicle for mocking celebrity culture and the media rather than as a subject in itself; the instant production of an adult clone has no basis in real cloning, which produces a newborn twin. The story

In this Polish adult animated black comedy, based on a comic, a professor's laboratory experiment produces a computer image of a hedgehog, and he sends his assistant to find a real one. The assistant hires two skinheads, Stefan and Zenek, to obtain the DNA of the anthropomorphic hedgehog Jerzy and then kill him. They get the DNA, but Jerzy survives. The lab clones him, having concluded that Jerzy has the full potential of a pop star but cannot be controlled. The clone assaults Jerzy's lover, Yola, who blames and leaves Jerzy, and the skinheads lure Jerzy under a Warsaw bridge, knock him out and throw him in the river; he is rescued by a prostitute, Lilka. While the clone becomes an international celebrity, Jerzy breaks into one of his concerts, exposes him and fights him until a falling beam crushes the clone. The assistant is arrested, Jerzy and Yola reconcile, and the surviving clone ends up cooked by two Vietnamese chefs.
