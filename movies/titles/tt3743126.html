Sharktopus vs. Pteracuda

2014 Film

The genetics

Like the first film, in which a geneticist creates a shark with octopus tentacles for the US Navy, this sequel is built on the creation of hybrid animals for military use. The Pteracuda, combining a barracuda with a pterodactyl, is the work of the same company, and the film adds the fact that the original Sharktopus reproduced, leaving an egg from which a new creature hatches. The scientist figures are a corporate chief who sees the creatures as weapons and dies by them, and a rogue scientist who hacks one to cause destruction. Genetics is a premise only: the film does not show how the creatures were made, and it is a deliberately absurd monster comedy rather than a serious depiction of science. Hybrids between such distant animals, let alone an extinct flying reptile, are impossible, but the film makes no claim to plausibility.

The story

Marine biologist Lorena Christmas finds an egg sac left by the original Sharktopus and raises the creature at a marine park owned by her greedy uncle. Meanwhile Dr. Rico Symes, head of Symodyne, the company that created Sharktopus, tests the Pteracuda, a barracuda-pterodactyl hybrid designed for military use and controlled by a chip in its brain. A Symodyne scientist, Vladimir Futon, hacks the chip and sends the creature to attack populated areas. Symes and his security chief, Hamilton, take the now grown Sharktopus from the park to fight the Pteracuda, and kidnap Lorena to help them. The two creatures battle and kill swimmers, divers, a plane's passengers and a television host, before Symes is torn apart by them. Lorena and Hamilton lure the creatures together with a disco ball and destroy the Pteracuda's chip, apparently killing both, but Sharktopus reappears in the final shot.
