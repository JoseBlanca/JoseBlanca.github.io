The genetics

The film's monsters are the product of a secret corporate genetics programme. EXOr, a terraforming company, genetically modified prisoners to make them more robust for hard labour on colonised planets, and possibly to exterminate native life forms; after a prison revolt, the modified inmates break out as monsters. The company's response is even worse: General Lynex orders the destruction of the planet's population and blames a virus, and later the escaped prisoners. The genetic transformation is also contagious, as Sy is changed into a monster after being struck by one. The film presents EXOr's experiments and cover-up as the core of its critique of corporate greed and unethical experimentation; the modified creatures are victims as much as threats. Turning adult humans into new creatures by genetic modification is far beyond real science.

The story

Kane Sommerville, a disgraced former pilot, works for EXOr, the leading terraforming corporation, on a space station above a colonised planet, while his eleven-year-old daughter Indi stays in the planet's capital, Osiris. When monsters break out of a high-security prison, Kane learns that EXOr's project leader, General Lynex, plans to cover up the disaster by destroying all life on the planet, civilians included, under the pretext of fighting a virus. He deserts, steals a glider to rescue Indi and is shot down. On the surface he teams up with Sy Lombrok, an escaped prisoner, and later with two eccentric former prison drivers, Gyp and Bill. The monsters are prisoners genetically modified in a secret EXOr project. Kane reaches Indi in the besieged city, but he, Gyp and Bill are killed; Indi and Sy, infected by a monster, reach a bunker seconds before an atomic blast. A year later the transformed Sy and Indi seize a transport to the space station.
