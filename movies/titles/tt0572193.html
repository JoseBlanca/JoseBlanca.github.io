The genetics

The episode deals directly with human genetic enhancement and its legacy. Soong raised the Augments as his "children", and they themselves show the dangers his critics feared: they are stronger and smarter than ordinary humans but also arrogant and increasingly violent, and Malik's ruthlessness strains his relationship with Soong. Smike, born with the Augments' genome but without their abilities, shows that genetic design does not guarantee the intended result, and his rejection and murder by his siblings shows how they value people by their traits. Starfleet, for its part, keeps the embryos from the Eugenics Wars in storage instead of destroying them. The arc revives the genetically engineered Augments who appeared alongside Khan Noonian Singh in the original series and is a cautionary tale about eugenic ambitions. Its enhancements are fictional; nothing comparable can be engineered into human embryos.

The story

In the middle episode of a three-part story, Captain Archer and his crew pursue Dr. Arik Soong and the Augments, genetically enhanced humans Soong raised from embryos stolen after the Eugenics Wars. On the planet Trialis IV they find the building where the young Augments were raised and capture Udar, nicknamed "Smike", an Augment cast out by his siblings because he lacks their enhanced abilities except for his hearing. Meanwhile Soong and the Augments seize a Denobulan medical ship and use it to enter Cold Station 12, a secret Starfleet medical facility that stores pathogens and 1,800 frozen augmented embryos. They take the staff hostage, including Dr. Lucas, a friend of Enterprise's Dr. Phlox. Tension grows between Soong and the ruthless Augment Malik, who tortures Lucas and threatens other scientists with deadly pathogens. Malik finally forces Lucas to give up the security codes. The Augments escape with the embryos, but not before Malik kills Smike, steals pathogens and sabotages the station's containment systems.
