The genetics

This short CGI film for 4D attraction theatres is set in the Resident Evil universe, where the pharmaceutical giant Umbrella has secretly developed the T-virus as a biological weapon and turned a city into zombies. Its own story concerns a newer creation: Dr. Cameron's virus, which 'fuses with genes' and lets an infected body regenerate in other living hosts, making her almost immortal. Having infected herself, she becomes a shape-shifting monster that moves from body to body and uses Umbrella's soldiers as test subjects. Both practitioners are villains: the corporation sends its men to steal her research under the pretence of a rescue, and the scientist, driven by pride in her work, kills them and survives to go on experimenting. The film offers the franchise's usual picture of virology and genetics as weapons in corporate hands, with no attempt at plausibility.

The story

In Raccoon City, whose citizens have become zombies after infection with Umbrella's T-virus, a squad of the company's Biohazard Countermeasure Service led by Claus is sent to rescue Dr. Cameron, a scientist researching a new virus. Tracking her ID card, the team is attacked by a monster that kills one of them and, though blown apart, survives by moving into other animals. In the sewers squad member Roger, a senior Umbrella man, reveals that the real mission is to recover Cameron's research data, and that she infected herself with her own virus and became the monster. Two more soldiers die, one turning into a similar creature. As Claus and Roger escape by Humvee, Roger impales Claus with tentacles and reveals Cameron's face: she now lives in his body, sees the squad as guinea pigs and will continue her experiments.
