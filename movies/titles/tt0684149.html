The genetics

The genetic element is Camille herself, a pleasure GELF (genetically engineered life form), a type of being created in the Red Dwarf universe by genetic engineering. She was designed to appear to each person as the object of their desire, which drives the comedy and the episode's reflection on appearance and love: Kryten chooses to accept her true form, a green blob. Her makers never appear and nothing is said about who engineered her or why; the engineering is simply part of the setting, and it is treated with light humour rather than as a warning. A creature that projects a different appearance to each observer is pure fantasy and has no basis in real genetics.

The story

Lister tries to teach the mechanoid Kryten how to lie, but Kryten cannot do it in front of others. Sent to pilot Starbug, Kryten answers a distress call and rescues the only survivor of a doomed ship, Camille. Each crew member sees her differently: to Kryten she is a mechanoid, to Rimmer a hologram, to Lister a woman who shares his interests, and to the Cat a copy of himself. Lister notices that Camille gives different answers to each of them and reveals that she is a pleasure GELF, whose true form is a green blob with tentacles. Hurt but undeterred, Kryten compliments her real appearance and dates her, and they watch Casablanca together. When Camille's husband arrives to take her back, Kryten, in a parody of the film's ending, tells her to leave with him, lying to spare her feelings and proving he has learned his lesson.
