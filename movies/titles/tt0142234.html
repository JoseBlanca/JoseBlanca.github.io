The genetics

Cloning drives the plot. Jaguar and his scientist, Dr. Collie, grow artificial bio-warriors in tanks and create a clone of the dead Broly from a blood sample, intending to use these fighters to humiliate Mr. Satan. Their motives are petty and personal rather than scientific, and the experiment escapes their control: the culture fluid used in the laboratory proves more dangerous than the clone itself, kills the bio-warriors and most of the scientists and threatens to cover the whole Earth. The film treats cloning as a way of bringing back a dead fighter with his powers intact, a common idea in fiction that does not match real cloning, which produces a genetically identical individual who must still develop from an embryo and would not share the original's memories or abilities. The genetics is a premise for a martial arts adventure and is not explored further.

The story

Mr. Jaguar, a rich man who was Mr. Satan's rival at summer camp, invites the martial arts champion to his island laboratory to fight his "bio-warriors", humanoids created by the scientist Dr. Collie, threatening otherwise to expose an embarrassing secret. Android 18 goes along to make sure Mr. Satan pays her a debt, and the boys Goten and Trunks stow away. During the tournament the boys discover a tank holding what looks like Broly, the Legendary Super Saiyan they had killed. A shaman explains that he took a sample of Broly's blood to Jaguar, who had a clone created. The clone breaks out, and the fight ruptures tanks of a bio-fluid that devours matter. Drenched in it, the clone becomes the deformed Bio-Broly and later merges with the fluid. The heroes discover that seawater turns the fluid to stone, flood the island and destroy the petrified giant.
