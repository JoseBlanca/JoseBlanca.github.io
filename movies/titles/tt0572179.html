The genetics

The episode opens a three-part arc about human genetic enhancement. The Augments were raised by Soong from embryos preserved from the time of the Eugenics Wars, the conflict that in Star Trek's history produced Khan Noonien Singh, and their strength and intelligence are matched by arrogance and violence. Soong is a brilliant but irresponsible geneticist who sees himself as their father, steals the embryos and refuses to accept that his project has gone wrong; later in the arc he tries to remove aggressive behaviour from the remaining embryos, and he ends it doubting that engineering humans is feasible. He is the ancestor of Noonien Soong, creator of the android Data. The arc reflects Star Trek's long-standing suspicion of human genetic enhancement, while letting Soong argue for its potential, and its "Augments" echo real concerns that enhancing traits could create a privileged class.

The story

In 2154, genetically enhanced humans known as Augments seize a Klingon Bird-of-Prey after killing its crew. Starfleet sends the Enterprise to stop them, and Captain Archer collects Dr. Arik Soong from prison, where he is serving a sentence for stealing the embryos from which the Augments were raised. Soong, who regards them as his children, promises to persuade them to stand down. In the Borderland, a lawless region between Klingon and Orion space, Orion slavers attack the Enterprise and capture several crew members, including T'Pol, and Archer needs Soong's help to rescue them from a slave market. Soong tries to escape during the rescue and then refuses to lead Archer to the Augments. Aboard the Klingon ship, Persis manipulates the Augment leader Raakin, who is killed by Malik. The Bird-of-Prey docks with the disabled Enterprise, Malik forces Archer to release Soong, and Soong announces that they will now go after the remaining Augment embryos.
