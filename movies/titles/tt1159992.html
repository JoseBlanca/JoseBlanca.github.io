The genetics

Cloning is one of the Sontarans' infiltration tools in this first half of a two-part story. Alongside mind control and the ATMOS gas devices, the invaders grow human copies in tanks; the main one is a clone of Martha Jones that receives her memories and her clothes and replaces her at UNIT as a mole. According to Wikipedia, the production originally planned for all the factory workers to be clones, but cut this back to Martha; it was the first time the revived series showed the cloning process on screen. The cloners are the Sontarans, a militaristic warrior species, and the copy is cast as an evil double who calmly reports that there is no cause for alarm as the invasion begins. The growth of an adult copy with transferred memories is pure science fiction.

The story

Fifty-two people die simultaneously in cars fitted with ATMOS, a zero-emissions system invented by the teenage prodigy Luke Rattigan, among them a reporter who had called the system dangerous. Martha Jones, now a UNIT medical officer, calls in the Doctor, who arrives with Donna Noble. UNIT raids the ATMOS factory, whose workers have never taken a sick day, and two soldiers find a cloning tank in the basement before being captured by General Staal of the Sontarans. The Doctor discovers that Rattigan is working for the Sontarans and escapes from their ship. Martha is captured, and the Sontaran Commander Skorr grows a clone of her, loaded with her memories, that takes her place in UNIT. At Donna's home, the Doctor finds that ATMOS hides a gas weapon; Staal activates all 400 million devices, and the episode ends with cars filling the world with poison while Donna's grandfather Wilfred is trapped in one.
