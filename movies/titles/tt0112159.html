The genetics

Biological creation runs beneath the series' mecha and religious imagery. Rei Ayanami is a clone made from the remains of Yui Ikari, and Nerv keeps a tank of spare Rei bodies to run the Dummy Plug system, treating her as a replaceable tool; according to the series wiki, the Evangelions themselves are cloned from the Angels Adam and Lilith, encased in armour. The backstory traces the Second Impact to experiments on Adam, and the secret council SEELE and Gendo Ikari pursue the Human Instrumentality Project, a forced evolution that would merge all human souls. The scientists and commanders behind these projects, Gendo, Ritsuko Akagi and SEELE, are secretive and manipulative, driven by personal obsessions and grand ideological schemes, and the children who pilot the Evas pay the price. The genetics is mythic rather than scientific: cloning and evolution are fused with Kabbalah, Christian symbols and psychoanalysis.

The story

In 2015, fifteen years after the 'Second Impact' cataclysm killed half of humanity, the paramilitary organization Nerv defends the fortified city of Tokyo-3 against monstrous beings called Angels. Fourteen-year-old Shinji Ikari is summoned by his estranged father, Nerv's commander Gendo Ikari, to pilot Evangelion Unit-01, a giant biomechanical machine. With fellow pilots Rei Ayanami and Asuka Langley Soryu he fights a succession of Angels, at a growing psychological cost: his friend Toji is crippled, Asuka breaks down and Rei dies sacrificing herself, only to reappear as 'the third Rei'. Nerv's chief scientist, Ritsuko Akagi, reveals that Rei is one of many clones made from the remains of Shinji's mother, Yui, used for the 'Dummy Plug' system. After Shinji kills Kaworu, the last Angel, Gendo launches the Human Instrumentality Project to merge humanity's souls, and the final episodes follow Shinji's inner struggle to accept himself.
