The story

Two young environmentalists secretly filming at a Nevada mine to expose illegal toxic-waste dumping record an illicit sale of a seed whose leaves have remarkable healing properties. The deal goes wrong, the seller is shot, and the dropped seed instantly sprouts roots that spread at enormous speed. A covert government agency, SCOPE, calls in the palaeobotanist Dr. Jocelyn Stein, who recognises the seeds as coming from the Garden of Eden, which she and her colleague Dr. Jeremy Frame had been searching for. She realises that Frame has genetically modified the ancient seeds so that the plants absorb toxins, but that as a result they will spread over the whole planet, destroying all life in their path. In the manner of a television disaster movie, the heroes flee the spreading growth and opening fault lines, an obstructive official is devoured by the menace, and a discredited theory finally provides the means to stop it.
