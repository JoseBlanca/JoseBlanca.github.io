The story

In this standalone sequel to Twisted Pair, Cade Altair, one of a pair of identical twin brothers described as hybrid artificial intelligences, donates a large sum to a run-down psychiatric hospital. The hospital turns out to be the front for a joint corporate and government conspiracy that kidnaps young patients and harvests their blood for genetic experiments. Cade's brother Cale resurfaces, abducting patients for the experiments before Cade can save them while trying to cure himself with experimental drugs. Instead of simply rescuing the patients, Cade trains them to become mystical warriors, and his plan succeeds in stopping the conspiracy.
