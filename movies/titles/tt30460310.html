The genetics

Genetics has only a background role. The hero's powers and the metahumans of the series come from human experimentation rather than genetic engineering as such: Reilly was bitten by a prisoner turned into a spider-human hybrid in a German wartime camp, an image that evokes the abuses of medical experiments on prisoners, and the metahumans suffer conditions that a doctor, Alethea Faber, tries to cure with treatments that make them worse. Her experiments also aged her own son rapidly. The sources consulted describe the mutations in general terms and do not present them as explicitly genetic, so the series' interest for genetics lies mainly in its portrayal of scientists and doctors whose interventions on human bodies produce suffering, in a noir setting where the body becomes a site of experiment.

The story

In an alternate New York of the 1930s, Ben Reilly is an aging private investigator who once fought crime as "the Spider" but gave it up after the death of his fiancée, Ruby Williams. He acquired his powers as a sergeant in the First World War, when, freeing prisoners from a German camp, he was bitten by a feral, mutated human-spider, the result of experiments carried out on prisoners there. A case involving the nightclub singer Cat Hardy draws him back into action and into conflict with the crime boss Silvermane, the city's mayor and a group of metahumans, among them Sandman and Tombstone. The series also involves Dr. Alethea Faber, a doctor trying to find a cure for the metahumans, whose treatments worsen their condition. The eight-episode series stars Nicolas Cage and premiered on MGM+ and Prime Video in May 2026; it was cancelled after one season.
