The genetics

Genetic enhancement defines the series' hero. Master Chief and his fellow Spartans are genetically engineered supersoldiers, products of the SPARTAN-II programme created for the military by Dr. Catherine Halsey; a newer SPARTAN-III programme recruits further soldiers in the second season. Halsey, the series' central scientist, is presented as brilliant but misanthropic: she believes emotion is the root of selfish behaviour and secretly aims to 'improve' humanity by removing it, she has made flash clones of herself, and she modelled the AI implanted in John's brain on her own. The Spartans are humanity's main defence against an alien genocide, yet the series dwells on what the programme took from them, notably John's attempt to recover his humanity. Real genetic engineering cannot produce supersoldiers; the idea belongs to military science fiction.

The story

Set in the 26th century, the series follows humanity's war against the Covenant, an alliance of alien races bent on wiping it out. Master Chief John-117, a genetically engineered Spartan supersoldier, fights the Covenant on the colony of Madrigal with Captain Jacob Keyes and his daughter, scientist Miranda Keyes, and meets Kwan Ha, the teenage daughter of a rebel leader. John carries in his brain the AI Cortana, modelled on the brain of Dr. Catherine Halsey, creator of the Spartan programme, and forms a bond with Makee, a human raised by the Covenant's leaders. In the second season John and his Silver Team hunt the Covenant, meet the deserter Soren-066, contend with the Office of Naval Intelligence and face the invasion of the planet Reach, while more is revealed about Halsey, whose misanthropy drives her to 'improve' humanity by removing emotion. The series was cancelled after two seasons.
