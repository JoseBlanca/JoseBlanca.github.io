The genetics

The zombie outbreak comes from a laboratory: a disgraced virologist, working in a hidden lab, accidentally created the zombie virus while trying to develop a COVID-19 vaccine. The film, made during the COVID-19 pandemic and billed as the first Telugu zombie film, uses this as a satirical premise rather than a scientific one, and does not describe the virus in genetic terms. Science fails in the story: the well-meaning researcher unleashes the disaster and is killed by his first victim, and the cure comes not from medicine but from the divine water of a temple. Critics read the film as a satirical potpourri mixing pandemic, zombies and Rayalaseema faction rivalry.

The story

Game designer Mario travels with his friends Bhadram and Maggie to Kurnool for the wedding of his programmer Kalyan to Pushkala, daughter of the violent chieftain Bhooma Reddy, who is locked in a feud with the rival factionist Veera Reddy. On the way Mario runs over an infected man, who bites Bhadram, and a zombie outbreak spreads through the village during the wedding. Veera Reddy's men abduct Kalyan, and Mario sets out to rescue him with a wedding guest, Nandini, who turns out to be Veera Reddy's daughter Sailaja, and with Bhooma Reddy's henchman Kasi. Mario learns that the feud began when his own father jilted Veera Reddy's sister. Tracing the infection to a hidden lab, he meets a disgraced virologist who created the zombie virus by accident while trying to develop a COVID-19 vaccine, and who hints, before being killed, that the cure lies in a sacred temple. There Mario lures the horde into the temple's divine water, curing everyone, and the feuding families reconcile.
