The genetics

Underworld gives the vampire and werewolf myths a pseudo-genetic explanation. The two species descend from a common ancestor, Alexander Corvinus, whose descendant Michael carries a unique genetic strain; with his blood, the Lycan scientist Singe plans to create a hybrid without the weaknesses of either species. The creators conceived the conditions as a viral mutagen and a 'genetic anomaly transmitted by blood'. The hybrid project, backed by the Lycan leader Lucian, is the reason Michael is hunted, and the vampire elder Viktor opposes it as an abomination, to the point of having killed his own pregnant daughter. Singe is a minor, villainous figure killed by Viktor, while Michael becomes the sympathetic hybrid hero. The genetics is decorative: bloodlines, bites and transfusions stand in for real inheritance, and nothing like a cross between two 'species' through a bite exists.

The story

A centuries-old secret war divides vampires and Lycans (werewolves). Selene, a vampire assassin, discovers that the Lycans are hunting Michael Corvin, a young American doctor. Michael is bitten by Lucian, the Lycan leader believed dead, and Selene hides him while suspecting that the vampire Kraven is conspiring with the enemy. She awakens the vampire elder Viktor prematurely. The captured Lycan scientist Singe reveals that vampires and Lycans share a common ancestor, Alexander Corvinus, and that Michael, his descendant, carries a unique genetic strain that can produce a vampire–Lycan hybrid free of both species' weaknesses. Lucian also tells of Viktor's murder of his own daughter for loving a Lycan. Selene bites the dying Michael, who becomes a hybrid; she learns that Viktor killed her family and beheads him. In the final scene the elder Marcus awakens as a hybrid.
