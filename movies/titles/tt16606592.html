The genetics

The film's premise is a mysterious condition that the sources call both an epidemic and a mutation, transforming people into animals; its cause is never explained, and researchers and doctors are helpless, with a failed operation on Fix's beak leaving a wound that will not heal. Genetics is therefore only a passing frame: the story is a fable about difference, adolescence and society's fear of what it cannot control, in which the mutants are segregated and hunted. Émile changes after his mother has, but the film presents the condition as one that can strike anyone rather than as something inherited. The film sides with the transformed and with a father's acceptance of his son.

The story

In a near future, a mysterious epidemic is turning people into animals in body and mind, and those affected are confined in specialised centres. The chef François and his 16-year-old son Émile move to the south of France to be near François's wife Lana, who has been sent to a centre there; when the truck carrying the patients crashes, they escape into the forest. As François searches for Lana with the help of the gendarme Julia, Émile, who befriends the bird-man Fix, begins to change into a wolf, hiding it from everyone until his father discovers and accepts it. After Émile attacks a classmate who tortures him, villagers and militia hunt him; Fix is shot dead saving him, and Émile finds his mother, now a bear-like creature, among the escaped creatures. Captured, then rescued by his father from the gendarmerie, Émile is finally told by François to run, and escapes into the forest as his father is arrested.
