The genetics

Genetics appears as forensic science: DNA profiling of blood, saliva, hair and other traces is one of the team's routine tools for identifying victims and linking suspects to crime scenes, alongside fingerprints, toxicology, entomology and trace evidence. The scientists are the heroes of the series, portrayed as meticulous, sceptical and devoted to letting the evidence speak, and the lab staff, such as the DNA analyst Greg Sanders, are given full characters rather than serving as background. The series compresses forensic work considerably: results that take days or weeks in real laboratories often arrive within an episode. It has been credited with the so-called "CSI effect", the claim that crime dramas raise jurors' and victims' expectations about forensic evidence, particularly DNA testing. Early studies suggested the effect might be a myth, but later research found that television crime dramas do influence public and juror expectations, and prosecutors have reported pressure to present more forensic evidence in court.

The story

CSI: Crime Scene Investigation is a police procedural that ran on CBS for 15 seasons, from 2000 to 2015. It follows the crime scene investigators of the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department, who solve murders and other crimes mainly through physical evidence rather than confessions or witnesses. The team is first led by Gil Grissom, a forensic entomologist, with blood-spatter analyst Catherine Willows as his second in command and investigators Warrick Brown, Nick Stokes and Sara Sidle; the laboratory technician Greg Sanders, the team's DNA specialist, later becomes a field investigator. Medical examiner Al Robbins and police captain Jim Brass complete the core cast, and in later seasons D.B. Russell and Julie Finlay take over the lab. Each episode typically follows one or two cases from the crime scene to the laboratory, mixing scientific deduction with the characters' personal lives. The series ended with a feature-length finale, "Immortality", and spawned several spin-offs and a revival, CSI: Vegas.
