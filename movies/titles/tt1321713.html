The genetics

Genetic engineering is the origin of the mutants around whom the whole telenovela revolves. Dr. Júlia, a physician at the biotech company Progênese, created superpowered humans in secret experiments and kept some of them, such as Samira, in her laboratory from birth. She is a classic mad scientist: driven by her experiments, she has people murdered to protect them, takes an elixir of youth and allies herself with alien reptilians. The series uses the mutants to stage a debate about difference and persecution, in the manner of X-Men, a model for the first part of the trilogy: the public hunts the mutants, and a government department splits between those who want to imprison only the violent and those who want to exterminate them all. As the story progresses, genetics gives way to fantasy, with references to Dracula, The Island of Dr. Moreau and Jurassic Park, Brazilian folklore and science-fiction aliens. The science is not meant to be realistic.

The story

This Brazilian telenovela, broadcast by Record in 2008–2009, is the second part of a trilogy that began with Caminhos do Coração. In the first part, Dr. Júlia Zaccarias, a physician at the biotechnology company Progênese, had created genetically modified humans with superpowers in a secret laboratory on the island of Arraial. Now, having taken a rejuvenating elixir to escape prison, she runs the laboratory under the name Juli Di Trevi. The existence of the mutants becomes public, and men and women turned into vampires, werewolves, snakes, spiders and other creatures attack São Paulo, while a government department, DEPECOM, is divided between controlling the mutants and exterminating them. Maria, heiress to the Mayer fortune, founds the League of Good to protect peaceful mutants against the villains' league, which includes her twin sister Samira, raised in Júlia's laboratory. The story expands to include aliens, reptilians, vampires and a journey to the centre of the Earth.
