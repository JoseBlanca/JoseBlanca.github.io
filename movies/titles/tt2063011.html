The genetics

The plot turns on a man-made bioweapon. The rogue agent Sean Wong steals smallpox virus and plans to mutate it into a biological weapon, unleash it and profit by selling a vaccine through a corrupt pharmaceutical company; to do so he forces Rachel Kan, a disease-control doctor, to combine smallpox with a second virus, the Carnot virus, under threat to her mother. He then infects a child with the new virus to blackmail the heroes. The scientists in the film are a thief who dies early and Rachel, a coerced victim; the villainy lies with the agent, the arms dealer and corrupt police, who treat the virus as a commodity. The film does not explain how the two viruses are combined; the engineering is a thriller device rather than a subject of the story.

The story

During a mission in Jordan to escort a scientist who has stolen a sample of smallpox virus, International Defense Commission agent Sean Wong betrays his team, killing agent Jon Man's girlfriend and leaving Jon with a bullet in his brain that will paralyse him within weeks. Sean escapes with the virus and plans, with the arms dealer Tyler, to turn it into a biological weapon and sell a vaccine to a corrupt pharmaceutical company. Jon learns that he has a long-lost brother, Man Yeung, a criminal in Malaysia who is working for the gang. Yeung forces Rachel Kan, a doctor at the Asian disease control centre, to steal a sample of another virus, and Sean has her combine it with smallpox. The brothers clash, then join forces when Sean injects the new virus into Yeung's daughter and holds the antidote. After chases and gunfights ending on a ship, Yeung kills Sean, and Jon's fate is left uncertain.
