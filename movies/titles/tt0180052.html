The genetics

The villain is a clone of the hero. Rex Crater was made by the geneticist Dr. Runa Pendankin for the mobster Mike Marucci, who wanted a public face for his operations, from DNA taken from Pluto's appendix, removed while he was in prison. The clone turns against his makers, shoots them both into orbit and builds his own criminal empire, which brings him into conflict with Pluto; the plot twist allows Eddie Murphy to play both hero and villain. Cloning also appears as a running gag: Tony Francis has two cloned wives and does not care which is the original. Genetics is treated as a comic device rather than a serious subject, and the scientist is a minor figure. Real cloning would produce a much younger twin, not an adult copy who looks like the original at the same age.

The story

In 2080, on the lunar colony of Little America, the reformed smuggler Pluto Nash buys a nightclub from his friend Tony Francis to save him from mobsters, and with his friend Miguel and the android Bruno turns it into a success. Seven years later he hires Dina Lake, a young woman stranded on the Moon. When he refuses to sell the club to the mysterious entrepreneur Rex Crater, Crater's henchmen blow it up. Helped by Dina, Bruno, the retired detective Rowland and the smuggler Felix Laranga, Pluto learns that Crater has links to human cloning and to the mobster Mike Marucci, and he infiltrates Crater's casino at Moon Beach. There Crater reveals that he is Pluto's clone and that he has disposed of his creators by shooting them into orbit. After a fight, Pluto throws Crater out of a window to his death and later reopens the rebuilt Club Pluto.
