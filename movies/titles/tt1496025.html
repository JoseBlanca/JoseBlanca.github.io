The genetics

Genetics drives the plot of this fourth Underworld film. Eve is a hybrid carrying the 'Corvinus strain', and the villain, Dr. Jacob Lane, is a Lycan posing as a human scientist who runs the biotechnology company Antigen. He keeps Selene and Michael in cryogenic storage as research subjects, has engineered his son into a near-invulnerable Super-Lycan, and wants Eve's DNA, or according to some sources her eggs or a forced pregnancy, to breed a perfected Lycan race. The film presents this as a sinister programme of human experimentation and racial improvement inside a corporation, a front that also misleads governments, and both Lane and his son are killed. Reviewers noted that the cloning and genetic experiments are invoked with little explanation. Its genetics is entirely fantasy: vampires and werewolves do not have genes for silver resistance.

The story

After the existence of vampires and Lycans is revealed, humans wage a campaign of extermination against both. Selene is captured and spends twelve years in cryogenic suspension at Antigen, a biotechnology company. Freed by a mysterious telepathic girl, she finds that the girl, Eve, is a vampire–Lycan hybrid and her daughter with Michael Corvin. Eve is hunted by Lycans, and a vampire coven that gives them refuge betrays her. Selene learns that Antigen, officially devoted to killing vampires, is run by Lycans posing as humans. Its director, Dr. Jacob Lane, wants Eve's DNA to perfect the Lycan race; his son Quint is already a genetically enhanced 'Super-Lycan' immune to silver. Selene and her allies storm Antigen, Selene kills Quint and Eve kills Lane, but Michael's cryogenic tank is found empty.
