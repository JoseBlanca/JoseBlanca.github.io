The genetics

The series uses cloning to tell a comic and sad story about midlife crisis and marriage. The spa's "treatment" is actually a clandestine cloning business that copies its clients, memories included, and then gasses the originals and buries them in a park. The spa's pitch is that the client's DNA is rebuilt or purified into a better version, but the series never shows a specific genetic change, and the clone's better mood and performance can be read as a matter of attitude: he is identical to Miles but experiences his life afresh, which makes the point that circumstances and outlook shape a person as much as biology. The couple's fertility problems run through the story and end in an ambiguous pregnancy in which the two possible fathers are genetically identical, so that paternity would be impossible to settle by an ordinary DNA test. The cloning technicians are shown as secretive, low-level operators worried about their corporate headquarters. Copying an adult together with his memories is impossible, and the series treats it as a fantastic premise rather than as science.

The story

Miles Elliot, a burnt-out copywriter whose marriage to Kate is strained and who is struggling to conceive a child with her, spends the couple's savings, which one critic says were meant for fertility treatment, on a visit to the mysterious Top Happy Spa, which promises to rebuild his DNA into a better version of himself. He wakes up buried in a forest and returns home to find a new version of himself living his life: the spa has secretly cloned him, and the clone, who has Miles's memories, is happier, more energetic and more successful. The two Miles first try to share his life, but jealousy grows as everyone prefers the clone. Kate learns the truth and makes the clone move out; he tries to have a clone of her made at the spa, but the staff refuse. Episodes revisit events from Kate's perspective, and at one point Miles is abducted and questioned by the FDA. In the end Kate announces that she is pregnant without knowing which Miles is the father, and the three decide to raise the child together.
