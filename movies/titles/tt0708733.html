The genetics

The episode's horror rests on a genetic idea: an alien parasite from Tarchannen III reproduces by overwriting its host's DNA, turning humanoids, gradually and irreversibly, into members of its own invisible species. Dr. Crusher orders a full genetic analysis, finds the organism (in the thymus, according to Memory Alpha) and removes it surgically, which reverses the change in Leijten and La Forge in time; the earlier victims cannot be saved. The only scientist is the ship's doctor, shown as competent and caring, and no one engineers anything: the threat is a natural alien life cycle, and Picard has the planet quarantined to protect both visitors and creatures. Rewriting an adult's whole genome into another species is fiction, though it loosely echoes real viruses and parasites that insert their genetic material into host cells.

The story

Lieutenant Commander Susanna Leijten visits the Enterprise because members of an away team she and Geordi La Forge served on five years earlier, on the planet Tarchannen III, have been deserting and stealing shuttles to return there. One shuttle burns up in the atmosphere. On the planet the new away team finds an empty shuttle and strange footprints; Leijten falls ill and begins to transform, her veins turning blue and her fingers fusing into claws. Reconstructing the old mission on the holodeck, La Forge discovers the shadow of an invisible humanoid creature, and then changes into one himself and slips down to the surface. Dr. Crusher finds that a parasite in Leijten's body is rewriting her DNA and removes it. Using ultraviolet light, Leijten finds La Forge among the creatures and talks him back aboard to be cured; the others are too far changed and are left on the protected planet.
