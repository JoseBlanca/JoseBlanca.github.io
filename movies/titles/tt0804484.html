The genetics

Cloning is central to the Empire's storylines, and critics singled out the "genetic dynasty" as one of the show's highlights. The Empire's legitimacy rests on its rulers being exact copies of Cleon I, an attempt by one man to rule forever through his genome. The series uses this to explore whether identical genes produce identical people: the clones are raised to be the same, yet differ in temperament and choices. In the first season, Brother Dawn, who is colour-blind, turns out not to be an exact genetic copy of Cleon I and becomes involved with an anti-Empire plot to replace him with an impostor; the Cleons then learn that Cleon I's genetic code was meddled with and that Dawn is not the only clone with genetic abnormalities, undermining the dynasty's claim to purity. A religious leader who teaches that clones have no souls adds a further challenge. The scientists behind the cloning remain in the background, serving the throne. In reality, clones share their donor's genome but develop as separate individuals, as identical twins do.

The story

Based on Isaac Asimov's novels, the series is set in the far future, when most of the galaxy is ruled by the Empire, a "genetic dynasty" of clones of the long-dead Emperor Cleon I. Three clones of different ages rule together: Brother Dawn, the young heir; Brother Day, the reigning emperor; and Brother Dusk, the retired elder. Unknown to the outside world, the dynasty has been guided for centuries by Demerzel, an ageless robot. The mathematician Hari Seldon has developed psychohistory, which predicts the behaviour of large populations, and he forecasts the inevitable fall of the Empire. With the help of the young prodigy Gaal Dornick, he persuades the Empire to let him establish a Foundation on the remote planet Terminus to preserve humanity's knowledge and soften the consequences of the fall. The series follows the conflicts between the Empire and the growing Foundation over the centuries. It premiered on Apple TV+ in 2021.
