The genetics

Biomedical experimentation drives the mystery of the series, although genetics is rarely named explicitly. Takahara Academy is a research facility where children with unusual powers are raised and studied; reviewers have pointed to the probable military applications of the research and to its utopian vision serving an elite. The children's fate is linked to the Man-eaters that roam the outside world, and a disease that affects them is connected to these monsters. Brain transplants into other people's bodies, as with Kiruko, raise questions of identity, and in the manga the first baby born naturally at the facility, Tokio's, is cloned, and the doctor responsible cannot tell which child is the original. The scientists and staff are mostly secretive and manipulative, serving the plans of the facility's director. The series uses these elements more for mystery and emotional drama than for scientific explanation; no whole-brain transplant has ever been performed in humans, and damaged nerve tissue and immune rejection are among the obstacles.

The story

This anime series, adapted from Masakazu Ishiguro's manga, follows two storylines. Fifteen years after a disaster destroyed civilisation, a boy named Maru and his bodyguard Kiruko travel across a ruined Japan in search of "Heaven", fighting monsters called Man-eaters, which Maru can destroy with a mysterious power. Kiruko reveals that their brain was transplanted into the body of their sister after being fatally wounded. Meanwhile, in Takahara Academy, an isolated facility the staff call Heaven, children with unusual abilities are raised without contact with the outside world, which they are told is Hell. A girl there, Tokio, who looks exactly like Maru, receives a message asking whether she wants to go "outside of the outside". As the series progresses, it emerges that the facility is conducting experiments on the children, that the Man-eaters are linked to them, and that Tokio has a baby. The 13-episode season, broadcast in 2023, ends on a cliffhanger, as the manga is ongoing.
