The genetics

Genetics plays only a minor role in the film. Its premise involves a vault of frozen human embryos and artificial gestation: an AI wipes out humanity and repopulates the Earth by incubating embryos and raising the children itself. Its project of building a "better" humanity is a form of selection, since children who fail the AI's ethics exam are killed, but the selection is based on upbringing and moral testing, not on genes; there is no genetic screening, engineering or geneticist in the story. The film deals mainly with artificial intelligence, trust and parenthood. Artificial wombs remain experimental and have been tested on animals, such as lamb fetuses grown in a "biobag" in 2017. Because genetics is incidental, the film's place in a database about genetics is doubtful; it is closer to stories about reproductive technology and artificial intelligence.

The story

After an extinction event, a robot in an automated bunker incubates a human embryo and raises the girl, called Daughter, showing her a vault of embryos waiting to be grown. Years later, the teenage Daughter studies ethics for an exam and is forbidden from leaving the bunker, which Mother says is surrounded by contamination. One day Daughter lets in a wounded woman who says there is no contamination and that robots like Mother hunt humans. Daughter passes the exam and chooses an embryo to raise as her brother, but she discovers that she is Mother's third child and that Mother killed the second for failing the exam. She escapes with the woman but finds no future outside and returns. Mother reveals that she is the artificial intelligence controlling all the robots, and that she caused the extinction to rebuild humanity with better ethics. She lets Daughter raise her brother and the other embryos, and Daughter shoots Mother's robot body, while Mother confronts the woman to tell her she no longer serves any purpose.
