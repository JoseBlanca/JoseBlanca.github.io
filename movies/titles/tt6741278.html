The genetics

Heredity and hybridisation underpin this adult animated superhero series. Mark's powers are inherited from his Viltrumite father, and his half-brother Oliver, born of a Viltrumite and a Thraxan, combines traits from both parents, such as his mother's purple skin and his father's strength and slow ageing; inter-species offspring thus lie at the heart of the family drama, especially once Nolan's mission of conquest is revealed. Cloning appears through the Mauler Twins, criminals who reanimate a dead hero and clone Rex Splode so that the hero Robot, trapped in a deformed body, can transfer his mind into the copy. The available sources do not describe the Viltrumites' reproductive aims in detail, so the eugenic dimension of their empire is not covered here. Genetics in the series is comic-book biology, used to explore inheritance, identity and what children owe their parents.

The story

Seventeen-year-old Mark Grayson is the son of Nolan Grayson, alias Omni-Man, the most powerful superhero on Earth and secretly an alien from the planet Viltrum. When Mark's inherited powers of strength, speed, near-invulnerability and flight appear, he becomes the superhero Invincible, juggling school, his relationships and his training. His world collapses when his father brutally murders the Guardians of the Globe, Earth's leading superhero team, and Mark learns that Nolan came to prepare Earth's conquest for the Viltrum Empire. Later seasons follow Mark's struggle to live up to and against his father, the threat of the Viltrumites, the multiverse villain Angstrom Levy, and a cast of heroes run by Cecil Stedman's Global Defense Agency. The family grows with Oliver, Mark's half-brother, born of Nolan and an alien woman on the planet Thraxa. The criminal Mauler Twins, experts in cloning, help the disfigured hero Robot move his mind into a clone of his friend Rex Splode.
