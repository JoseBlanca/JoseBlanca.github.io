The genetics

Cloning is the series' central twist. A DNA test, prompted by two adopted sisters' identical blood types, reveals that the sisters are clones, and the crew learns that their parents had them made with technology developed by Zack's father, a biotechnology researcher, as fresh bodies to receive their memories. Charce was created as a spare body for King Noah of Vixia, and Aries is a clone of Noah's daughter Seira, who opposed the project and smuggled Aries out to live a normal life. When a Genome Control Act threatens to expose the clones, the parents try to have the children vanish in space. The series condemns the originals, who treat their children as replaceable bodies and end up arrested, while the clones affirm that the bonds formed on their journey make them a true family, regardless of their origin. Real cloning yields a genetically identical individual, not a copy of a person, and memory transplantation is fiction.

The story

In 2063, nine students from Caird High School go on a planetary camp, but a mysterious sphere transports them 5,012 light-years from home. In an abandoned ship they name the Astra, they hop between planets to gather supplies, while suspecting that a saboteur is among them. When Quitterie notices that she and her adopted sister Funicia have identical blood types, Zack runs a DNA test showing they are genetically identical: the crew discovers that they are clones, made as younger bodies into which their parents meant to transplant their memories, and that the parents stranded them in space because a new Genome Control Act would expose them. The traitor, Charce, confesses that he is a clone of King Noah of Vixia, sent to eliminate the others, and that Aries is a clone of Noah's murdered daughter Seira. The crew returns home, exposes the conspiracy and the hidden history of their planet, and the originals are arrested.
