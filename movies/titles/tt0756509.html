The genetics

Human cloning and artificial gestation are at the core of the series' mythology, though the show is mainly a family and teen drama. Kyle, codenamed "781227" or "XY", was cloned from the scientist Adam Baylin and grown at Zzyzx, Baylin's facility for experiments in artificial gestation; Jessi, "781228" or "XX", was cloned from Baylin's former girlfriend Sarah Emerson in a competing experiment. The scientists and organisations around them are mostly portrayed as treating the clones as assets: corporations and a secret society seek to control or copy Kyle, while Baylin is shown more sympathetically as his mentor. The series suggests that Kyle's abilities come from his engineered development, but it also stresses that his identity is shaped by the family that raises him. Real clones would be born as babies, not as teenagers without memories or navels, and cloning would not create superhuman mental powers.

The story

Kyle XY is an American science fiction series broadcast on ABC Family from 2006 to 2009. A teenage boy wakes up naked in a forest near Seattle with no memory and no navel. He is taken in by a therapist, Nicole Trager, and her family, who name him Kyle. Kyle must learn everyday life from scratch but has extraordinary mental abilities. In the first season he searches for his origins and finds a link to Adam Baylin, a brilliant student who disappeared twenty years earlier. In the second season he learns that he was created by scientists in an experiment in artificial gestation at a facility called Zzyzx, and meets Jessi, a female counterpart created by a rival programme and controlled by the company Madacorp. The third season centres on the secret society Latnok, whose director, Michael Cassidy, killed Jessi's mother. In the series finale Kyle learns that Latnok plans to produce multiple clones of him and that Cassidy is his half-brother.
