The genetics

The genetic element is the inherited damage caused by a military weapon. Project Jellyfish developed a chemical agent that dissolves bones; the soldiers who worked on it were inoculated and appeared unharmed, but their exposure was passed to their children, who developed without skeletons and were stillborn, and doctors told one of the fathers that he carried a DNA pathogen. The bereaved fathers turn the weapon against the scientists and officials responsible, and the episode presents them as sympathetic avengers whose revenge must nevertheless be stopped, while the general who ran the programme is now a politician. A second strand concerns the Cortexiphan trials, Walter's drug experiments on children that left Simon Phillips with uncontrollable telepathy, which the episode presents as a source of lasting suffering and guilt. Both stories show scientific programmes harming people long after the fact. A chemical that destroys bone and causes heritable damage of this kind is fiction, although some chemicals can cause birth defects.

The story

In this Fringe episode, a scientist at the pharmaceutical company Intrepus is killed by a blue powder that turns his bones to dust. The package is traced to a former Marine, Aaron Downey, who falls into a coma after being hit by a car while fleeing. To learn what he knows, Walter recruits Simon Phillips, a former subject of his childhood Cortexiphan trials, who has lived in isolation because he cannot control his telepathy; he is comforted by Olivia, whose mind he cannot read. Simon and Nina Sharp help uncover Project Jellyfish, a secret military bioweapon programme that developed a chemical agent that destroys skeletons. Downey and two other men had been inoculated against it, but their exposure passed to their children, who were stillborn without skeletons, and they are taking revenge on those responsible. Olivia stops the last two before they can attack a gala for Congressman Jim Thorn, the general who oversaw the project.
