The genetics

The series is less about genetics than about biomedical experimentation gone wrong. Lee Byeong-chan, who holds a doctorate in cell biology, creates the virus in his school laboratory, reportedly from a hormone isolated from mice, with the aim of removing fear from his son; the result is a highly contagious agent that turns its hosts into rage-filled zombies. He is a tragic, morally compromised figure: a father who wanted to protect his child, who experiments on his own family and hides the consequences,. The series links his choice to the bullying and violence of the school, presenting the virus as an extreme answer to a social problem. No real virus could alter human emotions in this way, and the idea of "removing fear" with a single agent contradicts how complex behaviours are controlled by many genes, brain circuits and experiences.

The story

Based on a webtoon by Joo Dong-geun, this South Korean series follows a class of students at Hyosan High School when a zombie virus breaks out. Lee Byeong-chan, the school's science teacher and a former pharmaceutical researcher, created the "Jonas virus" to help his son Jin-su, who was being bullied, by removing his fear so that he could fight back. Instead, the injected Jin-su lost all his humanity and infected his mother, and Byeong-chan locked them up while he searched for a cure. An infected lab hamster bites a student, and the infection spreads through the school and the city of Hyosan, which the authorities eventually quarantine. Trapped in their classrooms, On-jo, Cheong-san, Nam-ra, Su-hyeok and their classmates fight to survive while the military and government struggle to contain the outbreak. Some of the infected keep their minds and become half-zombies. The series was renewed for a second season.
