The genetics

Genetic engineering lies behind the mystery. Mewtwo, the franchise's best-known artificial creature, is presented as a genetically engineered Pokémon held in a secret laboratory, and its DNA is used to make the R gas that drives Pokémon wild. The villain, Howard Clifford, is a wealthy industrialist and city founder rather than a scientist; he exploits the creature's genetic material and powers to fuse humans with Pokémon, convinced that merging them will bring progress, and is willing to have Harry killed to protect his plan. The scientists themselves stay in the background, glimpsed only through holographic records of the abandoned laboratory, and the villains also deploy Pokémon artificially enhanced in power or growth. Mewtwo, though created and exploited by humans, turns out to be a victim rather than the threat it first appears. The science is pure fantasy, intended for a family audience: no gas made from DNA could change behaviour or merge two organisms, but the story draws on a familiar image of genetic engineering as a tool of corporate ambition.

The story

Tim Goodman, who gave up his dream of becoming a Pokémon trainer, travels to Ryme City, where humans and Pokémon live side by side, after being told that his estranged father Harry, a police detective, died in a car crash. In Harry's apartment he meets Harry's Pokémon partner, an amnesiac Pikachu in a deerstalker whom only he can understand. Together they investigate R, a purple gas that makes Pokémon aggressive. They learn that Harry was investigating a genetics laboratory where Mewtwo, a genetically engineered Pokémon, was held and its DNA used to produce the gas. The city's founder, Howard Clifford, first blames his son Roger but turns out to be the mastermind: he transfers his mind into Mewtwo and releases the gas during a parade to fuse humans with their Pokémon, believing this is the key to the future. Tim defeats him and frees Mewtwo, who separates people from their Pokémon and reveals that Pikachu's mind had been fused with the injured Harry's body.
