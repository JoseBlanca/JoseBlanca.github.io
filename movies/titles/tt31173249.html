The genetics

The zombie outbreak is caused by a laboratory virus: scientists working on an island owned by a private corporation were trying to enhance human resilience, and their virus mutated uncontrollably. The film later reveals that the company's owner deliberately released it to test its capabilities on the island's inhabitants, making him the villain, while the scientists the heroine meets serve as informants and helpers who identify her rare immunity. The story

In this Cambodian zombie drama, a mother travels with her husband and children to a secluded island run by a private corporation owned by Robert, a foreign acquaintance of the family. An illness spreads that causes fever, violence and hunger for flesh, turning the infected into zombies. Her husband is bitten and turns. Fleeing with her baby to an abandoned facility, the mother meets scientists who explain that the outbreak comes from a failed experiment to enhance human resilience, whose virus mutated out of control. Her baby is infected and turns despite experimental treatments, and in despair she lets herself be bitten, but she resists the transformation thanks to a rare immunity. She becomes a leader of the survivors, learns from Robert that the outbreak was a deliberate test of the virus, kills him and destroys the facility, and continues to search for a cure.
