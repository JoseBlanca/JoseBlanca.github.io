The genetics

Cloning is the plot device that gives the film its title. On Kamino, the Kaminoans, a species of expert cloners, have produced an army of soldiers from the DNA of Jango Fett, a bounty hunter recruited by Count Dooku. The clones are genetically altered so that they age at twice the normal rate and are more obedient and easier to command, while Jango asked for one unaltered clone to raise as his son, Boba. The Kaminoans are presented as detached, courteous technicians who treat the clones as a product, and the army is later revealed to be part of the Sith plan to seize power: in the following film the clones turn on the Jedi. Real reproductive cloning produces a genetically identical twin that must develop at the normal pace and does not copy memories or skills; accelerated growth and engineered obedience are fiction.

The story

Ten years after the events of The Phantom Menace, the Galactic Republic is threatened by a Separatist movement led by Count Dooku. After two attempts on the life of Senator Padmé Amidala, Obi-Wan Kenobi follows the trail of the assassin's employer to the ocean planet Kamino, where he finds a clone army being produced for the Republic, supposedly ordered by a dead Jedi Master. Its genetic template is the bounty hunter Jango Fett, who also keeps an unaltered clone as his son, Boba. Obi-Wan follows Jango to Geonosis and discovers the Separatists' droid army, but is captured. Meanwhile Anakin Skywalker, guarding Padmé, falls in love with her and massacres the Tusken Raiders who killed his mother. When Obi-Wan, Anakin and Padmé are about to be executed, Yoda arrives with the clone troopers, beginning the Clone Wars, and Anakin and Padmé marry in secret.
