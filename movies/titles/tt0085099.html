The genetics

Genetics is a passing element of the premise. The leader of the Terrahawks, Doctor Tiger Ninestein, is the ninth of nine clones made by a scientist, Dr. Gerhard Stein, which explains his name and the fact that he can be replaced within 24 hours if he is killed. The series does not explore the cloning, who Stein was or what happened to the other clones; it is a comic touch in a puppet adventure about defending Earth from alien androids and monsters, where most of the science is technological. Real clones would be born as babies and could not replace an adult within a day.

The story

In this puppet series by Gerry Anderson, set in 2020, an alien force destroys NASA's base on Mars, and a small secret organisation, the Terrahawks, is formed to defend Earth from its base, Hawknest, in South America. The team is led by Doctor "Tiger" Ninestein, a somewhat bloodthirsty commander who is the ninth of nine clones created by Dr. Gerhard Stein and can be replaced by another clone if killed. His crew includes his second-in-command Mary Falconer, the pop star and pilot Kate Kestrel, the gunner Hawkeye, whose eyes were replaced with microcomputers, and Lieutenant Hiro, who commands an orbital battle station, helped by spherical robots called Zeroids. Their enemies are androids from the planet Guk led by the scheming Zelda, with her "children" Cy-Star and Yung-Star, who send monsters kept in cryogenic storage against Earth. The 39 episodes mix tongue-in-cheek humour with action.
