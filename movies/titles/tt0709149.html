The genetics

Khalek is a product of genetic engineering by a villain. Anubis built him in a laboratory by combining human and Goa'uld DNA and artificially accelerating his evolution, producing a being with the inherited memories of the Goa'uld, the series' parasitic villains, who is close to ascension. The episode uses the series' concept of "genetic memory", inherited knowledge passed through DNA, which is fictional; real DNA does not store memories, and evolution is not a ladder that an individual can be pushed up. The scientist, Anubis, is absent and malicious, and the episode's dilemma is about what to do with his creation: whether a being made as a weapon can be held responsible, killed preventively, or kept for research. The oversight committee's wish to study him despite the danger adds the familiar temptation to exploit a dangerous discovery.

The story

Investigating a gravitational disturbance near the planet P3X-584, SG-1 finds an underground laboratory containing a DNA manipulation device, records written in the language of the Ancients, and a man in a stasis pod. They bring him to Earth, where he calls himself Khalek and claims to have been a prisoner of a Goa'uld. By translating the laboratory's records, Daniel Jackson learns the truth: Khalek was created by the Goa'uld Anubis, who combined human and Goa'uld DNA, gave him the genetic memory of the Goa'uld and accelerated his evolution, but was defeated before finishing this "prototype". Khalek has superhuman abilities, including telekinesis, and is close to being able to ascend to a higher plane of existence. Daniel argues that he must be killed, but an oversight committee sends Richard Woolsey to ensure he is kept alive for study. Khalek escapes his confinement and SG-1 must stop him.
