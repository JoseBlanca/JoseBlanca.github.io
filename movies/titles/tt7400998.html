The story

In the near future, the environment has become so toxic that people must stay shielded from the sun and live much of their lives in virtual reality. Adam Bird, a tech worker and father of three, struggles with his family and with what it means to be human in such a world. The government insures parents of three or more children: if one parent dies, a clone takes his or her place. When Adam learns that he has serious heart disease, news his ex-wife Reena will welcome, he must keep his company going so that their insurance remains valid and hold on to the real world.
