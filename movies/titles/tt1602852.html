The genetics

Genetics is one of the two main tools of this documentary, alongside archival genealogy. DNA testing is used to explore the ancestry of the twelve guests, connecting personal identity with the history of migration to the United States. The final episode turns to the then-emerging field of whole-genome sequencing: Gates has his own genome and his father's sequenced and analysed by the personal genomics company Knome and the Broad Institute, to see what they can reveal about ancestry and health. Geneticists appear as experts who interpret the results for the participants. The series belongs to a line of programmes by Gates, including African American Lives and Finding Your Roots, that use genetic genealogy to tell stories about American identity.

The story

Faces of America is a four-part PBS documentary series, broadcast in 2010 and hosted by the Harvard scholar Henry Louis Gates Jr. It uses genealogical research and genetics to trace the family histories of twelve well-known Americans: Elizabeth Alexander, Mario Batali, Stephen Colbert, Louise Erdrich, Malcolm Gladwell, Eva Longoria, Yo-Yo Ma, Mike Nichols, Queen Noor of Jordan, Mehmet Oz, Meryl Streep and Kristi Yamaguchi. The episodes, "Our American Stories", "Becoming American", "Making America" and "Know Thyself", use these personal stories to explore immigration and the making of American identity. In the final episode, Gates looks at what can be learned from his own complete genome sequence and his father's, analysed by a personal genomics company and the Broad Institute. The series was followed by Finding Your Roots, also hosted by Gates.
