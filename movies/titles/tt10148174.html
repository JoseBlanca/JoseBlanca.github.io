The genetics

Genetics plays only a marginal role in this limited series, which is mainly a coming-of-age story; science appears as research into the zombie plague. Leo and his colleagues at the CRM facility try to understand what causes reanimation and how to make the undead decay faster, a long-term project that Hope advances with an idea based on yeast fermentation, while another scientist, Lyla Belshaw, secretly studies reanimation as it happens in bitten or killed test subjects. The CRM's military leaders put science at the service of power and use a chemical weapon against their own allies. The origin of the outbreak, long a mystery in the franchise, is touched on only at the end: the post-credits scene suggests that it began or was made worse by a team of French scientists, introduces a faster and more aggressive kind of zombie, and shows a CDC virologist discussing overseas variants.

The story

Ten years after the zombie apocalypse, sisters Iris and Hope Bennett live in the Campus Colony, a safe community at a Nebraska university. Their adoptive father Leo, a scientist, has been taken to work at a research facility run by the Civic Republic Military (CRM), a powerful, secretive force. When he sends distress messages, the sisters and two friends leave the colony and cross the country to New York, pursued by their guardians, Felix and Huck. Huck turns out to be a CRM agent assigned to deliver Hope, a gifted student whom the CRM wants for its research. In the second season, the young people find that the CRM destroyed Omaha and the Campus Colony with a modified chlorine gas, disguising the massacre as a walker attack, and that a CRM scientist is studying reanimation on human test subjects. In a post-credits scene in France, a man accuses a scientist and her colleagues of having caused and worsened the outbreak.
