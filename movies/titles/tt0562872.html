The genetics

Two biological elements drive the story. The Daleks have been defeated by the Movellan virus, a biological weapon that attacks Dalek tissue; they free Davros to find a cure, but he and the Doctor both end up releasing it to kill Daleks, and it finally infects Davros himself. The second is the Daleks' plan to duplicate the Doctor and his companions in a "duplication chamber" and send the copies to assassinate the Time Lords' High Council; the process, which also copies minds, is stopped when Stien breaks his conditioning. Both are weapons in the hands of villains in a grim, violent story, and the Doctor turns the bioweapon against the Daleks as a last resort. The duplicates are fantasy rather than cloning in the biological sense, and a reviewer noted that "the whole duplicates business makes little sense". The virus fits the familiar image of a pathogen tailored to one species.

The story

In 1984 London, policemen led by the mercenary Lytton, who works for the Daleks, gun down escapees from a time corridor; one, Stien, gets away. In the future the Daleks free their creator Davros from a prison station, because they lost their war against the Movellans to a virus that attacks Dalek tissue and need him to find a cure. The Fifth Doctor, Tegan and Turlough are dragged down the time corridor to the London Docklands. Stien turns out to be a Dalek agent, and the Daleks plan to duplicate the Doctor and his companions to assassinate the Gallifrey High Council, but Stien breaks his conditioning and stops the process. Davros turns against the Daleks and uses the Movellan virus on those who come to kill him; the Doctor releases the virus against the Daleks on Earth. Davros is infected, Stien destroys the station and ship, and Tegan stays on Earth.
