The genetics

Genetic manipulation drives the whole plot. In a secret laboratory, scientists have produced a super-intelligent dog and, for military purposes, the Outsider, a hybrid creature designed as an assassin and linked to the dog so that it hunts it down. The animal experiments are presented sympathetically through Barbara and the dog, but the military programme and the lab director's recklessness unleash a killing machine, and the film treats this kind of research as dangerous. The genetic background is sketchy: the sources describe the lab only in general terms, and the psychic link between dog and monster is pure fantasy. Raising an animal's intelligence to human-like levels through genetic engineering is far beyond current science.

The story

At Anodyne Laboratories, a secret genetics facility in the California desert, animal psychologist Barbara White works with Einstein, a golden retriever whose intelligence has been raised by experiments. Facing closure, the lab's director secretly lets animal-rights activists in to free the animals, and they also release the Outsider, a hybrid monster bred as a military assassin and psychically linked to the dog. Einstein escapes and causes the crash of a jeep carrying Marine captain Paul Ferguson to prison. Paul flees with the dog, discovers its intelligence and, with Barbara's help, tries to evade both the authorities and the Outsider, which leaves a trail of bodies as it tracks Einstein. This loose remake of Watchers, based on Dean Koontz's novel, was released straight to video.
