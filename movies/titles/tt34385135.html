The genetics

Yeon Sang-ho's film builds its horror on a biotechnology product. Seo Young-chul, a brilliant biologist dismissed from Chains Bio, has created a mutated slime mould, based on Physarum polycephalum, that infects people, regenerates their cells and links them through mycelium into a swarm intelligence that learns from every failed attack; with his supposed vaccine he becomes able to steer it. He uses it as a weapon for revenge against the company he says plagiarized him and against Se-jeong, whom he blames for his father's suicide, and he speaks of a 'new humanity'. The company itself, which presents the slime mould's properties as a commercial marvel, is a backdrop. The heroine is also a scientist: an outcast biotechnology professor who understands the swarm's biology and defeats it by overloading its shared senses. The ending, with the vaccine gone and an infected body stirring, leaves the threat unresolved. Real slime moulds solve mazes without a brain, but they do not infect humans.

The story

The scientist Seo Young-chul warns the police of a bioterrorist attack and injects himself with a supposed vaccine against his weapon, a mutated slime mould. In the same building, the biotech company Chains Bio presents the regenerative powers and collective intelligence of the slime mould Physarum polycephalum. In the audience is Kwon Se-jeong, an ostracized, unemployed biotechnology professor invited by her ex-husband. Young-chul accuses the company's CEO of stealing his research and injects him with the mould, which spreads through the building as the infected mutate and attack. Locked in with a counter-terrorism team leader, a security guard, his disabled sister and other civilians, Se-jeong discovers that the infected share senses through mycelium and evolve as a swarm, and that Young-chul can see through their eyes. Survivor after survivor dies, and Young-chul, who blames Se-jeong for his father's suicide, takes control of the horde and spreads it into the city. Se-jeong overloads the swarm's shared senses; Young-chul dies in a burning car and the infected freeze, but one moves again.
