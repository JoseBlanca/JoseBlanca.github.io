The genetics

The final film of the series makes genetic science the root of its apocalypse. The T-virus began as an untested treatment: Umbrella's founder used it on his daughter, who suffered from the hereditary premature-ageing disease progeria, until it turned a child into a zombie. His partner, Dr. Isaacs, later released it on purpose in a religious-fanatic plan to wipe out humanity while Umbrella's elite slept in cryogenic storage. Cloning runs through the film: Isaacs keeps clones of himself, one of which finally kills him, and the heroine learns that she is a clone of Alicia Marcus. Umbrella also holds an airborne antivirus, which Alice releases to save the survivors. The scientists range from the tragic, desperate father to the genocidal corporate villain, but the overall picture is of corporate biotechnology as a catastrophe. Progeria is a real genetic disease, but no virus could treat it or raise the dead.

The story

A flashback reveals the origin of the T-virus: Umbrella founder Dr. James Marcus used an untested virus created by his colleague Dr. Charles Ashford to treat his daughter Alicia's progeria, abandoned it when a treated child became a zombie, and was then murdered on the orders of his partner, Dr. Alexander Isaacs, who took over the company. In the present, after Albert Wesker's betrayal in Washington, the Red Queen AI tells Alice that Umbrella holds an airborne antivirus but will wait until the last humans die. Alice returns to Raccoon City, joins Claire Redfield and other survivors and enters the Hive, where she learns that Umbrella released the virus deliberately to cleanse the Earth, keeping its elite frozen to rebuild it, and that she is a clone of Alicia. The Isaacs she killed before was also a clone. Alice releases the antivirus, the Hive is destroyed, and Alicia's memories are uploaded into Alice.
