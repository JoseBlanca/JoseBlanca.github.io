The genetics

Genetic engineering is the business of the villains. The Chronos corporation turns human beings into Zoanoids, genetically engineered monsters that look human but can transform into combat creatures, and its laboratories, run by a head of genetics research, keep a gallery of these forms and experiment on captives; the CIA agent Max Reed is forcibly mutated into a cockroach-like Zoanoid and dies when his body rejects the new form. The Guyver itself is alien biotechnology that bonds permanently with its host, regenerating both itself and the person wearing it. The scientists are portrayed as corporate servants of a hidden, non-human leader seeking power, and their work is shown only as a source of weapons and suffering. The film is a low-budget adaptation of a Japanese manga and does not attempt any scientific realism; genetics serves as a pretext for transformations, monsters and fights.

The story

Dr. Tetsu Segawa, a researcher for the Chronos corporation, steals an alien device called the Guyver unit and is killed by Lisker, a Chronos henchman who can transform into a monstrous Zoanoid. Before dying, Segawa hides the unit, and it is found by Sean Barker, the boyfriend of Segawa's daughter Mizuki. The unit fuses with Sean and covers him in a powerful biomechanical armour. With the help of CIA agent Max Reed, Sean fights the Zoanoids, who kidnap Mizuki to recover the device. In one battle the armour's control metal is ripped off and Sean dies, but the metal regenerates in Chronos's laboratories and, after it is accidentally swallowed by Dr. East, the head of genetics research, it brings Sean back to life. Sean defeats Lisker and the Chronos president, Fulton Balcus, who reveals himself as the Zoalord.
