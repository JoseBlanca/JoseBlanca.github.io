The genetics

The film's horror starts in a research laboratory that experiments on animals: a monkey there carries an infection that drives people into a murderous rage, and a reporter who sneaks in is bitten and spreads it to a college campus. The available plot summaries describe the monkey only as 'rabid' and do not say what the laboratory was doing or whether the agent was created or modified there; the film counts as genetics only because the contagion comes out of a research laboratory. The scientists are not characterised in the sources. Reviewers saw the film mainly as a showcase for gore effects by Carlo Rambaldi and his son, the director, and as a borrowing from George A. The story

In Miami, Frank Duffy, a reporter for a local newspaper, sneaks into a research laboratory that carries out experiments on animals. There he is bitten by a rabid monkey and falls prey to an uncontrollable fury. He spreads the infection to the nearby college, where a carefree Halloween party turns into a bloodbath. The available sources give only this brief outline and do not describe how the outbreak ends or who survives.
