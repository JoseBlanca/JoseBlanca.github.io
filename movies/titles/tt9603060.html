The genetics

Genetics is a backstory mention in this streaming spy film. Its agent Alok Sahar is a survivor of the Eugenics Wars, the late-20th-century conflict of Star Trek lore in which genetically engineered 'Augments' seized power. As a boy he was taken by Giri, an enhanced warlord who killed his family, genetically enhanced him to make him stronger than a human should be and forced him to commit atrocities. The episode gives the character his strength and his guilt, but the plot itself, a hunt for a stolen doomsday weapon, does not turn on genetics. The film thus echoes the franchise's long-standing view of genetic enhancement as tied to tyranny.

The story

In the Mirror Universe, the teenage Philippa Georgiou wins a deadly contest for the throne of the Terran Empire, poisons her family and enslaves San, the friend who helped her. Years later, in the Prime Universe of the early 24th century, Georgiou is running a nightclub on the space station Baraam when agent Alok Sahar recruits her into a Section 31 team. They try to intercept the Godsend, a doomsday weapon Georgiou once ordered built, but it is stolen by a masked man. Alok tells Georgiou that he comes from the 20th century, where a genetically enhanced warlord of the Eugenics Wars, Giri, murdered his family, enhanced him and forced him to commit atrocities. The team uncovers a mole and learns that the thief is San, who wants to use the Godsend to destroy the Prime Universe. After a fight, San dies, and Alok and Georgiou send the weapon back into the Mirror Universe.
