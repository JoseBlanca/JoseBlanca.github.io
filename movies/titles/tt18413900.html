The genetics

The film's mutants come from illegal 'genetic experiments' by the Medusa Corporation, which injects people with a serum to turn them into superpowered fighters for gladiatorial combat. The heroine is one of its victims who turns the powers she was given against the company. The story

In 2077, agent Wu Qingqing goes undercover in a Korean hospital run by the Medusa Corporation, which is conducting illegal experiments to produce mutants for gladiatorial combat. She is captured and injected with a serum meant to turn her into one of its fighters. An armed team sent to rescue her and seize a data chip is slaughtered by the corporation's mutants, but Qingqing escapes and discovers that the serum has given her vast combat powers. She is taken in by a police officer, Zhou Yang, while Medusa's head, Li Yongshun, sends more mutants and armed squads after her, among them fighters who can teleport or grow an arm of rock.
