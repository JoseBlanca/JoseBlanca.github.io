The genetics

Genetics is only part of the setting. The Republic's soldiers are clone troopers, such as Captain Rex and Commander Cody, who fight alongside the Jedi, but the film does not show or discuss how they were created or what it means to be a clone; all of them are voiced by the same actor. The story is about the war, the rescue of Jabba's son and the political plot against the Jedi, and no other genetic element appears.

The story

During the Clone Wars, Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi lead clone troopers, including Captain Rex and Commander Cody, against a Separatist droid army on the planet Christophsis. Yoda sends Ahsoka Tano to be Anakin's Padawan, and she wins his respect by helping him disable an enemy shield. The Jedi then learn that Rotta, the infant son of Jabba the Hutt, has been kidnapped in a plot by Count Dooku and Jabba's uncle Ziro to frame the Jedi and turn the Hutts against the Republic. Anakin and Ahsoka rescue Rotta on Teth and escape Dooku's assassin Asajj Ventress, whom Obi-Wan later defeats. On Coruscant, Senator Padmé Amidala uncovers Ziro's conspiracy and has him arrested. On Tatooine, Anakin distracts Dooku while Ahsoka returns Rotta to Jabba, and Padmé's message convinces Jabba to grant the Republic passage through Hutt space.
