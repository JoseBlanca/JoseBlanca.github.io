The genetics

Genetics appears through one character. Ha Yun-sang is a geneticist at a biotechnology institute, and his research is considered important for humanity's survival after the asteroid impact, which is why the elitist group fleeing the country offers to take him, and Se-kyeong, along. He refuses to leave without her, and she will not abandon the children. The sources do not say what his research involves, and no genetic work is shown; in the end he devotes himself to documenting the city and its people for possible future visitors. His profession serves mainly to set up a moral choice between escape and loyalty.

The story

With 200 days left before an asteroid strikes the Earth, South Korea is under martial law; the rich have fled and those left behind face shortages, crime, suicides and scams. In the city of Woongcheon, the former teacher Jin Se-kyeong tries to protect children and discovers a trafficking ring run by escaped convicts, responsible for the deaths of many of her former students. Her fiancé, the geneticist Ha Yun-sang, returns from the United States to be with her. Army captain Kang In-a investigates the same convicts, and the assistant priest Woo Sung-jae takes charge of his parish when his superior disappears. The crime rings are linked to a cult-like elite raising money to escape abroad, which offers Yun-sang a place because of his research. The friends fail to secure seats for the children, and the elite's escape fails when one plane cannot land and the other crashes. As the end nears, the characters try to find peace, and Se-kyeong sets out with a gun to confront the traffickers.
