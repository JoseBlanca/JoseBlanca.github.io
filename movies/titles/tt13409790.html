The story

This Nigerian drama, directed by Uduak-Obong Patrick, follows the Ezeji family after their six-year-old son Ekene is suddenly diagnosed with sickle cell disease. The diagnosis disrupts the family's life, and the parents, Nnamdi and Yemi, struggle to keep their peace and unity while dealing with doctors, a genetic counsellor, Ekene's school and the wider family, including his grandparents. The available source gives only this outline and does not describe how the story ends. The film won the award for best screenplay at The African Film Festival in 2021 and best international film at the Urban Film Festival in Miami.
