The genetics

Genetic manipulation provides the premise of this monster film. Bill, the owner of a biotech company, has genetically bred Graboids to be more powerful and intelligent, purely to provide more challenging prey for himself and his wealthy hunting clients. The resulting creatures are superior to the originals of the series, and their offspring inherit the improvements, including Shriekers able to use sound as a weapon. The genetic engineer is presented as a reckless rich man who places his entertainment above other people's safety and is eventually killed by his creation, a familiar warning about tampering with nature for frivolous ends. Jas, a field biologist, represents responsible science and helps to stop him. The film offers no explanation of how the creatures were modified, and the genetics is a plot device rather than a subject. Its theme recalls Jurassic Park, with engineered animals bred for the amusement of the rich.

The story

The seventh Tremors film. Bill, a big-game hunter who owns the biotech company Avex-Bio Tech, takes wealthy clients to hunt Graboids, giant subterranean worms, on Dark Island, a private island next to a research station. When biologist Jasmine "Jas" Welker discovers a dead Graboid and its offspring, the Shriekers, which kill a colleague, she sends her assistant Jimmy to find the veteran monster hunter Burt Gummer, now living as a recluse. Jas learns that Bill has bred the Graboids to be stronger and more intelligent for his hunt and has cut off all communications. Burt reluctantly arrives, and with only old weapons from a World War II bunker the team fights the enhanced creatures, whose Shriekers can now use their screams as weapons. Bill refuses to stop the hunt and is eaten by the largest Graboid, "the Queen". Burt lures the Queen into a trap of spikes and dynamite, sacrificing himself to kill it.
