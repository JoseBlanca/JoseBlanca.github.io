The genetics

Genetic engineering supplies the MacGuffin of this video-game adaptation. Tina has been genetically engineered with biological material from the Eridians, the vanished alien race of Pandora, because her 'father', the arms-dealing magnate Atlas, believes that only someone with Eridian biology can open the Vault and give him its advanced technology. The engineering is thus a corporate villain's tool for power, and Tina is its victim, hunted by his private army. The plan fails: the real key is Lilith, who turns out to be a full Eridian herself, and Atlas is trapped in the Vault. The film does not explain how the engineering was done and treats it as background to a comic action adventure, which critics widely panned. Mixing human and alien genetic material is pure science fiction.

The story

On the planet Pandora, the mercenary Roland and the 'Psycho' Krieg abduct the teenager Tiny Tina from the facility where she was held. Elsewhere, the bounty hunter Lilith is hired by the corporate magnate and arms dealer Atlas to bring back his daughter Tina. Returning to Pandora, her home planet, for the first time since childhood, Lilith finds Tina with the help of the robot Claptrap, realises that Atlas wants her against her will and joins Roland, Krieg and Tina when Atlas's private army attacks. She learns that Tina was genetically engineered with material from the Eridians, the ancient race that once lived on Pandora, because Atlas believes only she can open the Vault that holds their technology. With Lilith's foster mother, Dr. Patricia Tannis, the group finds the key, but at the Vault Tina cannot open it; Lilith reveals that she herself is an Eridian, opens it and traps Atlas inside.
