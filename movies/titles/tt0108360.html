The genetics

The premise is an inter-species genetic treatment: a geneticist cures a man's degenerative disease with feline DNA, and the cure slowly turns him into a predatory, cat-like killer. The film combines this idea with an erotic thriller in the style of Basic Instinct, and the scientist's motives are personal as much as professional, since her patient is also her lover. Sources differ slightly on the treatment itself, which Wikipedia describes as injections of feline brain material and other summaries as a genetic implant of cat DNA. According to the Moria review, the genetic angle is barely explored and its main visible effect is Tom's sudden fear of water, a trait the reviewer notes is not genetically determined in cats. As science, the idea that animal genes would transfer behaviour and personality to a human is fantasy; it belongs to a long line of "hybrid monster" stories rather than to real gene therapy.

The story

Tom, a young modern dancer, suffers from a rare degenerative condition of the nervous system. Jacki, a scientist in genetic research who becomes his lover, treats him with an experimental therapy based on genetic material from a cat. The treatment restores his health, but it has side effects: Tom gradually takes on feline traits and becomes devious and dangerous. As he grows attached to Imogen, a fellow dancer, Jacki becomes jealous, fearing she will lose both her patient and her lover. Tom turns on the people around him, killing Jacki and then those who stand between him and Imogen, until a final pursuit in a lumberyard.
