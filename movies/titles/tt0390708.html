Geneshaft

The genetics

This anime series is built around a society organised entirely by genetic engineering, and it explores the consequences of that technology dominating human life. The state controls reproduction and designs every individual for a function, people are ranked by the quality of their DNA, and those of supposedly inferior gene types, such as the main character Mika, are openly mocked. Emotions and family ties are engineered away, and the sex ratio is set by policy on the belief that male genes carry aggression. Some characters are defined by their pride in their "perfect" DNA, while a rebel faction wants to restore humanity's natural order. The series is therefore a dystopia about eugenics and genetic determinism, in which the crew's journey leads them to question the system. The idea that complex traits such as leadership, personality or emotional life could be precisely designed into people goes far beyond real genetics, in which such traits depend on many genes and on environment.

The story

In the late 23rd century, genetic engineering defines human society. A totalitarian world government has kept peace for 200 years by controlling all births and designing each person's genes for their role in society; lifespan is limited to 45 years, family structures have been abolished and love and lust removed. Women outnumber men nine to one, since men are thought to carry irreducibly destructive tendencies, and each man is watched by an emotionless female "Register". Five years after a giant ring-shaped object appears near Earth and devastates a space station and much of the planet's surface, a crew of genetically engineered men and women aboard the spaceship Bilkis sets out to investigate the ring and the alien technology found on Ganymede. Fighting the ring's machines with the giant robot Shaft, the crew meet people and situations that lead them to doubt whether they are as superior as they believe, and how perfect their society really is.
