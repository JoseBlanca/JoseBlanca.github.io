The genetics

Genetic engineering defines the hero and drives much of the conflict. Ed is an 'electro-genetically' enhanced teenager, built by his kindly mentor from alien instructions, whose engineered body lets him bond with alien technology. The villain, Bedlam, covets that genetic design: he wants to collect Ed's DNA to change his own genetic signature or produce his own Ed, and his company, DNA Deliveries, fields armies of unstable, regenerating clone couriers that dissolve into slime when beaten. The series thus sets a benevolent creator, whose creation protects the city, against a corporate villain who wants to exploit genetics for power and profit. Its genetics is cartoon science: clones that regenerate and melt, and a genome that can be 'matched' by stealing someone else's DNA, are pure fantasy.

The story

This animated series is set in the futuristic Progress City. Ed is a genetically engineered teenager who works as a courier for Dojo Deliveries, together with Burn, Deets, Fizz and Loogie, under their mentor Ol' Skool. Ol' Skool created Ed from instructions in an ancient alien artefact, secretly supplied by the Caretaker, a guardian of alien machines who believes Ed is destined to save both Earth and her own planet. Besides delivering packages, the team fights identity theft and data crime, while Ed uncovers his origins and finds alien artefacts that bond with him and give him new abilities. Their enemy is Mr. Bedlam, Ol' Skool's treacherous former partner, a greedy industrialist who wants to control the city, sends armies of regenerating clone couriers and robots after the team, and tries to capture Ed to copy his genetic signature or make his own Ed.
