The genetics

Cloning and hybridisation drive this two-part story. The alien colonists exist as many identical clones, including several women who look exactly like Mulder's missing sister, and they work in clinics using human foetal tissue for a programme to create alien–human hybrids. The episode thus links real reproductive medicine settings with a secret project of genetic manipulation, a recurring theme of The X-Files mythology. The scientists here are the clones themselves, working for an alien agenda and hunted by their own kind for contaminating their race. Scully's contribution is more grounded: she identifies a retrovirus in the alien blood and works out that cold inactivates it, which saves Mulder, and her report credits science for the cure. Retroviruses that insert their genetic material into host cells are real, but the clones, their alien origin and the hybrid programme are fiction.

The story

An American submarine finds a craft under the Arctic ice and is disabled by it. Continuing from "Colony", an alien bounty hunter disguised as Mulder kidnaps Scully and offers to exchange her for Mulder's sister Samantha, who has reappeared. Samantha explains that she and a group of identical doctors are clones descended from two original aliens, who worked in abortion clinics to obtain foetal tissue for experiments aimed at establishing a colony on Earth; because their hybrid experiments were considered to taint their race, the bounty hunter was sent to kill them. The exchange goes wrong and Samantha's body later dissolves into green liquid. In a clinic, Mulder finds several more Samantha clones, who admit that the woman he protected was one of them, before the bounty hunter kills them and burns the building. Mulder pursues him to the stranded submarine, is infected by his toxic blood and is saved by Scully, who learns that it contains a retrovirus inactivated by cold.
