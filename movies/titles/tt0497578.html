The genetics

Genetics drives the second season of this animated superhero series. The teens' patron, the industrialist Janus Lee, turns out to have recruited them in order to take their DNA without their knowledge, and he uses it to create clones powered by animal genes, the 'Mu-Team', which he turns against the original team. Genetic engineering (cloning and the mixing of human and animal DNA) is thus presented as a tool of betrayal and exploitation in the hands of a corporate villain seeking power. The sources mention a character called Dr. Recombo but do not describe any role. The science is cartoon fantasy, in line with many children's action shows of the period that use animal-human hybrids as villains.

The story

In Landmark City, five teenagers, Axel Manning, Catalina Leone, Crey Kingston, Zack Hawkes and Ollie Sharker, win a television contest that is really a front through which Janus Lee, head of Lee Industries, recruits talented adolescents. As the team A.T.O.M. (Alpha Teens On Machines), they receive prototype weapons, gadgets and transforming vehicles to fight the crime boss Alexander Paine, who killed Axel's father, and his henchmen. At the end of the first season they defeat Paine, but learn that Lee's true aim was to harvest their DNA and use it to create clones enhanced with animal DNA. In the second season the teens unite against their former benefactor and his 'Mu-Team', an army of animal-powered mutant clones, while also facing other villains, until they stop Lee.
