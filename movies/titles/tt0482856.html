The genetics

Cloning is the series' premise, played for comedy and aimed at children and young teenagers. The clone is ordered online from a company advertising through spam, made from a fingerprint and a scab, and delivered fully grown in a box, a parody of consumer biotechnology rather than a depiction of real science. The series explores identity through the contrast between two genetically identical boys with opposite personalities: the original is lazy, while the clone is diligent and likeable, implying that sharing a genome does not mean sharing a character. No scientists play a significant role. In reality a clone would be an embryo that develops and grows up like any child, making it a much younger identical twin rather than an instant double of the same age.

The story

Carl², or Carl Squared, is a Canadian animated sitcom that ran for four seasons on Teletoon from 2005 to 2011. Carl Crashman is a lazy 14-year-old whose only talent is slacking. After a bad day, while complaining about his life online, he accidentally orders a clone from a spam e-mail, supplying his fingerprint, a yearbook photo and a scabby bandage. An online cloning company sends him an exact copy of himself in a box, whom he names C2. C2 looks, talks and walks like Carl, though with a higher-pitched voice, but he is ambitious, hard-working and charming. Carl uses him to slack off even more, but C2 often does the opposite of what Carl wants. Carl keeps the clone secret from everyone except his best friend, Jamie James, and the episodes follow the comic complications of sharing one identity.
