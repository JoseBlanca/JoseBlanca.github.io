The genetics

The first part of a two-part story, the episode is built around an alien eugenic and hybridisation project. Reduced to four survivors, the Daleks of the Cult of Skaro conclude that their obsession with purity has driven them to near-extinction and that their race must evolve by merging with humans. They abduct the poor of Depression-era New York, scan their brains to sort them by intelligence, turn the 'low intelligence' ones into half-pig slaves and keep the clever ones for the 'Final Experiment', which Dalek Sec begins on himself by fusing with a human with the help of a 'chromatid solution'. The Doctor, for his part, identifies the organic matter as Dalek tissue from its DNA. The episode draws on Universal horror and The Island of Doctor Moreau, and its hybrid-makers are monsters, though Sec's break with Dalek dogma hints at the ambivalence explored in the second part. The biology is fantasy.

The story

In November 1930 the Doctor and Martha Jones arrive in New York, where people are vanishing from Hooverville, the Central Park shantytown of those ruined by the Depression. With the community's leader, Solomon, and a young man, Frank, they take sewer work offered by the businessman Mr. Diagoras. Underground they find alien organic matter and are attacked by Pig Slaves, half-human, half-pig creatures; Frank is captured. At the Majestic Theatre the showgirl Tallulah is searching for her missing boyfriend Laszlo, whom the Doctor finds half-transformed into a Pig Slave. Laszlo explains that the Daleks sort their captives: those of low intelligence become Pig Slaves, the clever ones are kept for an experiment. In the unfinished Empire State Building, the four surviving Daleks of the Cult of Skaro reveal their plan to merge humans and Daleks, and Dalek Sec fuses with Diagoras to become a human Dalek.
