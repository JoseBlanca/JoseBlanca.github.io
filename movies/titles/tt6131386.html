The genetics

Genetics is the premise of this low-budget family mockbuster of Jurassic Park. Tommy, a schoolboy, brings a dinosaur back to life by genetically editing a bird egg so that it reverts to its dinosaur ancestry (French Wikipedia describes it as cloning), using a home-made device that looks as if it were made of Lego. The young scientist is the hero and the dinosaur becomes his friend; the adults with scientific power, the lab supervisor Dr. Reynolds and government researchers, are greedy antagonists who want to seize the creature. The review notes the 'handwave science' of a child succeeding where funded laboratories fail. Reverting birds towards ancestral dinosaur traits by editing developmental genes is a speculative idea discussed by some researchers, but nothing close to hatching a dinosaur has been achieved.

The story

Twelve-year-old Tommy Harris competes for his school's Young Academic Palaeontologist prize, which offers the winner a project at Vista Tech labs under Dr. Reginald Reynolds. His project is to bring dinosaurs back by genetically editing bird eggs so they revert to their dinosaur ancestry, but his rival Ethan sabotages his presentation, wins the prize and makes Tommy his assistant, leaving him all the work. Tommy continues at home with a home-made device that drips liquid onto a large egg, and hatches a herbivorous dinosaur he calls Spike. He hides the fast-growing creature from his parents, comes to see him as a friend, and his sister Chloe discovers him and paints his claws. On the day of the competition Spike escapes, and Tommy must find him before greedy adults do: Dr. Reynolds wants to claim the dinosaur for himself, and government researchers are also after him. In the end Ethan switches sides to help Tommy.
