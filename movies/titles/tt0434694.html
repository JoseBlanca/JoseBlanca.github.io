The genetics

Genetics is the setting and, in the end, the stake of this mecha war drama. Society is split between Coordinators, humans genetically enhanced before birth, and unmodified Naturals, and the war is driven by resentment and bigotry on both sides, embodied by the anti-Coordinator movement Blue Cosmos. The Earth Alliance also fields "Extended" pilots like Stella Loussier, Naturals raised to Coordinator level by experiments and conditioning that the sources do not describe as genetic. The final arc turns to eugenics: Durandal's Destiny Plan would decide each person's job and place in society by their genes and enforce this with the Requiem superweapon against anyone who resists. The heroes, themselves Coordinators, reject it in the name of free choice, and the series presents genetic determinism imposed from above as the last villainy of the war rather than as a path to peace.

The story

Two years after the war of Mobile Suit Gundam SEED, in Cosmic Era 73, an uneasy peace holds between the Earth Alliance of ordinary humans (Naturals) and the PLANTs, space colonies of genetically enhanced humans (Coordinators). The theft of three new ZAFT mobile suits by Phantom Pain, a unit of the anti-Coordinator movement Blue Cosmos, and the crash of the colony Junius Seven onto Earth by ZAFT extremists reignite the war. The young Coordinator pilot Shinn Asuka, who lost his family in the last war, fights for ZAFT aboard the Minerva, while the veterans Kira Yamato and Athrun Zala are drawn back in. After Blue Cosmos leader Lord Djibril fires the superweapon Requiem at the PLANTs and is killed, PLANT chairman Gilbert Durandal unveils his Destiny Plan, a world order assigning everyone's role by their genes. Kira and Athrun defeat his forces, Durandal is killed by his follower Rey, and the extended finale ends in peace talks.
