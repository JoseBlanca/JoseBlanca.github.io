The genetics

The genetic element is DNA identification: a DNA test reveals that the supposed orphan is the son of a living Cardassian official, which turns a story about war orphans into a custody dispute and leads to a political conspiracy. The episode is mainly about biological versus social parenthood. Rugal is Cardassian by birth but Bajoran by upbringing, and he has absorbed his adoptive people's hatred of his own species. The story gives weight to both sides: it presents Proka's love as genuine but questions an upbringing built on hatred, and it sends Rugal back to his biological father, a decision driven as much by the exposure of Dukat's plot as by blood ties, with the boy going reluctantly. Using DNA to identify a missing child's parents is a standard forensic technique today.

The story

On Deep Space Nine, a Cardassian boy, Rugal, bites the tailor Garak. Rugal is a war orphan left behind when Cardassia withdrew from Bajor, and he has been raised by a Bajoran, Proka, who loves him but has taught him to hate Cardassians. Gul Dukat, the former Cardassian prefect of Bajor, takes up the cause of the orphans and then reports that the boy's DNA has identified him as the son of Kotan Pa'Dar, a prominent Cardassian politician who believed him dead. Pa'Dar comes to claim him, Rugal rejects him as a 'Cardassian butcher', and Commander Sisko presides over a custody hearing. Meanwhile Dr. Bashir and Garak visit a Bajoran resettlement centre and uncover Dukat's plot: he had the boy left behind deliberately to disgrace his rival. Sisko gives custody to the biological father.
