The genetics

Genetic engineering is the premise of this deliberately absurd monster film. The Sharktopus is a cross between a shark and an octopus made in a private laboratory under contract to the Navy, and its creator, Nathan Sands, is a scientist working for military clients who loses control of his creation and then tries to cover up the damage. His daughter Nicole, also a scientist, ends up destroying the animal. The film follows the standard pattern of the creature feature, in which a hybrid made as a weapon escapes and turns on people, and it makes no attempt at realism. Sharks and octopuses are only very distantly related, a vertebrate and a mollusc, and their bodies are built on entirely different plans, so a functional hybrid of the two is impossible; real transgenic animals carry at most a few genes from other species.

The story

The US Navy hires the geneticist Nathan Sands and his daughter Nicole to create a new weapon: S-11, an intelligent shark with the tentacles of an octopus. The creature is controlled by electromagnetic pulses from a device attached to its head, but during a test it discards the device and heads for Mexican waters in search of food, killing the people it meets. Andy Flynn joins Nicole in the hunt for the creature. After a long rampage, Andy shoots S-11 with a device that allows Nicole to hack the chip in its brain; she overloads it, the chip explodes and the creature dies. The Syfy television film was directed by Declan O'Brien and produced by Roger Corman.
