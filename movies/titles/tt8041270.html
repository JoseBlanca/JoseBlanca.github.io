The genetics

Genetic engineering is everywhere in the film. Biosyn has engineered locusts with Cretaceous DNA that spare its own crops, so that it can control the world's food supply, an ecological crisis that director Colin Trevorrow developed after consulting scientists. Maisie is a human clone whose mother also edited her genome to prevent an inherited disease, and the cure for the locust plague comes from a custom-engineered pathogen, released by Henry Wu, that spreads through the swarm. The geneticists are divided: Dodgson is a corporate executive who exploits the science for profit, while Wu, a creator of dinosaurs in earlier films, now seeks redemption by undoing the damage. The film introduces feathered dinosaurs to the series, with the palaeontologist Jack Horner again advising, but its premise remains impossible: DNA degrades over time, and even under the best conditions samples older than about 0.4 to 1.5 million years do not retain enough of it for sequencing; claims of DNA from Cretaceous dinosaur bones in the 1990s may have stemmed from contamination.

The story

Four years after dinosaurs escaped into the wider world, they roam freely, and the company Biosyn Genetics runs a sanctuary in Italy's Dolomites where it conducts genomics research. Owen Grady and Claire Dearing secretly raise Maisie Lockwood, a teenage girl sought for her unique genetic makeup, until she and a baby raptor born by asexual reproduction are kidnapped and taken to Biosyn. Meanwhile, swarms of giant locusts are devastating U.S. crops but spare those grown from Biosyn seeds. Ellie Sattler and Alan Grant find that the locusts were engineered with Cretaceous and modern DNA and, with Ian Malcolm's help, go to Biosyn for proof. There the geneticist Henry Wu, forced by chief executive Lewis Dodgson to work on the locusts, reveals that Maisie is the clone of her mother Charlotte, who altered her DNA to spare her the fatal disease Charlotte herself suffered. The groups escape the burning facility, Dodgson is killed, Wu releases a locust carrying a pathogen that eradicates the swarms, and Biosyn Valley becomes an international sanctuary.
