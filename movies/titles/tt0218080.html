The genetics

The film's genetic element is its weapon, a deadly virus described both as a chemical weapon and as "our darkest military secret", carried on a submarine for research and seized by terrorists who plan to release it on American soil. The story

US special forces officer Captain Matt Hendricks and the virologist Dr. Linda Christian are aboard a submarine carrying a deadly virus, Agent Red, being transported for research, when they are trapped with a group of Russian terrorists who seize the boat. The terrorists intend to launch the virus, for which no antidote exists and which could kill millions, against the United States, and the two must stop them. The available sources say little more about the plot or its ending. The film had a troubled production: judging the original version unreleasable, producer Andrew Stevens had the script rewritten and some 40 minutes reshot in three days by Jim Wynorski, padding it with footage from the 1998 film Counter Measures.
