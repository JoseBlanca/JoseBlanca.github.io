The genetics

Genetics appears only through the premise of a cloned hero: the protagonist is a clone of Donald Trump, described as Chinese and kept in a maximum-security laboratory until he escapes. The film says nothing about who made him, how or why, and the cloning plays no further role in a plot driven by prophecy, aliens and a battle with Satan. This adult animated comedy, widely panned by critics as a low-effort far-right fantasy, uses cloning merely as a device to place its hero in the far future.

The story

In 2044, artificially intelligent robots have drained the Earth of its resources, and humanity flees into space. A Chinese-made clone of Donald Trump, the 45th and 47th President, escapes from the maximum-security laboratory where he was held and stows away on the last shuttle off the planet. His craft crashes on Mars, where he is stranded alone for 999 years, joyriding in a Mars rover, while the rest of humanity wars with an evil alien race, the Illuminati. Learning that he is prophesied to destroy the Illuminati, the clone joins the surviving humans and takes the fight to the Illuminati's headquarters in Hell, where he must battle Satan himself.
