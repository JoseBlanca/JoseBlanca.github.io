The genetics

Genetics appears in a single strand of the finale: Lex Luthor's survival. Lex has kept himself alive by harvesting vital parts from his own clones and grafting them onto his body, which makes cloning the means of the arch-villain's return. The clones are treated as spare parts, a sinister use that fits Lex's role as the series' antagonist; his return also involves his father's bargain with Darkseid. The episode is otherwise about Clark accepting his destiny as Superman and defeating Darkseid, and the source gives no detail about how the clones were made. Growing a clone as a source of organs is a long-standing science-fiction idea; in reality a clone would be a separate person, and organ replacement research focuses on growing tissues, not whole copies.

The story

In the two-part series finale of Smallville, Clark Kent and Lois Lane prepare to marry as the planet Apokolips descends towards Earth. Tess Mercer discovers that Oliver Queen is possessed by Darkseid and is captured by Granny Goodness. Meanwhile, the Lionel Luthor of a parallel Earth tries to bring back his dead counterpart's son, Lex, who has been surviving in the ruins of the Luthor mansion by grafting vital parts from his clones onto his own body; Lionel plans to use Tess's heart, and after she shoots him he makes a deal with Darkseid to restore Lex. At the wedding, the possessed Oliver tries to strip Clark's powers with a gold kryptonite ring, but Clark frees him. Lex reveals he always knew Tess was his sister and stabs her; dying, she poisons him with a neurotoxin that erases his memories. Clark finally flies, defeats Darkseid and pushes Apokolips away. Seven years later Lex is President and Clark is Superman.
