The genetics

Genetics is one strand of this dense mystical science-fiction film. Its most direct genetic element is cloning: Rei Ayanami turns out to be one of the "Ayanami Series", copies of Shinji's mother Yui made by NERV, which is why this Rei has no memory of Shinji and only follows orders; the revelation breaks him. Gendo Ikari's larger plan, shared in rival form with the shadowy SEELE, is to force humanity's evolution through a mass extinction and reunite with his wife, and the pilots are stuck in an unaging state the film calls the "Curse of Eva". Science in the film is a tool of secretive organisations that manipulate and use people, especially children. The cloning and the evolutionary "Impacts" are fantastical, entangled with angels and religious symbolism rather than real biology.

The story

Fourteen years after the Near Third Impact, Shinji Ikari is recovered from orbit inside Evangelion Unit-01 by WILLE, the anti-NERV organisation led by Misato Katsuragi. He learns that his earlier awakening caused a global catastrophe, that the Eva pilots have not aged, and that WILLE has fitted him with an explosive collar to stop him piloting again. Feeling betrayed, he goes with Rei Ayanami to the ruins of NERV, where his father Gendo wants him to pilot the new Unit-13 with the boy Kaworu Nagisa. Shinji bonds with Kaworu, but Kozo Fuyutsuki reveals that his mother Yui is sealed inside Unit-01 and that Rei is one of a series of clones of Yui. Hoping to undo the catastrophe, Shinji and Kaworu take Unit-13 to the depths of NERV, but pulling out the spears there starts a Fourth Impact, halted only when Kaworu lets the collar kill him. Asuka drags the broken Shinji away across a red desert.
