The genetics

Genetic engineering drives the episode, the first part of a two-part story. The Klingon Empire, alarmed by the genetically enhanced human "Augments" seen earlier in the season, has tried to create its own super-soldiers using Augment DNA, and the experiment has turned into a plague. Phlox, a doctor kidnapped to fix the problem, refuses to help produce more augmented Klingons, while the Klingon scientist Antaak is a reluctant participant in a military programme. The plague's symptoms include the loss of the Klingons' forehead ridges, and the story was designed to explain in-universe why Klingons looked human in the original series of the 1960s and had ridges from the 1979 film onwards. Critics welcomed the link to the Augments. Scientifically, the idea of a virus spreading another species' engineered genes and reshaping the anatomy of adults is fantasy, although viral vectors are used in real gene therapy to deliver genes into cells.

The story

As Enterprise returns to Earth and Trip Tucker transfers to the new starship Columbia, Doctor Phlox is kidnapped in San Francisco. Following clues that point to Rigelian attackers, Enterprise gives chase, while Lieutenant Reed is secretly given orders by an agent of a covert organisation he seems to know. Enterprise is attacked and boarded, and a captured intruder who looks human turns out to be a Klingon; saboteurs force the ship to flee at record speed to avoid a warp core breach. Meanwhile, Phlox is taken to a Klingon colony, where General K'Vagh and Doctor Antaak demand that he cure a plague. Examining a victim, Phlox finds that his DNA has been combined with that of genetically augmented humans: the Klingons had experimented with Augment DNA, but it mutated and spread as a plague. Given five days to stop the outbreak, Antaak proposes creating stable augmented Klingons, but Phlox refuses to help. The story continues in "Divergence".
