The genetics

The episode is about the consequences of human genetic enhancement. It continues "Unnatural Selection", in which parents buy black-market genetic improvements for their unborn children at the risk of Genetic Rejection Syndrome, a fictional condition that causes deformities, violence and psychosis. Here the generation of failed enhancements has grown up, and society deals with it by hiding the children away and hunting the adults. The story questions both the enhancement industry and the parents who abandoned their children, and adds a twist on nature versus nurture: Dylan insists that his rage was not caused by his genes or the serum, while Ray, once transformed, becomes a monster in his family's eyes. No scientist appears prominently. GRS is invented, but the episode reflects a real concern about heritable genetic modification: early experiments editing human embryos produced unintended mutations, and safety is central to the debate.

The story

Years after the events of "Unnatural Selection", genetic enhancement of babies is common but flawed, and children affected by Genetic Rejection Syndrome (GRS) have grown into deformed, violent adults who commit murders. Detective Ray Venable leads a team hunting them. He and his wife Marie once had a son with GRS, Dylan, whom they handed to an agency that discreetly locks such children away without telling parents what becomes of them. Now Dylan is harassing the family. Ray injects himself with a serum that temporarily gives him the heightened senses of GRS sufferers so that he can track his son, only to discover that Dylan planted the serum for him to find. Dylan forcibly injects him with more, making the change irreversible, and tells him that his violence was never caused by the drug. Ray kills Dylan, but his family now fears him.
