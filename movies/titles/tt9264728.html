The genetics

The villain is introduced as an aquatic geneticist who claims to help creatures adapt to changing environments, and his real project is to breed a new aquatic form of life using human women: he marks them with fertility symbols, impregnates them, keeps them in water tanks and transforms them, while several identical copies of him suggest cloning or a hive. The film is a horror story about a predatory partner, and his scientific profession serves as a mask for sexual menace and bodily violation. The genetic mechanism is never explained (parasites and a glowing regurgitated substance play a role), and the ending, in which the heroine smiles as she breathes underwater, presents her transformation as a disturbing loss of self rather than a happy outcome. The science is pure fantasy.

The story

Sixteen-year-old Libby Wells returns from summer camp to her family's lake house and meets her romance-novelist mother Michelle's new boyfriend, John Smith, an 'aquatic geneticist' who says he helps creatures adapt to changing environments and gives Libby a bracelet with fertility symbols. John behaves in increasingly predatory ways towards Libby, and she finds that he keeps parasites in the basement and walks into the glowing lake at night. Michelle, who has accepted his proposal, falls ill and finds she is pregnant; she refuses to believe Libby's accusations. In the basement Libby finds her mother in a water tank, giving birth to a dying creature, and her friend Marley's body being eaten by parasites. Captured, Libby sees several men identical to John and rows of women in tanks. John forces her to swallow a glowing substance, and she wakes in a filling tank, smiling as she discovers she can breathe underwater.
