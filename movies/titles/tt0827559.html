The genetics

This puppet adventure is the only Captain Scarlet episode built around biological warfare. Its threat is K14, a synthetic virus created in a British microbiology laboratory, described as the deadliest ever developed and capable of killing up to ten million people. The scientist Dr. Denton is shown as a legitimate researcher whose creation is stolen: he raises the alarm and briefs Spectrum on the danger. The theft is the work of the Mysterons, who kill his assistant, reconstruct her and send the copy to release the virus into the water supply of Los Angeles, and Spectrum's agents run the pursuit, decontamination and quarantine. The lab's purpose for the virus is never explained, but the episode makes its existence the danger: a man-made pathogen in a vial becomes a weapon of mass destruction once it leaves the laboratory. Synthetic viruses were speculative in 1968; the episode reflects Cold War fears of biological weapons.

The story

In 2068, Earth's security organization Spectrum wages a 'war of nerves' against the Mysterons, Martians who can recreate people and objects they have destroyed and use the copies as agents. At a microbiological laboratory near Manchester, Dr. Denton and his assistant, Judy Chapman, activate a culture of K14, the most lethal synthetic virus ever developed, able to kill millions. Driving home, Chapman is killed in a crash set up by the Mysteron agent Captain Black, and the Mysterons reconstruct her. The next morning the replica kills a security guard, steals the K14 vial and flies to New York, fulfilling the Mysterons' threat to destroy the 'Place of the Angels'. Captains Scarlet and Blue pursue her, but she fools them with an apparently broken vial and they are briefly quarantined. Realizing that the target is Los Angeles, Scarlet catches her at the Boulder Dam as she prepares to poison the city's water supply; he shoots her and recovers the vial intact.
