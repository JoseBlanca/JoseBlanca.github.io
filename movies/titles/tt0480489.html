The genetics

Elfen Lied uses a mutation as the basis of its conflict between two species. The Diclonii are a new branch of humanity that could replace their parent species: a Diclonius's vectors can transmit a "vector virus" to human men, whose children are then born as Diclonii, so the mutation spreads by infection rather than ordinary inheritance. The scientists who run the research facility hold Diclonii captive, experiment on them and treat them as a threat to be contained, and they take drastic measures against the virus's spread: one researcher, Kurama, whose daughter Mariko was born a Diclonius after he was touched by a Diclonius's vectors, urges a colleague to be sterilised. A central question of the series is whether the Diclonii's violence is part of their genetic code or the result of abuse by humans, and the anime's director, Mamoru Kanbe, said he intended it to show how atrocities are shaped by the way people are treated. In the manga, the Diclonii are kept in a cave named after the Nazi Lebensborn programme. A virus that turns a man's future children into another species has no real counterpart.

The story

This anime series, adapted from Lynn Okamoto's manga, follows the Diclonii, a newly mutated species that looks human apart from two small horns but has "vectors", invisible telekinetic arms that can cut through anything within reach. Lucy, a teenage Diclonius held in a secret research facility off the coast of Kamakura, escapes in a massacre but is wounded in the head and develops a second, childlike personality called Nyu, who can say little more than her name. She is taken in by two students, Kouta and his cousin Yuka, who know nothing of her past. The facility repeatedly tries to recapture her, sending a special assault team and other Diclonii, including Mariko, the daughter of one of its researchers. Flashbacks gradually reveal how Lucy was rejected by humans as a child, and that her meeting with Kouta, who has lost his childhood memories, is not a coincidence. The 13-episode anime ended while the manga was still running, so its ending differs from the manga's.
