The genetics

The genetic element is a biological weapon: Dr. Saxon develops a deadly virus in his laboratories, uses Sean as a human test subject and plans to sell or deploy it. The English plot summary also says Saxon commands an army of genetically engineered ninja clones led by Chan Lee, although the German summary describes ordinary ninjas and does not mention cloning, so how much the film makes of engineering and cloning is uncertain. Saxon is a stock terrorist scientist, a pure villain who orders the execution of his own agent and is killed by the hero. The science is perfunctory: Sean finally recovers from the virus through "mental strength" rather than the antidote, and the laboratory is mainly a setting for martial-arts fights.

The story

Sean Davidson, whose father was murdered by gangsters in 1979 and who was raised and trained in ninjutsu in Japan, travels to a karate tournament on the Caribbean island of Triana with his friends Curtis Jackson and Dexter. The tournament's host, General Andreas, is his father's killer. Andreas is working with Dr. Saxon, a terrorist known as "The Cobra", who has developed a deadly virus as a biological weapon and wants to test it on the best fighter. Saxon's agent, the female ninja Chan Lee, lures Sean into a trap; he is captured at Saxon's laboratories, which claim to make medicines, and injected with the virus. His friends rescue him, Sean kills Andreas, and in a final assault on the lab Chan Lee, who has switched sides, is killed. Sean overcomes the virus and kills Saxon, and the army storms the laboratory.
