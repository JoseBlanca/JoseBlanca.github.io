The genetics

Genetics provides the scientific frame of the series. The abilities are treated as a biological phenomenon, and a research geneticist, Mohinder Suresh, is one of the main characters; he follows his father's work, studies the source of the powers and later develops a cure for a new strain of the Shanti virus using the blood of Claire, a girl who can regenerate. Scientists are shown in several roles: as independent researchers, as employees of the secretive Company, which studies and contains people with abilities and weaponises the Shanti virus, and as part of Pinehearst's project to create a formula that gives powers to anyone. Mohinder's self-experiment with an incomplete formula gives him strength but also makes his body mutate, a classic warning about scientists who test on themselves. Arthur Petrelli presents the formula as a way to improve the world, while it is pursued for power. Like most superhero fiction, the series uses the language of genes and evolution loosely; no real genetic variant confers anything like these abilities.

The story

Heroes follows ordinary people around the world who discover that they have superhuman abilities and must work together to prevent catastrophic futures. In the first season, the geneticist Mohinder Suresh continues his late father's research into the biological source of the abilities, including a list of people who have them, while Noah Bennet works for "the Company", a secret organisation that tracks, controls and, if necessary, eliminates people with powers. The second season deals with the Company's research on the Shanti virus, whose strains affect both people with abilities and ordinary humans, and the threat of a global pandemic from a weaponised strain. In the third, Arthur Petrelli and his rival company Pinehearst seek a formula that would give ordinary people abilities, and Mohinder injects himself with an early version. The fourth season introduces a carnival whose leader gathers people with abilities to increase his own power. The series ran on NBC for four seasons, from 2006 to 2010.
