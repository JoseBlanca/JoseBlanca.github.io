The genetics

Genetics appears in one celebrated sequence. Government scientists recover a severed, armoured hand from the wreck of the Mondoshawan ship and use biotechnology to rebuild its owner: reviews describe them reading a DNA far more complex than human DNA and constructing a 'supreme genetic being', and the Italian Wikipedia calls the process accelerated cloning. The result is Leeloo, the Fifth Element, a perfect life form whose strength and powers save the Earth. The scientists are anonymous, competent government staff, and their work is the step that makes humanity's survival possible; the newly made Leeloo is frightened and confused, but no harm comes from the reconstruction itself. The rest of the film's science, from the healing tank to the Divine Light, is fantasy. Regenerating a whole adult body from a hand's DNA in minutes is pure invention, though it echoes the idea that a complete organism can be recreated from its genome.

The story

In 1914 the alien Mondoshawans remove from an Egyptian temple the only weapon able to stop a 'Great Evil' that returns every 5,000 years: four stones representing the classical elements and a sarcophagus holding a fifth element, a perfect being. In the 23rd century the Evil approaches Earth as a giant fireball, and the Mondoshawan ship bringing back the weapon is destroyed by Mangalore mercenaries hired by the industrialist Zorg. From a single surviving hand, Earth scientists rebuild the sarcophagus' occupant, a young woman named Leeloo, who escapes and lands in the taxi of former soldier Korben Dallas. With the priest Vito Cornelius, Dallas and Leeloo recover the stones from the opera diva Plavalaguna on the luxury liner Fhloston Paradise, survive Zorg and the Mangalores, and reach the temple. Disheartened by humanity's violence, Leeloo hesitates, but Dallas's declaration of love moves her to unleash the Divine Light that defeats the Evil.
