The genetics

The villains' project is genetic: Delta Tech pays a university laboratory, run by a scientist named Dr. Phelps, to implant insect DNA into students and so create a human hybrid able to survive a nuclear holocaust. In the film this somehow turns the students into near-invincible cyborgs, the 'mercenary cyborgs' of the brief Wikipedia synopsis, mixing genetic engineering with robotics; the company's android Adam is a purely mechanical creation. The corporation and its scientist are straightforward villains who experiment on young people and kill witnesses. Reviewers found the premise nonsensical, and indeed inserting insect genes would not make a person radiation-proof, let alone part machine. The available sources are thin and do not describe the ending.

The story

Television journalist Evelyn Reed investigates the Delta Tech corporation and is smuggled into a university research wing that it funds. She finds that the company is implanting insect DNA into students in the hope of creating a hybrid able to survive atomic radiation, and she sees Adam, an indestructible artificial soldier built for military use. After she films Adam in action, Delta Tech's thugs shoot her cameraman and she flees. Two federal marshals, Max Colley and Saint Sebastian, who were on their way to an assignment for Delta Tech, take her side, and the company sends Adam to eliminate all three. The brief available synopses say that a scientist is turning students into mercenary cyborgs and that the heroes set out to stop him, but they give few details of the ending.
