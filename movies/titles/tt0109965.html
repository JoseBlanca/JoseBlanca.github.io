The genetics

Genetics is only a light background element. The Guyver armour and the Zoanoids are the products of alien biological engineering: in the backstory, aliens tried to turn primitive humans into living weapons, and the Cronos Corporation now uses Zoanoids, humans transformed into monsters, as its henchmen. The film presents the armour as an alien bio-booster unit bonded to its host rather than in explicit genetic terms, and the sources do not say how the Zoanoids are made, so the genetic framing is thin. The idea of humans engineered into soldiers by outsiders, and of a corporation exploiting the technology, is a familiar theme of the Japanese manga on which the film is based.

The story

A year after bonding with the Guyver, an alien bio-booster armour, and destroying the Los Angeles branch of the Cronos Corporation, Sean Barker fights crime but is worn down by the unit's urge to kill. Drawn by cave paintings in Utah that match his dreams, he travels to an archaeological dig led by Marcus Edwards, whose daughter Cori guides him there. The dig uncovers an ancient alien ship. Arlen Crane, the dig's funder, turns out to work for Cronos, which is still active, and is himself a Zoanoid, one of the monstrous transformed humans that serve the corporation. Through the ship, Sean learns that in prehistoric times aliens created the Guyvers and Zoanoids in a failed attempt to turn early humans into soldiers. Crane fuses with a damaged Guyver unit, but Sean kills him, sends the ship home and refuses a government offer to fight Cronos.
