The genetics

Genetics is a passing element. The Republic's army consists of clone troopers, clones of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, who fight alongside the Jedi throughout the series, but the series does not deal with how they were made or what being a clone means. In the second volume, the Separatist Techno Union is shown to have enslaved and mutated the Nelvaanians, whom Anakin and Obi-Wan free; the sources do not say how the mutation was done. The series is about battles and Anakin's path towards the dark side, not about genetics.

The story

This animated series is set between the films Attack of the Clones and Revenge of the Sith and follows the Galactic Republic and the Jedi in their war against the Separatists and the Sith. Its first volume covers Obi-Wan Kenobi's assault on Muunilinst, home of the Banking Clan that funds the Separatists and hires the bounty hunter Durge; Count Dooku's new apprentice Asajj Ventress, sent to kill Anakin Skywalker, who defeats her on Yavin 4; and battles fought by Mace Windu, Yoda, Kit Fisto and others, including the first encounter with General Grievous. The second volume sees Anakin knighted and jumps to the end of the war, when Anakin and Obi-Wan free the Nelvaanians, who had been enslaved and mutated by the Separatist Techno Union, and Anakin has a vision of becoming Darth Vader. Grievous attacks Coruscant and kidnaps Chancellor Palpatine, leading into Revenge of the Sith.
