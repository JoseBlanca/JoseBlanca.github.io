The genetics

The episode concludes an arc about the Augments, genetically enhanced humans in Star Trek's history. Klingon scientists tried to create their own super-soldiers by adding Augment DNA to Klingons, but the modified genome turned into a contagious virus that threatens the whole species. Phlox, who refuses to build "living weapons", works to switch off the virus rather than perfect the Augments, and the cure leaves the survivors with smooth foreheads. The episodes were written to explain why Klingons look human in the original 1960s series but have ridged foreheads in later productions. Scientists appear on both sides: Antaak, who blames himself for the outbreak, and Phlox, a doctor with firm ethics; the experiment itself was ordered by the military and tested on prisoners and on a unit of soldiers. A virus that spreads a genetic modification evokes real viral vectors used in gene therapy, but the idea that such changes could sweep through a population as an epidemic is fiction.

The story

In this second part of a two-part story, Doctor Phlox is a prisoner at a Klingon colony, forced to work with the Klingon physician Antaak on a cure for a plague. The disease began as an attempt to create Klingon "Augments" using the DNA of genetically enhanced humans; the altered DNA mutated, spread as a virus and is killing its victims after stripping them of their cranial ridges. Meanwhile, Commander Tucker joins Enterprise from Columbia to purge the sabotage that keeps the ship at dangerous speed, and Captain Archer learns that Lieutenant Reed has been working for Section 31. As a Klingon admiral prepares to destroy the colony to contain the outbreak, Enterprise and Columbia arrive. Phlox infects Archer to obtain the human antibodies needed for the cure, and Antaak beams the virus onto the admiral's ship, forcing him to accept the cure. The Klingon High Council distributes it, but the cured keep their human-like appearance.
