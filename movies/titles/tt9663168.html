The genetics

The film fuses religious horror with a biotechnology plot. The villains run a biotech firm that is a front for a satanic cult: they steal the Shroud of Turin, extract what they take to be Christ's DNA, and implant it in an abducted woman to produce a child who will serve as Lucifer's body on Earth, after testing several captive women for compatibility. The genetics serves as a modern means to an apocalyptic end, and the scientists and executives involved are cultists who murder and kidnap, so biotechnology appears as the tool of evil, although the film's twist is that the child is part of God's own plan to destroy Lucifer. Critics compared it to Rosemary's Baby, The Da Vinci Code and The Boys from Brazil. Recovering usable DNA from the Shroud of Turin, let alone cloning a person from it, is not possible; the film treats it as a premise, not as science.

The story

After Lucifer's rebellion, the archangel Michael chains him in Hell. In the present day, a biotech company that is secretly a Lucifer-worshipping cult, led by Liz, kills Father Marconi and steals the Shroud of Turin, intending to use it to create a vessel for Lucifer. Michael takes possession of Marconi's body to stop them. The cult abducts several women, among them the researcher Laura Milton, to find a suitable mother; Lucifer, freed, possesses one and kills the others, and Laura is impregnated with a child created from the Shroud's DNA. She tries to kill herself and the fetus, without success, and gives birth. Michael reveals that God plans to use the child to destroy Lucifer; Liz turns on her followers to protect the baby and has the cult massacred. Michael closes the portal to Hell by blowing up the facility, Laura escapes with the child, who is taken to the Vatican, and in the final scene Lucifer's essence enters the baby.
