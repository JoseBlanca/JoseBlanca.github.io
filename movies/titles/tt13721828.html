The genetics

The film continues the Witch series, built on a secret project that creates superhuman assassins by genetic manipulation (Japanese Wikipedia calls Ark 1 a subject of a gene-manipulation project). The institute's scientists, led by its founder Dr. Baek and its current head Jang, abducted a pregnant girl for her twins, implanted devices in Ark 1, keep the mother wired in a tank and sell doses of 'power' to a gangster; when their subject escapes, they send tactical teams to eliminate her. The film presents the institute as a ruthless organisation whose experiments produce beings of uncontrollable violence, and ends by setting up a reckoning between the sisters, their mother and the project. Its psychic and telekinetic powers have no basis in genetics.

The story

Years earlier, a pregnant teenager was abducted in a faked bus accident by Dr. Baek's secret institute, which took a mysterious interest in the fraternal twins she was carrying. In the present, a girl called Ark 1 escapes from the institute's laboratory on Jeju Island after killing the soldiers and scientists inside. She is picked up by henchmen of the gangster Yong-doo along with Kyung-hee, a young woman he is trying to force to sign over her late father's land. Kyung-hee takes the girl to her uncle, a former doctor, who removes experimental devices from her skin, and then home to her younger brother Dae-gil. The institute's head, Jang, and the agent Jo-hyeon send forces after Ark 1, and Yong-doo trades her location for a dose of the institute's power. In the assault Kyung-hee and Dae-gil are killed and Ark 1 slaughters her attackers. Goo Ja-yoon arrives, reveals that she is Ark 1's sister and takes her to find their mother, who lies wired inside a huge tank.
