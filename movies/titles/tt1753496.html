The genetics

The film is about bio-organic weapons from the Resident Evil franchise used in a civil war. Its key element is the Las Plagas parasite: a human carrying a dominant Plaga can command creatures created with the Progenitor virus or the T-virus, such as Lickers, while people infected with subordinate Plagas become controllable Ganados. President Svetlana Belikova mass-produces the parasites in a bunker built in an old nuclear silo, carries an enhancing Plaga herself, supplies the weapons to the rebels so that their use will justify her crackdown and win her foreign support, and sends monstrous Tyrants into battle. For the rebels, the parasites are a desperate weapon that consumes their hosts, and the sympathetic Buddy is saved only at the cost of his legs. The film presents these biological weapons as tools of political manipulation, coveted by every side, including the unnamed employer of the spy Ada Wong. Parasites and viruses with such powers are fantasy.

The story

In this computer-animated Resident Evil film, US agent Leon S. Kennedy enters the Eastern Slav Republic, a former Soviet state at civil war, to investigate reports that bio-organic weapons are being used. Captured by rebels, he discovers that their elderly leader carries a dominant Las Plagas parasite that lets him control monstrous Lickers. When the leader succumbs to the parasite, the former teacher Buddy, who hates the government for bombing his school and killing his fiancée and pupils, takes a new parasite to command the creatures. The spy Ada Wong, posing as an agent of a bioterrorism alliance, is in the country to steal a Plaga sample. President Svetlana Belikova, who produces the parasites in a bunker and has supplied them to the rebels to justify crushing them, unleashes Tyrants against Leon and Buddy. A Russian-American intervention ends the war; Svetlana resigns, and Leon frees Buddy from the parasite by shooting his spine, leaving him paralysed.
