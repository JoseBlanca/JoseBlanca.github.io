The genetics

The episode centres on a genetics company and on human experimentation. Goldman presents his work as research on children with real genetic disorders such as Proteus syndrome, Crouzon syndrome, ichthyosis and Marfan syndrome, but the investigation reveals that he has been experimenting on pregnant women, including his own wife, producing children with telekinetic and telepathic powers whom he keeps in his facility. Mulder and Scully suspect a connection to the alien-human hybrid programme of the series' mythology, and the episode links the story to their own son, William. Goldman is the classic unethical scientist: protected by Defense Department ties, he treats his own children and his patients as subjects, and is killed by them. Real genetic conditions are mentioned accurately as conditions that cause physical differences, but the powers attributed to Goldman's subjects are fictional. The episode raises concerns about research on pregnant women and children without consent.

The story

At the headquarters of Nugenics Technology, the researcher Dr. Sanjay, tormented by a high-pitched noise in his head, deletes data and kills himself, after writing "founder's mutation" on his hand. Mulder and Scully learn that Sanjay was worried that his "kids" were dying and find photographs of severely deformed children in his apartment. The company's owner, Dr. Augustus Goldman, known as "the Founder", runs a hospital facility for children with genetic conditions, where a patient makes objects move on their own. A pregnant woman who told the agents her baby was abnormal is killed and her fetus disappears. Goldman's wife, confined in an asylum, says he experimented on her during pregnancy and that she cut her baby out of her womb after it began communicating with her telepathically. The agents trace the source of the noise to a young janitor at Nugenics, Kyle, who wants to meet his sister. Taken to Goldman's lab, Kyle finds his real sister, Molly, and together they kill Goldman and escape.
