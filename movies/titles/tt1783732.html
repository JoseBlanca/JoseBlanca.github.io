John Dies at the End

The genetics

Genetics appears only in passing. In the alternate Earth that David and John visit, the history that diverged from ours includes genetic experiments and the creation of animal-human hybrids, shown in a joke image of the Wright brothers flying a hybrid flying pig, and the god-like Korrok is described as a biological supercomputer. These details colour the absurd alternate world but play no part in the plot, which is driven by a hallucinogenic drug and supernatural forces.

The story

David Wong tells a sceptical reporter how he and his friend John were drawn into a supernatural war. After a party, John takes Soy Sauce, a black drug sold by a dealer named Robert Marley that gives users inhuman knowledge and throws them across dimensions and time; David injects himself by accident. Most of the people at Marley's party die or disappear, John apparently dies during questioning by the police and keeps contacting David telepathically, and creatures that only drug users can see begin to attack. With their friend Amy and her dog Bark Lee, the two pass through a ghostly door to an alternate Earth ruled by Korrok, a monstrous biological supercomputer worshipped as a god, which wants to devour them to learn how to travel between worlds. Bark Lee destroys Korrok with a bomb given to them by the psychic Albert Marconi, and David and John become reluctant demon hunters.
