The genetics

Genetic enhancement is the foundation of the series' world. The war between Naturals and Coordinators is a conflict born of genetic inequality: Coordinators are engineered to be stronger and more intelligent, Naturals resent them, and the natural-human supremacist group Blue Cosmos campaigns, with the slogan "for the preservation of our blue and pure world", for their destruction, while radicals on the PLANTs plan genocide in return. Behind the story is GARM R&D, a research company on the Mendel colony. Its chief researcher, Dr. Ulen Hibiki, developed Kira as the Ultimate Coordinator, a foetus grown outside the womb with enhancements intended to surpass all other Coordinators; Kira was the only one to survive the process. Hibiki also cloned the wealthy Al Da Flaga, and the clone, Rau Le Creuset, has short telomeres that make him age rapidly, which feeds his hatred of humanity. The series uses these ideas to explore prejudice and the ethics of designing people; its sequel, Gundam SEED Destiny, extends them to a plan to assign everyone's role in life by their genes.

How genetics is portrayed

Overall portrayal of genetics
negative The series sympathises with Coordinators as victims of prejudice, but sympathy for the products is not approval of the science: designing people creates the inequality behind the war, and its secret projects produce Rau's nihilism. The peace comes from people rejecting hatred, not from genetics.
Outcome of the genetic intervention
negative Genetic enhancement splits humanity into Naturals and Coordinators, feeding hate crimes, a nuclear war and rival genocide plans; Rau's cloning with short telomeres drives his hatred of humanity, and the Ultimate Coordinator project left failed subjects. Kira's success does not offset this.
Dr. Ulen Hibiki (GARM R&D, Mendel colony)
ambivalent Aim: curiosity (neutral). In backstory he ran the Ultimate Coordinator project, producing Kira as its sole survivor and Canard as a 'failed experiment', and cloned Al Da Flaga, producing the rapidly ageing Rau Le Creuset; his work yields both the hero and the nihilistic villain, and the material says little about how he himself is staged.
Coordinator gene-engineers (prenatal enhancement in society)
neutral Aim: personal (neutral). Prenatal enhancement that gives Coordinators superior intellect and strength is routine background in the Cosmic Era, done for parents who want better children; no specific practitioner is staged.

The story

In the Cosmic Era, humanity is divided between Naturals and Coordinators, people genetically enhanced before birth. Persecuted on Earth, most Coordinators have emigrated to space colonies called the PLANTs, and the two sides are at war. When the PLANTs' forces, ZAFT, attack the neutral space colony Heliopolis to seize prototype mobile suits built for the Earth Alliance, Kira Yamato, a young Coordinator student, pilots one of them to protect his friends, and finds himself fighting his childhood friend Athrun Zala, now a ZAFT pilot. Kira joins the crew of the warship Archangel and later, with the singer Lacus Clyne and other defectors, forms a third force that tries to stop both sides from destroying each other. Along the way he learns that he is the "Ultimate Coordinator", the product of a secret research project, and that the masked ZAFT commander Rau Le Creuset is a clone. The 50-episode series aired in Japan in 2002–2003.
