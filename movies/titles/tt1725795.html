The genetics

Genetics provides the premise for this Tamil science fiction action film. A genetics student believes that a historical figure's skills can be reawakened in a living descendant by matching their DNA, a version of the idea of "genetic memory", and the genetics professor is shown as a corrupt scientist who collaborates with a foreign bioweapons plot. The threat that drives the story is a biological attack with an ancient virus, the same strain Bodhidharma once cured, rather than an engineered one. The film links genetics with national pride, presenting ancient Indian knowledge as the answer to modern science. The notion that abilities or memories acquired by an ancestor can be inherited and reactivated through DNA has no scientific basis; learned skills are not encoded in the genome. Commentators compared the premise to the Assassin's Creed video games, and a critic in The Hindu noted that the telepathy and hypnotism were "comic book stuff". The film was also criticised for historical inaccuracies about Bodhidharma.

The story

The film opens with Bodhidharma, a Pallava prince skilled in martial arts and medicine, who travels to China, cures an epidemic and teaches the villagers medicine, hypnotism and the training that gave rise to Shaolin kung fu, before agreeing to die there. In the present day, the Chinese government sends the mercenary Dong Lee to Chennai to start "Operation Red", a biological war against India using the same virus Bodhidharma once cured. Meanwhile, Subha Srinivasan, a genetics student, believes Bodhidharma's abilities can be revived in a descendant whose DNA matches his, and finds a match in Aravind, a circus performer. Her professor rejects her idea and turns out to be secretly helping Lee for money. Lee hunts down Subha, Aravind and their friends and kills several of them. During the final fight, Aravind awakens Bodhidharma's powers, kills Lee and cures the disease with an ancient remedy.
