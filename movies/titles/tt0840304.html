The genetics

Ice Spiders is a low-budget creature feature in which genetic experimentation by the military supplies the monsters. Dr. Sommers' original aim is to obtain large amounts of spider silk for new types of armour, a project that is presented as legitimate, but Professor Marks secretly pushes the experiment further, accelerating the spiders' growth to make them bigger and stronger. The result is a breed of giant, cold-resistant black widows that escape and kill, and the film follows the familiar pattern of an ambitious scientist working with the military who refuses to destroy his creation and is killed by it, while the government covers everything up. The biology is not explained. The film does start from a real line of research: spider silk is exceptionally strong, and because spiders cannot be farmed like silkworms, scientists have produced silk proteins in genetically modified organisms, such as goats that secrete them in their milk, with applications that include ballistic armour.

The story

In a secret laboratory on a Utah mountain, Dr. April Sommers and other scientists are working for the army on a new breed of giant spiders. When the spiders escape, they kill most of the lab staff and head for the ski resort on the other side of the mountain, where a team of teenage skiers is training. Sommers teams up with Dash, a former Olympic skier who works at the resort, as the spiders kill hunters, a park ranger and guests at the lodge. Reading the experiment's records, Sommers realises that Professor Marks deliberately accelerated the spiders' growth, making them larger and stronger, insatiably hungry and resistant to the cold. With the help of an army captain, Dash lures the spiders into a snowboard half-pipe, where they are blown up with an avalanche cannon; Marks, who tried to protect them, is killed by the last one. The government then covers up the incident.
