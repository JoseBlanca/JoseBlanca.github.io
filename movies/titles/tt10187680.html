The genetics

DNA analysis runs through this Malayalam forensic thriller. A volunteer ex-police officer's DNA testing connects the current child murders to a decade-old series of killings, and the forensic expert Samuel then organises mass DNA screening of hundreds of police officers and hospital staff, which fails to find the culprit. The solution hinges on a real biological phenomenon: the killer received a bone-marrow transplant for blood cancer, so his blood carries his donor's DNA while his skin and hair retain his own, a form of chimerism that let him pass the blood test. The film presents forensic science, and the expert who masters its subtleties, as the key to the truth, in contrast with the police's hasty assumptions. Transplant-induced chimerism is genuinely known to complicate forensic DNA identification, although the film's sweeping mass screening is a plot convenience.

The story

In Thiruvananthapuram, ACP Rithika investigates the abduction and murder of young girls and is assigned forensic expert Samuel John Kattookkaaran, the brother of her estranged husband Xavier; their surviving daughter Nayana is in therapy with the psychologist Dr. Alphonse Kurien. Samuel deduces that the killer is a child, and Alphonse identifies a sketch as Reuben, an escaped juvenile psychopath he once treated. A DNA test links the new murders to a forgotten case from Rithika's first investigation ten years earlier, and Samuel has hundreds of police and hospital staff tested, without a match. Reuben is caught but kills himself, apparently obeying instructions through a hearing aid. A bus driver wrongly accused in the old case abducts Samuel and reveals a plot involving Rithika's daughters before being shot dead. Samuel finally exposes Alphonse as the mastermind: after a bone-marrow transplant his blood carries a donor's DNA, while his skin and hair still match the crime scenes.
