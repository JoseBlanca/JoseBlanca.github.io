The genetics

Genetics plays only a marginal role. The episode's starting point is a LuthorCorp agricultural experiment, aimed at using kryptonite to make crops grow, in keeping with the company's role in the series as a source of dubious biotechnology carried out in the name of progress or profit. The experiment, however, is a pretext: it is the explosion of superheated kryptonite, not any genetic modification, that splits Lex into good and evil halves, and the rest of the episode is a variation on the double, comparable to the Star Trek episode "The Enemy Within", with no further scientific content. The sources consulted for this summary describe the crop research only briefly and do not present it as genetic engineering, so its relevance to genetics lies mainly in the backdrop of corporate laboratory experiments on living things that runs through Smallville.

The story

Lex Luthor and a LuthorCorp scientist are experimenting with kryptonite, testing whether it can be used to grow crops, when the meteor rock is superheated, turns black and explodes, splitting Lex into two people: a good Lex and an evil double. The evil Lex imprisons his counterpart in the cellar of the Luthor mansion and takes his place. He makes advances to Lana, fights his father Lionel with a sword and brings back Lionel's ruthless side, and tries to kill Clark and Chloe. In the process he discovers Clark's secret, makes a kryptonite ring to weaken him, shoots Jonathan Kent in the leg and tries to force Clark to help him take over the world. Clark finds the real Lex in the cellar and uses his heat vision on the kryptonite ring, turning it black and merging the two Lexes back into one. Lex remembers nothing of what happened, but Lionel has returned to his old ways.
