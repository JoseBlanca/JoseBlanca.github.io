The genetics

Genetic engineering is the premise. Stitch and the 625 earlier experiments are illegal genetic creations of the alien scientist Jumba Jookiba, funded by Dr. Hämsterviel, and the plot turns on who controls them. Jumba, once a mad scientist, is now a sympathetic captive protecting his creations, while Hämsterviel is the villain who claims the experiments as his property and plans to clone Stitch a thousand times, presumably to build an army. The film's message, carried by Lilo, is that the engineered creatures are not weapons but family ('ohana') who deserve rehabilitation rather than destruction, and the dangerous Sparky becomes a friend. The genetics is cartoon fantasy (creatures stored as dehydrated pods and revived with water), used to set up the series about collecting the experiments.

The story

Stitch, alien Experiment 626, struggles to fit in with Lilo in Hawaii and longs for 'cousins' like himself. The mercenary Gantu, working for Dr. Jacques von Hämsterviel, the former partner and backer of Stitch's creator Jumba, kidnaps Jumba to obtain his other experiments, stored as dehydrated pods. Lilo and Stitch find the hidden container and, against Pleakley's warning, rehydrate Experiment 221, an electrical creature they befriend as Sparky. Hämsterviel trades Jumba for the container, escapes with Sparky's help, and the pods scatter across Hawaii during a struggle aboard his ship. Hämsterviel captures Lilo and Stitch and prepares to clone Stitch a thousand times, but Sparky sabotages the machine. The heroes free themselves, Hämsterviel is arrested, and the Grand Councilwoman lets Lilo and Stitch track down and rehabilitate the scattered experiments instead of destroying them, setting up the television series.
