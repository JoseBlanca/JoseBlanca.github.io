The genetics

Genetics enters through two strands. Lady Trieu, a tech billionaire, buys a farm by offering an infertile couple a baby made from their DNA, delivered on the spot, a transaction that shows her wealth, power and command of biotechnology. Adrian Veidt grows fetuses from a lake into adult clones of his servants Phillips and Crookshanks in minutes and treats them as disposable, using the corpses of earlier clones to test the limits of his prison. Trieu's daughter's inherited-seeming memories are hinted at but not explained in this episode. The two practitioners are powerful, manipulative figures whose biotechnology serves their own plans, and the episode treats Veidt's clones with dark humour and cruelty. Instant adult clones are fiction; real reproductive cloning would produce an ordinary baby that must grow up.

The story

In the fourth episode of HBO's Watchmen, the trillionaire Lady Trieu visits a childless couple, the Clarks, and offers them five million dollars and a baby created from their own DNA in exchange for their farm; they accept just as an object falls from space onto the land, which Trieu claims. At a heritage center, Angela Abar learns that Will, the old man she has been investigating, survived the Tulsa massacre; she then finds her missing car, secretly keeps a bottle of his pills and asks Wade to have them analysed. With Laurie Blake she visits Trieu at her Millennium Clock facility, where Trieu reveals she knows about the pills, and Will turns out to be staying with Trieu. Meanwhile Adrian Veidt, imprisoned on a remote estate, harvests human fetuses from a lake and rapidly grows them into new clones of his servants.
