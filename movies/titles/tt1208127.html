The genetics

Cloning is the Sontarans' plan and a key plot device. The Sontarans are a species of cloned soldiers, and losing their war with the Rutans, they want to turn Earth into a breeding world where new batches of clones can be grown; the gas pumped into the atmosphere is clone feed that would make the planet suitable for them, killing humanity in the process. They also clone Martha Jones in a machine that keeps the original unconscious and use the copy as a spy inside UNIT. The Doctor recognizes the clone because she shows no concern for her family, and she dies when the real Martha wakes. Cloning is thus presented as the method of a militaristic alien empire. Real cloning cannot produce an adult copy with the original's memories, and no gas could serve as clone feed.

The story

In the second part of a two-part story, the Earth's atmosphere is being choked by poison gas released from the ATMOS devices fitted to 400 million cars. The Sontarans capture the TARDIS with Donna inside, while a clone of Martha Jones, working for them, stops UNIT from launching nuclear missiles at their ship. UNIT counterattacks with the aircraft carrier Valiant. In the ATMOS factory the Doctor finds the real Martha held in a cloning machine; waking her kills the clone, who reveals before dying that the gas is clone feed meant to turn Earth into a breeding world for billions of Sontaran soldiers. The young genius Luke Rattigan, who helped the Sontarans, learns they were using him. The Doctor builds a device that burns off the gas, then offers the Sontarans a chance to withdraw, but Rattigan takes his place on their ship and sacrifices himself to destroy it.
