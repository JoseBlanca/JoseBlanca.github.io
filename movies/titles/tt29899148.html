The genetics

Like the films it accompanies, the animated series is set in a world remade by de-extinction, and its villains are those who turn dinosaur cloning into business and weapons. Dr. Sarr runs a secret cloning facility in Senegal for the trafficker Soyona Santos, the Broker, who clones dinosaurs and weaponizes them for the black market; one of his creations is a blind, leucistic Baryonyx. Biosyn, under Lewis Dodgson and chief geneticist Dr. Henry Wu, conducts illegal experiments and trains Atrociraptors as weapons, and the heroes' final mission is to expose it and rescue the animals held there. The geneticists are uniformly villainous, serving profit and control, while the protagonists, a paleo-veterinarian and young people who love dinosaurs, side with the animals. Cloning extinct dinosaurs remains impossible because their DNA does not survive tens of millions of years, a point the franchise has long set aside.

The story

Six years after Camp Cretaceous, with dinosaurs now loose on the mainland, the 'Nublar Six' reunite after their friend Brooklynn is apparently killed by an Allosaurus while investigating a conspiracy. Hunted by a silent woman known as the Handler and her trained Atrociraptors, they discover that Brooklynn had uncovered a global dinosaur-trafficking network involving corrupt officers of the Department of Prehistoric Wildlife. Stowing away on a smugglers' ship, they reach Senegal, where they find a secret dinosaur-cloning facility run by Dr. Sarr for 'the Broker', and learn that Brooklynn is alive and working undercover for the Broker to protect them. In Italy and Malta they confront the Broker, Soyona Santos, and investigate the genetics company Biosyn, while Brooklynn's double game strains their trust. In the final season they infiltrate Biosyn's facility in the Dolomites, run by Lewis Dodgson and Dr. Henry Wu, to rescue Ben's Ankylosaurus Bumpy and her young and expose the company's illegal experiments.
