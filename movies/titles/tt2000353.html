The genetics

Cloning drives this comedy episode, which plays the archetypal evil-duplicate plot in the show's absurd style. Callie creates a copy of Mark for the most trivial of reasons, to win a bowling tournament, and plans to dispose of him afterwards; the clone turns out to be a nastier, more aggressive version of the gentle original and causes a murder, a frame-up and a shooting. The episode never says how or with what technology Callie makes the clone, and she refuses to tell Mark where it came from. The clone is finally gassed and recycled for spare body parts, a black joke that treats the copy as disposable material. Nothing about it is meant seriously as science; according to the series creator, the point was the fun of seeing the usually meek Mark as a jerk.

The story

In the monster-filled New York of Ugly Americans, Callie Maggotbone of the Department of Integration clones her colleague Mark Lilly because he is a terrible bowler and she wants the department to win an upcoming tournament. The clone, blond instead of brown-haired and sadistic, locks the real Mark in his closet and takes over his life, turning Mark's therapy group into a fight club. A new assistant, Tad, banishes Callie to a sub-basement as a suspected saboteur but likes the clone's attitude. Reading Callie's diary, the clone learns that she plans to kill him after the tournament, so he murders Tad and frames her with her own trident. When Frank Grimes investigates and finds the real Mark, the clone shoots him in both legs. The real Mark tracks the clone to a tropical island, where he confesses; the clone is gassed and his parts give Grimes new legs and eyes.
