The genetics

The Island is an action film about reproductive cloning used to supply spare parts. Rich clients pay the Merrick Institute to keep a clone of themselves as an insurance policy for organ transplants, or as a surrogate to bear their children. They are told that the clones are kept in a vegetative state, but Dr. Merrick has found that the organs only survive if the clones are conscious, so he raises them as people kept in ignorance. Merrick is the archetype of the scientist who has abandoned ethics for profit and power. The film raises the question of whether clones have the same rights as other people, and its answer is that they are fully human. It also uses a familiar fictional device: Lincoln inherits some of his sponsor's memories, attributed to a cloning defect, which has no basis in biology. The film's premise closely resembles Parts: The Clonus Horror (1979), whose creators sued for copyright infringement. Human reproductive cloning has not been achieved, and research on producing tissues for transplants focuses on therapeutic cloning and stem cells rather than whole cloned people.

The story

In 2019, Lincoln Six Echo and Jordan Two Delta live in a sealed compound whose residents believe that the outside world has been contaminated and that their only hope is to win a lottery to go to "the Island", the last uncontaminated place on Earth. Lincoln, troubled by dreams of things he has never experienced, discovers that the residents are clones of wealthy sponsors, and that the lottery winners are killed to harvest their organs or used as surrogate mothers. He escapes with Jordan, who has just been selected, pursued by mercenaries hired by Dr. Merrick, the scientist who runs the institute. In Los Angeles they meet Lincoln's sponsor, Tom Lincoln, who betrays them because he needs a new liver. Lincoln tricks the mercenaries into killing Tom and takes his identity. Learning that Merrick plans to exterminate the latest clone generations, Lincoln and Jordan return to the compound, kill Merrick and free the other clones.
