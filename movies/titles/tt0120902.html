The genetics

The film's threat is an alien virus, the black oil, which can kill its host or let an alien organism grow inside it. A secret syndicate of powerful men is preparing for an alien colonization of Earth while secretly working on a vaccine, which its scientist Dr. Ben Bronschweig tests on an infected firefighter before the creature inside kills him. Bees bred in domes in a Texas cornfield appear to carry the virus, and people are held suspended in ice in a facility in Antarctica. The conspirators are villains who stage a bombing and murder to keep the secret, and the only vaccine shown to work reaches Mulder through a defector. Genetics is never discussed directly: the film is about a pathogen held and spread in secret by a conspiracy. An infectious agent that turns into an independent organism inside its host is not how viruses work.

The story

In prehistoric Texas a caveman is infected by a black, oil-like alien substance; in 1998 a boy who falls into a hole at the same spot is infected too, and the firefighters who go in after him never come out. FBI agents Mulder and Scully, taken off the X-Files, are blamed for the bombing of a Dallas federal building. The paranoid doctor Alvin Kurtzweil tells Mulder that the victims were already dead and the bombing was staged to hide how they died, and Scully finds an alien virus in one of the bodies. Following tanker trucks, the agents find two domes in a cornfield full of bees. A secret syndicate collaborating with alien colonists has Scully stung by a bee and taken away, but one of its members, the Well-Manicured Man, gives Mulder a vaccine and her location. Mulder rescues Scully from a facility under the Antarctic ice as an alien ship rises from it, and the evidence is destroyed.
