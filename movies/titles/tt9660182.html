The genetics

The series builds on the franchise's T-virus, a retroviral bioweapon made by the Umbrella Corporation that destroyed Raccoon City in 1998 and, in this story, spreads to bring about a global apocalypse. To this it adds a cloning plot: the original Albert Wesker, a rogue Umbrella operative, created several clones of himself, among them Al, the twins' father, and the twins themselves were conceived through a genetic engineering project, a secret they uncover in 2022. Umbrella is the villain throughout: a pharmaceutical giant that covers up its past, keeps the clones in custody and in 2036 rules the remnants of humanity with military force while hunting Jade. The sources do not detail the twins' engineering. The T-virus is fictional, but the corporate bioweapon that escapes control and the company that profits from disaster reflect long-standing anxieties about biotechnology and corporate power.

The story

The series alternates between two timelines. In 2022, fourteen-year-old twins Billie and Jade Wesker move with their father, Albert 'Al' Wesker, to New Raccoon City, a company town in South Africa built by the Umbrella Corporation, which recovered from the 1998 Raccoon City disaster under its CEO Evelyn Marcus. Al works for Umbrella on controlling the T-virus. The twins uncover dark secrets about the company and their own origins: their father is one of several clones of the original Albert Wesker, a rogue Umbrella operative, and they were conceived through a genetic engineering project. As they investigate, the T-virus spreads and triggers 'the Fall', a global collapse. In 2036, some six billion people have become 'Zeroes', man-eating infected humans, and 300 million survivors live in walled city-states. Umbrella, now armed and powerful, hunts the adult Jade, who has a partner and a daughter and fights to survive and bring the company down. The series was cancelled after one season.
